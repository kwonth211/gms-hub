Choose a sustainable play style rather than a fixed damage tier. Gear, party support and positioning change the result within every class.

Solo or regular party

Solo players need a manageable recurring supply budget. Regular groups can divide floors and coordinate buffs or healing. Short sessions benefit from a fallback map that does not require waiting for a party.

Before the second job

Compare range versus melee, single targets versus grouping, existing gear and your preferred party role. Do not choose from predictions about unreleased later jobs.

Read gameplay conditions

Check upgrades, buffs, party size, casts and recovery rather than level alone. Open the relevant class guide and compare routes with your own setup.
