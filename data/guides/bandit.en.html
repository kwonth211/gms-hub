Put a Bandit's points in this order: Haste 1, Dagger Mastery 20 (by level 37), Savage Blow 30 (by 47), Haste 20, Steal 1, Nimble Recovery 12, Steal 30, Dagger Booster 9. Until Mastery is full at 37, grind with the Double Stab you finished in first job. LUK is your main stat; pick one dagger line for your next weapon and meet only its STR or DEX requirement.

Skill order by level: why Mastery comes first

  1. Level 30: Haste 1. Moving between platforms gets faster right away.
  2. Level 32: Dagger Mastery 5, then Dagger Booster 1. One point of Booster already gives full attack speed.
  3. Level 37: Dagger Mastery 20. The higher minimum damage turns monsters on the edge of one hit into a guaranteed one hit. Grind with Double Stab meanwhile: level 1 Savage Blow is only 65% ×4, so there is no reason to switch early.
  4. Levels 44 to 47: Savage Blow. The sixth hit unlocks when it reaches 21 at level 44, and it is maxed at 47. Switch as soon as it kills in fewer uses than Double Stab.
  5. Level 54: Haste 20, then Steal 1. Steal 1 unlocks the stun and the +35 attack buff.
  6. Level 58: Nimble Recovery 12. You fight up close, so getting HP and MP back on dodges cuts your potion bill.
  7. Levels 68 to 70: Steal 30, then Dagger Booster 9. Steal's success rate climbs from 30% to 90%.

Simulating levels 30 to 70 1,500 times per build shows that, against this Mastery-first order, Steal first takes 5 to 16% longer and Savage first 8 to 10% longer.

The other road is Mastery, then Steal, grinding with Double Stab. The same simulation puts it 8 to 10% ahead of the default, because it spends less MP and Steal's buff adds 35 attack.

The trade-off: Double Stab hits weaker than Savage, often needs one more use, and has shorter reach. If stunning two monsters from a distance with Steal and then closing in suits your hands, take this road.

Reach and the Steal buff

  • Double Stab: 70 to 84 pixels forward and almost no height, since it is a thrust. Stun with Steal from range, then close in.
  • Savage Blow: 95 pixels forward and up to 62 pixels above your feet. On maps full of slopes and stairs it also reaches monsters slightly above you.
  • Steal's attack +35: a separate attack bonus, not weapon attack. With a level 40 Gephart it is worth about 8 weapon attack, roughly 14% more damage, and about 8% at level 70.
  • Stealing items: you can steal from each monster only once, and you can keep trying until it works. The stolen item comes on top of the monster's normal drop; it does not pull that drop out early.

Stats: LUK first, one dagger line only

Raising both STR and DEX for weapons you will never use costs you that much LUK. Pick the dagger line you will use next, meet only its requirement, and put the rest into LUK. On the STR line that means 40 STR for Bazlud at 45 and 45 STR for Sai at 50.

Even on the STR line, your armor needs some DEX. The level 35 Avenger overall (Red Avenger) asks for 25 DEX and 60 LUK, and the level 40 Dark Shadow asks for 30 DEX and 70 LUK. Raise DEX only to what your armor needs. Gear for each level is in the Thief equipment list.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

  • Taking Savage Blow at 30: at level 1 it is 65% ×4, barely different from Double Stab's 160% ×2. Finish Mastery 20 first.
  • Raising both STR and DEX: commit to one dagger line. DEX goes only as high as your armor needs.
  • Staying on monsters that take two uses: two uses up close means twice the hits taken. A map one step lower where you one-shot costs fewer potions.
  • Fighting with Steal's buff off: the +35 attack lasts 60 seconds. Steal again the moment it drops.

Frequently asked questions

Steal first or Savage Blow first?

Neither: Dagger Mastery first. Finishing Mastery 20 at 37 and then moving to Savage Blow is the fastest path. Steal first takes 5 to 16% longer and Savage first 8 to 10% longer.

I use an STR dagger. Do I still need DEX for the Avenger overall?

Yes, but only up to your armor's requirement. The level 35 Avenger overall needs 25 DEX and the level 40 Dark Shadow needs 30. Any DEX beyond that is LUK thrown away.

What does an STR Bandit use from 40 to 44?

The Korean Fan. It needs only 70 LUK and swings very fast. Gephart needs 35 DEX, so an STR Bandit cannot wear it; at 45, reach 40 STR and move to Bazlud.

Is a scrolled Bazlud enough?

Into the early fifties, yes. A level 50 Bandit ranker was still using one. Keep it while Lupins die in one use, then move to a level 50 Shinkita or Sai once you have the mesos.

Where does a level 45 Bandit go?

Take a scrolled Bazlud to the Lupins in Monkey Forest or Monkey Swamp. If Lupins die in one use, you make a profit. With enough accuracy, two-hit grinding in Forgotten Hollow works too.

When does a fusion Bandit switch to daggers?

Reset your skills into daggers at 40 to 45. Before that, save up for the reset, the new dagger and its stat requirement. Switch at 45 and you can wear a Bazlud right away with 40 STR.
