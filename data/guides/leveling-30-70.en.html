In your 30s, run KPQ, the Excavation Sites or the Ant Tunnel; from 35 to 45, go to the Burnt Land, the Cave of Evil Eye, Lorang Lorang or Cold Eyes; from 45 to 55, Monkey Forest, Wraiths or Florina Beach; from 55 to 70, the Remains tombs, Golem's Temple and the Camps. Before level, check the accuracy a map needs at your level. Wraiths need 104 accuracy at level 48, and the Skeleton Soldiers in Camp 1 need 111.

Your 30s: where to train right after second job

After the level 30 advancement, stay on your old map until the new skills have points in them. Warriors spend 30 to 38 in "Ligator parties": they stay in KPQ stage 1 and kill the Ligators that keep spawning instead of clearing the stage. Classic World's party quest has no level cap, and you enter through Lakelis on Kerning City channels 3–12. A two-shot party in the low 30s averaged 1,863 EXP a minute.

With a party, go to the Excavation Sites. Site I gives about 6,000 EXP every five minutes, and Site III, from level 36, about 7,500. By level 40 every monster there dies in one hit. In the Ant Tunnel, go only when the Horny Mushrooms on your floor die in one hit; then you also make about 7,500 every five minutes.

Hunters and Fire/Poison mages go to the Ligators in the Swamp of Despair. Swamp of Despair I spawns 41 of them, and they are slow enough that they almost never touch you. Ligators have high defense, so for other classes level 35 is the real starting point. The Murky Swamp, through a hidden entrance behind the Kerning City hospital, gave a level 35–37 assassin 88,000 EXP and 69,000 mesos an hour, solo.

  • Ligator Lv 31
  • Wooden Mask Lv 23
  • Rocky Mask Lv 24
  • Jr. Wraith Lv 34

Jr. Wraiths in the early 30s cost you money because you miss too often. Line 2 Area 1 spawns 83 of them, and they are weak to fire and holy, which makes them great for Fire/Poison mages and Clerics. Hunters with Focus at 10 stop missing at 35, and since Jr. Neckis share the map, watch your misses on them too.

Levels 35–45: Burnt Land, Evil Eyes, Lorangs, Cold Eyes

Go to the Burnt Land once a Fire Boar dies in two hits or fewer. Both I and II give 7,000–8,000 EXP every five minutes; when I is full, II has fewer players. A level 35–37 assassin made about 75,000 EXP an hour in Burnt Land III. Fire Boars resist fire, so Fire/Poison mages hunt only the Dark Axe Stumps there.

An Evil Eye has 606 HP, so once your minimum damage passes 606 every hit is a kill. Fighters get there with Power Strike at 37, Pages at 39. A Spearman who one-shot them made 1,500 EXP a minute and 50–60 mesos a kill. Cave IV (23 Evil Eyes) is the best, then III, II and I.

Lorang Lorang is on Florina Beach; talk to Pason in Lith Harbor to get there. Lorangs have low avoidability, so 79 accuracy is enough at the same level, and the map spawns 35 of them. The first floor never runs dry but hits you more; the second floor saves potions. Return scrolls do not work on Florina, so buy White Potions (350 mesos) from Valen's shop in town.

Lucky Seven does not kill a Lorang in one hit, so an assassin without mastery loses money there. A Hunter with an upgraded Vaulter 2000 two-shot Lorangs from level 43. Lorangs and Clangs are weak to lightning, which makes Florina easy for Ice/Lightning mages.

Cold Eyes need about 82 accuracy at level 40. A LUK assassin reaches that around level 39 with no gear. Another Path spawns 23 Cold Eyes and nothing else. Fire/Poison mages hit them every time at 38 and need three Fire Arrows each.

Melee classes do well in West Rocky Mountain IV too. A 10-minute solo run at 40–45 gave 2,380 EXP a minute and 70,000–100,000 mesos an hour. That beats the Monkey Forest and Cold Eye parties at the same level, which make 2,100–2,300 a minute and only 10,000–30,000 mesos.

Sleepy Dungeon II is a mixed map that is almost always empty. When Curse Eyes and Lupins die in two hits, it gives 8,500–10,000 EXP every five minutes. Clear it top to bottom, then step into the Cash Shop and back out to return to the top.

Take a party to the Curse Eye caves. In Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North IX (20 Curse Eyes), five players each get 1,500–1,800 EXP a minute. Curse Eyes become a two-hit kill at 38 for Fire/Poison, 39 for Ice/Lightning, 40 for Fighters and Pages and 50 for Spearmen; Bandits one-shot them around 48.

  • Fire Boar Lv 32
  • Evil Eye Lv 27
  • Lorang Lv 38
  • Cold Eye Lv 40
  • Curse Eye Lv 33

Levels 45–55: Monkey Forest, Wraiths, Florina Beach

Claw thieves one-shot Lupins in Monkey Forest and Monkey Swamp from 43 to 47. When Zombie Lupins die in one hit, move to them. A level 55 assassin made about 110,000 EXP and 70,000 mesos an hour in Monkey Forest II. The EXP is lower, but no other map left more mesos.

A Zombie Lupin has 1,774 HP and throws magic from range, so it costs more potions than you expect. For a quieter map, take the secret portal in the Kerning sewers to Monkey Swamp III (31 Zombie Lupins). Bring a Cleric and Heal cuts your potion use a lot.

Wraiths need 104 accuracy at level 48. Line 2 Area 3 spawns 68 Wraiths and nothing else, and Line 1 Area 4 has 59. A level 49 Fire/Poison mage with 104 accuracy and 255 magic made 2,149 EXP a minute alone, and a party of two-shotters makes 200,000–240,000 an hour.

Wraiths heal themselves, so slow kills cost extra hits. A level 48 Cleric two-shotting them still lost 200 mesos a minute. When the subway is full, go to Camps 1–3 or Sleepy Dungeon IV.

Clangs on Florina Beach need 98 accuracy and give 1.6 times a Lorang's EXP per kill. Fighters with a sword start at 48; other warriors go at 50 with hat accuracy scrolls and a level 50 overall with DEX. Hot Sand mixes Torties (82 accuracy) and Clangs, and a party of two or three around level 50 made 2,600–2,900 EXP a minute there.

Torties have zero magic defense and move very slowly, so magicians round them up easily. A level 65 Ice/Lightning mage made 274,000 EXP an hour alone in Hot Sand with Thunder Bolt. Torties drop the 10% glove attack scroll and the 60% cape STR scroll.

Hunters, Crossbowmen and Ice/Lightning mages also hunt Drakes in Dangerous Valley. You stop missing Drakes at 43, and from the fishing spot on the first floor your attacks reach the whole spawn. Drakes resist both fire and ice, and a patch cut their EXP from 142 to 133.

Levels 55–70: the Remains tombs, Golem's Temple and the Camps

Remains <Tomb> III has only two floors, which makes it easy to run, and many players come over from Wraiths around level 50. Skeledogs need 89 accuracy and Mummydogs 95, far less than a Wraith's 104. Both are undead and take 25% more from holy attacks. A party around level 72 timed ten minutes there and made 215,000 EXP and about 120,000 mesos an hour.

In Remains <Tomb> I, three players take the left, middle and right. Skeledogs are only level 44 but have high avoidability, so go around 55 to stop missing. It is the easiest map to get into when the Drake and Golem maps are full.

Golem's Temple is behind the warning sign on the map between Henesys and Sleepywood. Stone Golems are level 56, but their attacks miss you often, so level 55 is enough; their large hitbox lets you hit them from the platforms above and below. Golem's Temple II suits a pair, such as a warrior and a magician.

The Drake Hunting Ground lies deep in the Ant Tunnel, past the Cave of Evil Eye. Go when Drakes die in two hits; melee classes take floors 2–4 and ranged classes floor 1. Four players holding one floor each get about 2,040 EXP a minute per person. Dragon Skins drop there and sell well as a material for gloves and shoes.

In Camp 1, 20 of the 25 monsters are Skeleton Soldiers, so you need 111 accuracy. The standard setup is a six-player party with one player on each of floors 1–4, and players start around 58. Around 60, move to Camp 3, where Officers make up most of the spawn and need 120 accuracy. A strong level 75 party made 265,000 EXP and 80,000 mesos an hour in the Camps.

Bowmen have their own route. Crossbowmen two-shot the mushrooms in Ant Tunnel IV at 40, and from 50 both Hunters and Crossbowmen one-shot them in Ant Tunnel III. From 60 to 70, they sweep the Jr. Wraiths (69) and Stirges on Line 1 Area 2 with piercing attacks.

Fire/Poison mages past 50 can solo Crocos (3,136 HP, 146 EXP). Dangerous Croko II spawns 24 of them. Turn Magic Guard off; blocking the same damage with MP costs nearly twice the mesos.

  • Mummydog Lv 47
  • Stone Golem Lv 56
  • Skeleton Soldier Lv 57
  • Officer Skeleton Lv 63

Common money-losing mistakes, and the fix

  • Going to Dark Stone Golems early: a Hunter in the early 50s removed every miss with top-grade accuracy potions and hat accuracy scrolls, then still left because the contact damage put him in the red. Reach 101 accuracy with gear first; until then, hunt Cold Eyes, Lorangs or Wraiths.
  • Jr. Wraiths in the early 30s: the misses cost you. Go around 35, and as a Hunter only after Focus 10.
  • Clerics who only cast Heal: Heal earns EXP only when party members are killing monsters. Solo, kill with Holy Arrow; heal-train only in a six-player party spread across the map.
  • Fire Arrow from on top of a platform: Fire Arrow only passes through a platform when you shoot from below it. Stand lower than the monsters.
  • Sleepy Dungeon V: a patch cut its spawn from 31 monsters to 20 and slowed the respawn. For Dark Stone Golems, use Sleepy Dungeon IV.

Frequently asked questions

How much EXP an hour should I make at level 40?

Good spots at 40–45 give 120,000–140,000 an hour. Monkey Forest and Cold Eye parties made 2,100–2,300 a minute, and West Rocky Mountain IV solo made 2,380. A five-player Curse Eye party gives each player 1,500–1,800 a minute, or 90,000–108,000 an hour.

Wraiths and Mummydogs both take two hits. Which should I hunt?

Hunt Wraiths if you have 104 accuracy. A Wraith has less HP and gives 122 EXP against a Mummydog's 117. If your accuracy is short, go to Remains <Tomb> III, where Mummydogs need only 95.

Where should a level 40 Hunter go?

Lorang Lorang. With an upgraded bow, Lorangs die in two hits from 43. Before that, shoot from the roof of Deep Ant Tunnel I or the Cold Eye rooftop spot, and skip maps like the Curse Eye caves where you end up hitting with your bow at close range.

Where should a level 43 Cleric train?

Lupin maps or a Curse Eye party. A level 43 Cleric tracked 1,650–1,770 EXP a minute on Lupins and 1,475–1,765 in a Curse Eye party, against only about 920 solo on Jr. Wraiths or Masks. Wraiths are hit only 63% of the time at 45 and every time at 50, so move to Wraiths at 50.

I am a level 35 Fighter. Where do I go besides KPQ?

Stay in Ligator parties until 37, then go to the Cave of Evil Eye. Power Strike one-shots Evil Eyes at 37. When your Zard two-shots Curse Eyes at 40, move to a Curse Eye cave party.

I play Fire/Poison and cannot afford the potions. What do I change?

Turn Magic Guard off and hunt only monsters weak to fire. Dark Axe Stumps, Jr. Wraiths, Ligators, Cold Eyes and Crocos are weak to fire; Drakes and Fire Boars resist it. Lemons and Oranges are the cheapest way to restore MP.

How long does 30 to 70 take?

A long time. One Cleric took about 400 hours to go from level 1 to 70. Even in a four-player Drake Hunting Ground party at level 50, one level takes about four and a half hours.
