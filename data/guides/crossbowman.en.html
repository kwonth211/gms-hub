A Crossbowman pierces a line of monsters on one floor with Iron Arrow. Each shot hits hard and the single-target Final Attack is strong, but terrain and attack speed matter more. It's comfortable where monsters line up, and much less so on maps split by height. Skill and weapon numbers below come from the game data; the hunting notes are reports from players who levelled the class.

Crossbowman or Hunter

  • Crossbowman: crossbow + Iron Arrow. Lines monsters up on long, flat platforms and pierces them. At the same level a crossbow usually has slightly more attack than a bow.
  • Hunter: bow + Arrow Bomb. Best where monsters bunch around the blast, with a stun to open distance. Hunter guide

Stats and crossbows

DEX is your main stat. Put STR only up to what your current crossbow needs, counting STR on your gear. Bows and crossbows ask for different STR: the level 30 Ryden (bow) needs 30 STR and the Eagle Crow (crossbow) 20; at 70 the Red Hinkel needs 70 and the Gross Jaeger 62. Don't copy a Hunter's "level minus 10" STR.

The Crossbowman's Final Attack doesn't spread over several monsters like the Hunter's; it hits one monster hard. Slotting a single-target move into a piercing rotation can slow the overall clear, which is why Crossbowmen take it late.

Skill build (121 second-job points)

  1. Level 30: Iron Arrow 1
  2. Up to 33: Crossbow Mastery 5 → Booster 1
  3. By 34: Booster 5, Soul Arrow 1
  4. By 39: Crossbow Mastery maxed at 20
  5. By 49: Iron Arrow maxed at 30
  6. Then: finish Booster and Soul Arrow first → Final Attack, Amazon's Judgment 1
  • Unlike the Hunter, finish Booster and Soul Arrow before Final Attack. A stronger shot doesn't kill faster if attack speed and lining up don't back it.
  • While Iron Arrow is low, your finished first-job Arrow Blow (240% at max) or Double Shot (120% × 2) is cleaner on a single monster.

Hunting with Iron Arrow

  • Stand where monsters line up on the same floor and pierce from the front.
  • Watch the last monster. If the front died and the back survived, one pierce didn't finish the job; count the extra shot's MP and time.
  • Six monsters on screen spread up and down is not a six-monster pierce. Even on long platforms the height has to line up to reach the back.
  • Even in range, floors and slopes block arrows. Change floors or match the monster's height. Inside 65 you swing weakly instead of shooting.
  • When it feels slow, check in order: buffs on → monsters too close → arrows blocked → how many it really pierces.

Three checks before moving up: almost no misses, hits-to-kill you can handle, and hunting that keeps going before potions run out. Hunt each candidate for 5 minutes with the same gear and buffs and note EXP, potions and mesos. Pick a map from the Crossbowman training routes and check hits with the hits-to-kill calculator.
