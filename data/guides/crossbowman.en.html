Unlike the Hunter, you push Final Attack to the end, and you pick maps where monsters line up on long, flat platforms.

A Crossbowman pierces up to six monsters standing in a line on one floor with Iron Arrow. Each shot hits hard and the single-target Final Attack is strong, but the pierce breaks on maps where monsters sit at different heights. That is why long, flat platforms decide your map more than your level does.

Choosing a crossbow: stats, speed and price

DEX is your main stat. Raise STR only to what your current crossbow needs, counting STR on your gear. Crossbows ask for less STR than bows: 20 for the level 30 Eagle Crow, 60 for the level 70 Gross Jaeger. Putting in STR equal to your level, Hunter-style, costs you that much DEX.

Iron Arrow weakens as it travels. If the first monster takes 180%, the second takes 144% and the third about 115%. Whether the last monster dies in the same number of shots is what sets your speed.

How to hunt with Iron Arrow

  • Stand where monsters line up on your floor and pierce from the front.
  • Watch the last monster. If the front died and the back survived, you pay MP and time for another shot. Then fire at shorter lines.
  • Six monsters spread up and down is not a six-monster pierce. Even on long platforms the height has to match all the way to the back.
  • Floors and slopes block arrows even inside your range. Change floors or match the monster's height. Inside 65 you swing weakly instead of shooting.
  • You can't shoot in mid-jump. Land on the new floor, then fire.
  • When it feels slow, fix in this order: buffs on → monsters too close → arrows blocked by terrain → how many it really pierces.

Getting enough accuracy

With Focus at 10 from first job, Jr. Wraiths stop dodging at level 35. Maps that ask for more, like Wraith (104 at level 50) or Clang (98), need gear. Hat Accuracy scrolls: 100% gives +1 ({{price:2040000}}), 60% gives +2 ({{price:2040001}}), 10% gives +3 ({{price:2040002}}).

If you hunt Cold Eyes, bring 25 of their quest items to Arwen the Fairy in Ellinia for a Helmet Accuracy scroll at 60% or 10%. In Forgotten Hollow, Nyroth in the town lets you pick one 10% weapon attack scroll for your class.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Putting STR equal to your level, like a Hunter → from level 30 on, a crossbow needs 10 less STR than a bow of the same level. Put the spare points in DEX.
  • Maxing Final Attack first → it hits one target, so it does nothing for piercing speed. Finish Booster and Soul Arrow first.
  • Picking maps with many floors, like Monkey Forest → climbing up and down breaks every pierce. Go to long straight platforms like the subway.
  • Skipping the Heckler because "crossbows are slow" → the Heckler swings at speed 6, the same as a bow.
  • Picking Dark Stone Golem for its EXP → constant hits drain your mesos. In the 50s go to Ant Tunnel III, Wraith or Cold Eye.

Frequently asked questions

I'm a level 61 Crossbowman. Can I hunt the skeletons in the Camp?

Camp 1, yes, once you have 111 accuracy. 20 of its 25 spawn points are Skeleton Soldiers, and 111 accuracy at level 60 means no misses on them. Camp 3 is mostly Officer Skeletons, which need 134 at level 60; short of that, go to Line 1 <Area 2> or Cold Eye.

Where do I get the level 60 Golden Crow?

Right now it barely shows up on the market. Scroll a Rower and use it until 70. Clangs drop the Rower, and it sells for {{price:1462007}}.

Should I swap my Heckler for a Silver Crow?

Attack goes from 59 to 67, but speed drops from 6 to 7. If scrolls have already raised your Heckler's attack by more than 8, keep it until the level 50 Rower. With a clean Heckler, switch at 45.

How much STR does a Crossbowman need?

Only what your next crossbow asks for: Eagle Crow 20, Heckler 30, Silver Crow 35, Rower 40, Golden Crow 50, Gross Jaeger 60. Count STR on your gear too.

When do I take Final Attack?

After Booster 20 and Soul Arrow 20, from level 61. It hits one monster for 360%, so it only pays off where you kill one at a time, like Drakes.

What level should a Crossbowman go to Forgotten Hollow?

From 54. A level 40 crossbow two-shots Glowshrooms there, and at 57 a level 50 crossbow two-shots Aqumanders too. Maps and entrances are in the Forgotten Hollow guide.

When can I use third-job skills like Strafe?

Not yet. Third job isn't out, so you keep levelling with second-job skills past 70. Still deciding between the two? Read the Hunter guide too.
