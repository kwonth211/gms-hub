Where to hunt from level 1 to your second job at level 30. The training tables here come from data GMS Hub collects itself: the level recommendations of three guide sites, NiaMeowDB, MS-Classic and Henesys.GG, merged map by map, with maps recommended by several sites shown first. A map that several guides agree on is less likely to disappoint than one only a single site suggests.

Three things to check before picking a map

Do monsters die in one or two hits? Early potion costs are steep in Classic World. If a monster takes three hits or more, you burn potions faster than you gain EXP. Even on a recommended map, drop down a step if it's more than three hits for you. The hits-to-kill calculator tells you exactly.

Is it crowded? Popular maps are packed this early after launch. A quiet, less popular map often gives more EXP than sharing monsters on a famous one. Try another channel on the same map, too.

Can you party there? Parties earn bonus EXP, so on maps where party hunting works, it pays off. You must keep attacking, though: a member idle for 10 seconds gets no party EXP.

Levels 1-10: just follow the quests

Following the Maple Island and Lith Harbor quests gets you to level 10 naturally, so there's little to decide here. Quest rewards include potions and gear, so do them all. How to leave the island and what to set up on day one is in the getting started guide.

Levels 10-20: Kerning construction site or Ellinia

After first job the paths split. Around Kerning City, the construction site is a safe choice: several kinds of monsters share the map, so pick the floor that matches your damage. Octopus (level 12) takes most classes three hits at this point, though, so it is poor value. If it's crowded, move toward the northern part of the site or to maps with Orange Mushrooms. If Kerning isn't for you, the fields south of Ellinia have plenty of monsters too.

From around level 15, the top of the monkey forest above Ellinia is another popular spot. The upper platforms, where monsters spawn two or three at a time, are the most efficient, and you can stay until about level 20.

Bubblings in subway Line 1 are the only map all three sites recommend. They suit melee classes, warriors especially. That also makes the map crowded, so ranged classes have little reason to compete for it. Around level 20, Stirges in the subway's Transfer Area are easier for ranged classes.

Level 20: pause for one quest

At 20, do Jane's quest in Lith Harbor first. It rewards a random weapon attack scroll; even if it isn't for your weapon, selling it in the Free Market pays for early gear. It also unlocks a quest chain at level 44. Items and steps are in seven early quests worth doing.

Upgrade your weapon at 15 and again at 20. Gear attack counts for a lot in Classic World, and a new weapon alone can save you a hit per kill. To save mesos, you can keep a level 15 weapon and then gear up for 25 in one go.

Levels 21-30: Ant Tunnel, or the Kerning party quest

If grinding gets boring, the Kerning City party quest opens at level 21. It's the most recommended activity for levels 21-30 and busy enough that parties fill fast. In chat, "rush" means a party that skips the final bonus map and "bonus" means one that hunts it to the end. On your first run, one quest needs the King Slime kill, so tell the party if you have it. The Kerning party quest guide walks through every stage.

If your gear is strong, the Excavation Site above Perion's rocky road is also known for good EXP after level 25. Monsters there are tough, though; if you can't kill them in one or two hits, the Ant Tunnel or the party quest is better.

Where to avoid

MS-Classic doesn't recommend The Tunnel That Lost Light I because higher-level monsters spawn there too. Many map names look alike, so check monster levels before trying a new map.
