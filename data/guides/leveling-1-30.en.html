Keep gear, skills, party and duration consistent. Record EXP gained, looted mesos, actual loot-sale income and consumables used, including supplies purchased earlier. Avoid double-counting looted supplies that you consume. Subtract potion, ammunition and travel costs; record unsold rare loot separately rather than assigning a speculative sale price.
