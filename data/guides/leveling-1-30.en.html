1–10: bundle quests and prepare for advancement

Combine nearby kill and collection tasks on Maple Island. Keep some reward potions for your first job. After reaching Lith Harbor, travel to your chosen class town and check advancement at level 10. Before buying equipment, verify requirements and leave a supply reserve.

20–30: do not default to Ant Tunnel

Compare tree dungeons, one-hit Stirges for claw users, and KPQ after 21. Early Ant Tunnel play requires a separate gear and party check. Dagger users should favor grounded targets. Test Stirges again after a Meba upgrade; entering KPQ and comfortably contributing are different milestones.

Combine quest errands and upgrades

Pia and Jane can fit mushroom collection routes; check Lakelis when starting KPQ. Do not budget unsold scroll rewards as cash. Buy upgrades when they improve reliable kill thresholds, while retaining consumable funds.

When supplies are draining

  1. Reduce chasing and contact damage.
  2. Investigate repeated misses before buying more attack.
  3. Count casts, not just the largest damage number.
  4. Compare price per usable recovery, excluding overheal.
  5. Include party waiting time in ten-minute comparisons.
