Keep enough funds for both your next weapon and another training session. Level bands tell you when a move becomes possible, not when you must go.

1–10: bundle quests and prepare for advancement

Combine nearby kill and collection tasks on Maple Island. Keep some reward potions for your first job. After reaching Lith Harbor, travel to your chosen class town and check advancement at level 10. Before buying equipment, read its requirements and leave a supply reserve.

20–30: do not default to Ant Tunnel

Use the tree dungeons, one-hit Stirges for claw users, and KPQ after 21. Early Ant Tunnel play requires a separate gear and party check. Dagger users should favor grounded targets. Go back to Stirges after a Meba upgrade; being allowed into KPQ and pulling your weight there are different milestones.

Combine quest errands and upgrades

Pia and Jane can fit mushroom collection routes; check Lakelis when starting KPQ. Do not budget unsold scroll rewards as cash. Buy upgrades when they improve reliable kill thresholds, while retaining consumable funds.

When supplies are draining

  1. Reduce chasing and contact damage.
  2. Investigate repeated misses before buying more attack.
  3. Count casts, not just the largest damage number.
  4. Go by price per HP or MP you actually recover, not counting overheal.
  5. Count party waiting time in every ten-minute tally.
