Where to train at each level, and when to move

Levels 1–10: quest your way off the island

On Maple Island, take every kill and collection quest for the map you are on and clear them together on the Snail Hunting Grounds and the Amherst fields. Snail Hunting Ground I holds 40 Snails and nothing else. Once the island quests run out around level 8, sail to Lith Harbor and finish level 10 there.

The island maps were given slower respawns in the November 18 patch, and they are still slow. If you run short of monsters, leave for Lith Harbor right away. Keep the potions your quests give you for the first sessions after your job advancement.

  • Snail Lv 1
  • Shroom Lv 3
  • Red Snail Lv 4
  • Orange Mushroom Lv 8

Levels 10–13: Henesys Hunting Ground

After your first job, level to 13 in Henesys Hunting Ground I. It mixes 10 Shrooms, 6 Red Snails and 5 each of Orange Mushrooms, Slimes and Green Mushrooms. Once your attack has a few points in it, go up to III, which holds 17 Slimes, 13 Green Mushrooms and 11 Orange Mushrooms.

All three hunting grounds respawn more slowly since the November 18 patch, and map I lost the most. If more than three players share one platform, change channel.

Levels 13–17: The Pig Beach

On the 3-Way Road-Split between Henesys and Lith Harbor, a hidden portal behind the yellow flowers leads to the Pig Beach. Ranged classes stand on the flat ground to the left; melee classes take the stairs on the right. A Pig hits for about 20 and drops around 12 mesos, so you spend almost nothing on potions. Holding the left side alone took 1 hour 15 minutes from 13 to 17.

Leather, Stiff Feathers and Pig's Heads drop often; use them for early quests or sell them. The Iron Hog spawns only once an hour.

Levels 15–21: Caution Falling Down or the Ellinia tree dungeons

Thieves and bowmen go to Caution Falling Down, by the Kerning City construction site, at 15. It holds 12 Octopus, 5 Orange Mushrooms and 3 Blue Mushrooms, and six players split it two on top, two in the middle and one or two at the bottom. Right after level 10 an Octopus takes three hits, so wait until it takes two, around 15. The Blue Mushrooms at the bottom hit hard, so take a higher platform.

Falling off drops you all the way to the floor. If the climb back eats too much time, move to the Construction Site North of Kerning City (23 Red Snails, 11 Dark Stumps, 9 Orange Mushrooms). On the main construction site, the bricks and pipes at the bottom left block arrows and stars, so ranged players stand elsewhere.

Warriors and magicians head to Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North IV in Ellinia. It spawns 13 Slimes and 31 Green Mushrooms; you want Slimes in one hit and Green Mushrooms in one or two. A Green Mushroom hits for more than 60 if it touches you, so fight from a platform edge. When the map is taken, The Forest of Wisdom (25 Slimes, 30 Green Mushrooms) has the same monsters.

Between 15 and 21, the Green Mushroom spot at the top of the Ellinia forest gives about 2,500 EXP every five minutes. At this level that beats running the Ant Tunnel alone.

  • Octopus Lv 12
  • Slime Lv 6
  • Green Mushroom Lv 14
  • Blue Mushroom Lv 19

Levels 20–30: Stirges, Bubblings and KPQ

Claw thieves go to the Transfer Area in the Kerning subway at 20. It spawns 38 Stirges and nothing else, and Lucky Seven needs to kill each one in a single hit. A level 20 claw thief made about 3,500 EXP every five minutes there. If one hit is not enough, switch to the level 25 Meba and come back.

Stirges fly, so dagger thieves and warriors should stay away from them. Magicians start with the 64 Bubblings on Line 1 Area 1. A Bubbling has 259 HP, and if a Bolt or Claw does not kill it in one cast your MP drains fast. The map often has more than 15 players; when there is no room, go back to the Green Mushrooms in the tree dungeon.

From 21, every class should run the Kerning party quest. A party in the low 20s makes about 40,000 EXP an hour, and about 60,000 in the high 20s. At 60,000 an hour, 21 to 22 takes roughly 25 minutes. The full run and every stage answer are in the KPQ guide.

Without a party, go to the Land of Wild Boar from 24 (22 Wild Boars, best with 3–5 players), Ant Tunnel Park from 25 (23 Zombie Mushrooms, 31 Evil Eyes) and Excavation Site I from 27 (23 Ghost Stumps, 10 Wooden Masks). Ant Tunnel Park has a shop inside the map, so you restock potions and stars without leaving. Excavation Site I gives about 6,000 EXP every five minutes, but only with solid gear.

  • Stirge Lv 20
  • Bubbling Lv 15
  • Wild Boar Lv 25
  • Zombie Mushroom Lv 24

With a level 20 Two-Handed Sword, Ribbon Pigs die in about one hit and Bubblings mostly in two. Most quests between 16 and 21 ask for drops from the mushrooms and swamp monsters you are already killing. What to change when potions eat your mesos

  1. If you keep getting hit while chasing, drop to a shorter platform or a weaker monster. A Pig hits for about 20; a Green Mushroom for more than 60.
  2. If a monster takes three hits or more, buy the next weapon or go back to the previous map. A higher-level monster does not leave you more EXP per potion.
  3. Choose potions by price divided by the HP or MP they actually restore. Overheal is money thrown away.
  4. If you wait a long time for a party, time ten minutes including the wait. If the field gives more, go back to the field.
  5. Do not count a quest scroll as money until it has sold.

For maps that keep potion costs low at 20, open level-20 supply-saving spots.

Frequently asked questions

What level is the first job advancement?

Level 10. Finish the Maple Island quests around level 8, reach 10 near Lith Harbor, then travel to your class town. Spend your first SP on your main attack and head to Henesys Hunting Ground.

What level can I start KPQ?

Level 21. If you lack damage right at 21, come back around 24. Accept Lakelis's Proof of Companionship quest before your very first run.

Where should a level 26 magician train?

The Kerning party quest. Without a party, go to the Bubblings on Line 1 Area 1, and if that is full, the Green Mushrooms in Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North IV. The Forest of Wisdom gets crowded; if every channel is busy, KPQ is the better use of your time.

Why are there so few monsters in Henesys Hunting Ground?

Its respawn rate was cut in the November 18 patch. Henesys Hunting Ground I lost the most, and the Kerning construction sites and Perion Street Corner slowed down too. The Ant Tunnel area got its old rate back in the November 24 patch.

When should I go to the Ant Tunnel?

When Horny Mushrooms die in one hit and Zombie Mushrooms in two. Claw thieves with a level 20 claw and a party can go in their 20s, but leave at once if Horny Mushrooms take more than two hits. Ant Tunnel III has the most monsters: 38 Horny Mushrooms and 61 Zombie Mushrooms.

How long does 1 to 30 take?

About 12 hours of play. A thief on this route took 12 hours 13 minutes. Pig Beach from 13 to 17 took 1 hour 15 minutes, Caution Falling Down from 17 to 21 took 1 hour 50 minutes, and most of the rest was KPQ.

Can a dagger thief train on Stirges?

No, dagger thieves should hunt monsters that stay on the ground. Stirges fly, and chasing them with melee attacks only gets you hit more. Train in the tree dungeons from 15 to 21 and in KPQ from 21.
