A few early quests in Classic World are worth far more for their rewards than their EXP. Quests that hand out scrolls are especially valuable: scrolls sell high in the Free Market, so even ones you won't use pay for your early gear. Here are seven quests from level 16 to 44 that are a shame to miss, in level order.

Prices move fast this early after launch. Check the current price on the live market or the price sites before you sell.

1. Pia and the Blue Mushrooms (level 16, Henesys)

Take the portal from Henesys into the park; Pia stands at the bottom next to the teddy bear. Bring her Green Mushroom Caps and Blue Mushroom Caps and you're done. Blue Mushrooms are most common in the Blue Mushroom Forest, through a hidden portal in the forest east of Henesys; Green Mushrooms are plentiful in Henesys Hunting Ground III.

The reward is a helmet accuracy scroll at 100%, 60% or 10%. Classes short on accuracy can use it themselves.

Plan ahead: Jane's quest next needs 15 Blue Mushroom Caps too. Collect extra now and you won't have to come back.

2. Jane and the Mushrooms (level 20, Lith Harbor)

Jane is the NPC with the bunny headband inside the house with a sign in Lith Harbor. She wants 15 Blue Mushroom Caps and 15 Horny Mushroom Caps, and 30 kills of each mushroom. Kills only count after you accept the quest, so take it before you start hunting.

Horny Mushrooms live in the Ant Tunnel east of Sleepywood, most of all in Ant Tunnel III and IV. The reward is a weapon attack 60% scroll, one of 14 kinds at random. Even if it isn't for your weapon it sells well, enough to gear up for your 20s. Finishing it also unlocks "Jane's Challenge" at level 44.

3. Proof of Partnership (level 21, Kerning City)

From Lakelis, the NPC who runs the Kerning City party quest, next to the sewer entrance. Clear the party quest with a party of four players level 21 or higher and kill King Slime at the end.

The reward is an earring 60% scroll (STR, DEX, INT or LUK), once per character. Many parties skip King Slime ("rush"), so tell your party before you go in if you have this quest.

4. Arwen and the Glass Shoe (level 29, Ellinia)

The fairy Arwen, under the treehouse on the left halfway up Ellinia, says a burning monster on the road to Perion stole her glass shoe. The culprit is the Fire Boar. They live in the Burnt Land below Perion: go through the two Deep Valley maps and take the hidden portal. Every Burnt Land map from I to V has Fire Boars.

Bring one Glass Shoe and you get a Fairy Wing or a Piece of Ice, 50/50. The quest is repeatable, so every shoe you pick up is another clear. Keep Fire Boar tusks too; they're used in other quests.

5. Shumi's lost money (level 35, Kerning City)

Shumi, right in front of the subway entrance, dropped a coin, a bundle and a sack of money in the construction site. It's pure jump quests, no hunting. Buy a ticket at the subway booth and use the gate to enter. Open the chest at the end of each floor to find Shumi's things.

B1 gives 23 return scrolls, B2 30 Blue Potions, and B3 mesos and 30 Mana Elixirs. All three together are worth over 210,000 EXP. The EXP is the main reason to do it, so if you're good at jump quests, saving them for just before a level-up works well.

6. The Cursed Doll (mid 30s, Ellinia)

From the fairy Rowen inside the treehouse on the right halfway up Ellinia, but it won't appear right away. First finish the Sauna Robe quest chain, available from level 32. The chain alone is worth over 49,000 EXP and gives you a Sauna Robe.

After that, bring Rowen increasing numbers of Cursed Dolls, dropped by Zombie Lupins. The main prize is at the 150-doll stage: a glove attack 60% or glove magic 60% scroll. The earlier stage gives ores, and the later one a job hat that is usually worth little. Zombie Lupins are level 41, so party up if soloing is rough; they gather in the tree dungeon deep in the monkey forest above Ellinia.

7. Jane's Challenge (level 44, Lith Harbor)

If you finished quest 2, this continues at 44. It's three rounds of fetching in a row: monster drops from Drakes, Wraiths, Mushmom spores and Maladies, shop potions, and a Moon Rock made in Ellinia from seven kinds of ore. Each round also has a kill count.

Rewards are 10 Elixirs for round one, 15 Power Elixirs for round two, and at the end one of glove attack 60% or 10% or glove magic 60% or 10%. The monsters for round two are level 55, which is hard at 44, so most players finish it around level 50.

Short on time? Do these three

If you can't do them all, don't skip Jane and the Mushrooms at 20, the Cursed Doll, and Jane's Challenge at 44. All three reward attack scrolls, which sell for the most and upgrade your early gear right away. Wondering whether to use a scroll or sell it?
