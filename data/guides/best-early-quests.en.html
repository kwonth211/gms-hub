The early quests worth doing are Bruce and Ayan at 10, Pia at 16, Jane at 20, Lakelis at 21 and Sabitrama at 25. Ayan's quests unlock the 60% cape scroll at 48, and Jane's unlocks the glove attack scroll quests at 44, so finish both while you are low level. Shane, Pia, Stan and Jane want 130 mushroom caps between them, so collect them all the first time you hunt mushrooms.

Each quest in level order, with NPC locations

Level 10: get Bruce and Ayan out of the way

Bruce stands in front of the big yellow mushroom house on the right side of Henesys. For The Reason Behind the Mushroom Studies, kill 20 Shrooms and 20 Orange Mushrooms in the Mushroom Garden past the hill west of Henesys, and bring 10 Mushroom Spores and 10 Orange Mushroom Caps. Ayan, beside the tepee on the right side of Perion, only wants 30 Stumps dead. With both done, Bruce's I Need to Find My Daughter opens: deliver a toy sword and a letter once each and part one is over.

The rewards are small, but at 48 Ayan offers two alligator hunts. The first pays a class weapon attack 10% scroll of your choice, the second a 60% cape STR, DEX, INT or LUK scroll of your choice. Skip part one and the alligator quests never open.

Levels 10–25: the six crafting apprenticeships

From 10, town craftsmen take you on as an apprentice. There are six professions (blacksmith, weaponcrafter, tailor, carpenter, leatherworker and arcforger), and one character learns all of them. Learn blacksmithing from Silas Irons in Perion first; Mr. Thunder's weaponcrafting quest only appears after it.

Each profession's level 15 quest appears at crafting level 2 and its level 25 quest at crafting level 5. Every level 25 quest pays 3,060 EXP, 18,360 for all six. The catalyst rewards can be traded; an Armor Crafting Catalyst sells for {{price:4130002}} right now.

Levels 14–18: Shane, Pia and Stan in one mushroom trip

At 14, Shane in Ellinia asks twice for 30 Green Mushroom kills and 15 Green Mushroom Caps. The first pays 535 EXP and 5 Bronze Ore, the second 535 EXP and 5 Iron Ore. Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North IV spawns 31 Green Mushrooms, so both finish while you train there.

Pia, at 16, is the child holding a teddy bear in front of the big compass house in the middle of Henesys Park. Bring her 20 Green Mushroom Caps and 20 Blue Mushroom Caps for a random 100%, 60% or 10% hat accuracy scroll. Blue Mushrooms are thickest in the Blue Mushroom Forest, through the hidden portal at the top left of The Forest East of Henesys.

At 18, Alex's Request in Kerning City sends you on to Chief Stan in Henesys. Stan wants 25 Green and 25 Blue Mushrooms killed plus 15 caps of each. He uses Pia's maps, so collect extra caps on the first trip and you never go back.

Level 19: Luke the Security Guy

Follow The Road to the Dungeon out of Henesys; Luke stands by the big tree at the far right of the Henesys Dungeon Entrance map. Kill 20 Jr. Neckis for 10 skins and 20 Stirges for 10 wings, and bring one Salad. Jr. Neckis are thickest in Dangerous Croko I below Kerning City (27), and Stirges in the subway Transfer Area or Dangerous Steam by the Ant Tunnel (38 each).

The reward is one of eight gems, such as a Diamond, Garnet or Topaz, at 12.5% each. No monster drops the Salad; buy it for about 220 mesos from Raymond in the Henesys Town Hall or Max in the Kerning City Civic Center.

Level 20: Jane and the Mushroom

Jane is in Lith Harbor. Kill 30 Blue Mushrooms and 30 Horny Mushrooms, and bring 15 Blue Mushroom Caps and 15 Horny Mushroom Caps. Hunt Horny Mushrooms in Tree Dungeon, Forest Up North III or V (15–16 each) or Ant Tunnel IV (41).

The reward is one of 14 class weapon attack 60% scrolls. Finishing it opens Jane's three challenges at 44, and the last one pays a 60% or 10% glove attack or glove magic attack scroll, 25% each.

Level 21: Lakelis and Proof of Companionship

Lakelis stands next to the three sewer pipes at the bottom of Kerning City. Accept the quest, clear the Kerning party quest to the end and kill the King Slime, and she gives you a 60% earring scroll. Low-level parties sometimes skip the King Slime and fill the passes from the upper floors, so tell your party before you enter that you need him dead. The KPQ guide walks through every stage.

Level 23: the Kerning City hospital quests

The first step trades 40 Stirge kills and 20 quest items for 20 Blue Potions. The second trades 50 Zombie Mushroom kills and 25 Charms of the Undead for an Old Raggedy Cape. Each step pays 2,430 EXP. Ant Tunnel III spawns 61 Zombie Mushrooms, the same map you use for the Zombie Mushroom hunt in Maya's level 21 errands.

Level 25: Sabitrama and the Forest of Patience

Sabitrama stands by the sign at the top of Sleepywood. Pay the entry fee to Shane, by the little house in the trees near the middle of Ellinia, and he sends you into the Forest of Patience. Climb stages 1 to 5 and pick the Pink Anthurium from the herb at the end of stage 5.

Inside the jump maps your speed and jump are locked to the base values and movement skills do not work. Stage 3 is the hardest. After your first clear the entry is free, and at 50 the same forest gives the Double-Rooted Red Ginseng for a Star Rock.

EXP quests to pick up in your 30s

  • Level 29, Arwen and the Glass Shoe: bring Arwen in Ellinia one Glass Shoe from a Fire Boar for 4,430 EXP and a Piece of Ice or a Fairy Wing. It repeats, but the shoe itself sells for {{price:4001000}}, so do it once and sell the shoes after that.
  • Level 30, Maya's collections: once the level 21 errands are done, the first two collections pay 4,780 EXP each. Finish the third and fourth at 47 for the Green Napoleon cape.
  • Level 35, Shumi's Lost Money: three construction-site jump maps, no materials, for 20,160 EXP, 10,000 mesos and 30 Mana Elixirs.
  • Level 37, Riel's Special Taste of Florina Beach: four steps for 29,240 EXP and 15,210 mesos.

Buying materials and selling rewards

  1. If a monster is too hard for you, choose whichever costs less: waiting a few levels or buying the drops.
  2. Keep anything the next quest needs and sell only the rest. Save Blue Mushroom Caps until Jane is done.
  3. Never plan your budget around the best random reward.
  4. Add a reward to your weapon and potion money only after it has actually sold.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose Pia's reward?

No, it is a random 100%, 60% or 10% hat accuracy scroll. Right now the 60% sells for {{price:2040001}}, the 100% for {{price:2040000}} and the 10% for {{price:2040002}}.

I cleared KPQ without taking Lakelis's quest. Can I still do it?

Yes. A run cleared before you accepted the quest does not count, so accept it now and kill the King Slime on your next run. Each character gets the reward once.

Is the Forest of Patience reward a DEX scroll?

No, it is a 60% or 10% overall armor DEF scroll, half and half. The 60% sells for {{price:2040501}} right now, so this is not a money quest. Do it for the 3,060 EXP and 1 fame.

What level are the glove attack scroll quests?

There are two lines. Finish Jane and the Mushroom at 20 and Jane's challenges open at 44; finish the Sauna Robe chain from 32 and Rowen the Fairy's Cursed Doll quests open at 36. The full route is in the glove attack quest guide.

My kills are not counting. Why?

Monsters killed before you accepted the quest do not count. Open your quest window, make sure the quest shows as in progress, and hunt again.

Why do Ayan's quests at level 10?

Because they unlock Taking Out the Alligators at 48. Its second step lets you pick a 60% cape scroll, and that quest never opens without part one. Part one only asks for Shrooms, Orange Mushrooms and Stumps, with nothing to buy.

Are the crafting apprentice quests worth it?

Yes, if you plan to reach 25. The six level 25 quests add up to 18,360 EXP, and the catalysts they pay can be sold. Learn blacksmithing first, since weaponcrafting only opens after it, so start in Perion.
