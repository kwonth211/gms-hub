What does holding out for 2m tie up across five scrolls?

Five scrolls sold at 2m each bring in 10m; at 1.70m each, they bring in 8.50m. The reduction costs 1.50m in total. These are gross asking-price totals before taxes or fees.

If you need claw or glove money now, aim to clear them for 8.50m. If you can wait for a higher price, keep watching how much supply remains below 1.80m before listing. Do not leave an old price untouched for days just because it appeared in this table.

The highest asking price is not your profit, even if you bought the scrolls cheaply. Use what you paid and what you receive from selling.
