A new attack is not automatically more efficient. Count casts per kill and MP used; Heal and freeze do not work the way they did in older MapleStory.

Stats, equipment and recovery

Build around INT and your intended weapon requirements, including bonuses. Keep an MP reserve after purchases. Count only the recovery you actually use, and buy upgrades that change how many casts a kill takes.

Fire/Poison

Each second job has its own detailed guide with skill build, gear and training spots by level: Fire/Poison · Ice/Lightning · Cleric.

Switch to Fire Arrow once it kills in fewer casts than your previous attack. Elemental matchups are yours alone; ignore other classes’ Fire Boar or Drake recommendations. Count time waiting for damage-over-time finishes. Multi-target Poison Breath needs grouped targets to justify its MP use.

Ice/Lightning

Enemies do not stop completely. Use the time gained from slower approaches to restore distance. Single targets and groups cost different MP and damage taken; count them separately. Count your own casts; another Magician’s kill thresholds do not transfer.

For skill builds and training spots in detail, see the Ice/Lightning Wizard guide for levels 30–70.

Cleric

Separate healing from damage. Wraith and Camp footage includes Holy Arrow as the main attack with Heal for recovery; Heal-only grinding is not an automatic route. Keep using earlier attacks while new skills are still low. Include time supporting the party and MP spent attacking or teleporting.

Teleport and Magic Guard

Low-level Teleport can be expensive when used for every movement. Walk when it is safe and save Teleport for repositioning. Decide Magic Guard use from survival margins and recovery costs rather than a blanket instruction to disable it. Death and interrupted sessions also have costs.
