A new attack is not automatically more efficient. Compare casts per kill and MP consumption, and do not assume older MapleStory Heal or freeze mechanics apply unchanged.

Stats, equipment and recovery

Build around INT and your intended weapon requirements, including bonuses. Keep an MP reserve after purchases. Compare usable recovery after overheal, and prioritize upgrades that change a reliable kill threshold.

Fire/Poison

Each second job has its own detailed guide with skill build, gear and training spots by level: Fire/Poison · Ice/Lightning · Cleric.

Compare Fire Arrow with your previous attack. Test wall and platform collisions before choosing a route; an animation passing a target does not guarantee damage. Consider elemental matchups separately from other classes’ Fire Boar or Drake recommendations. Count time waiting for damage-over-time finishes. Multi-target Poison Breath needs grouped targets to justify its MP use.

Ice/Lightning

Do not assume enemies stop completely. Use the time gained from slower approaches to restore distance. Compare isolated and grouped targets separately, including damage taken. Test your own skill rather than borrowing another Magician’s kill thresholds.

For skill builds and training spots in detail, see the Ice/Lightning Wizard guide for levels 30–70.

Cleric

Separate healing from damage. Wraith and Camp footage includes Holy Arrow as the main attack with Heal for recovery; Heal-only grinding is not an automatic route. Earlier attacks may remain useful while new skills develop. Include time supporting the party and MP spent attacking or teleporting.

Teleport and Magic Guard

Low-level Teleport can be expensive when used for every movement. Compare walking with tactical repositioning. Decide Magic Guard use from survival margins and recovery costs rather than a blanket instruction to disable it. Death and interrupted sessions also have costs.
