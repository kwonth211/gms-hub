Magicians advance at level 10 in Ellinia. For first job, max one attack skill to 20 and put 3 points in Magic Guard; every AP goes into INT, with only as much LUK as your next wand asks for. At level 30 you pick Fire/Poison, Ice/Lightning or Cleric.

First job skill order

You get 1 SP when you advance at 10 and 3 SP for every level after, which adds up to 61 SP by level 30. Both builds below spend exactly 61. Magic Claw needs Energy Bolt 1 before you can learn it, so your very first point always goes into Energy Bolt.

  4. Level 23: Magic Guard 3. Magic Armor stays locked until Magic Guard reaches 3.

Max MP Increase adds its percentage the moment you take it, so taking it late loses you nothing. That is why some players skip part of MP Recovery and max Max MP Increase instead. If you plan to run the Kerning party quest, take Magic Guard 1 early because of King Slime.

Energy Bolt or Magic Claw?

Let your second job decide. Fire/Poison takes Magic Claw; Ice/Lightning and Cleric take Energy Bolt. Maxed, both deal 130 magic attack per cast in total, but they behave differently.

  • Energy Bolt: cheaper at 16 MP and longer range at 350. It is a spell that travels to the target, so walls and platforms stop it. It shines on flat maps like Line 1 Area 1, where the Bubblings line up.
  • Magic Claw: hits one monster twice for 20 MP, with a range of 300. Fire Arrow gets blocked by terrain the same way Energy Bolt does, so a Fire/Poison who built Magic Claw in first job has a second attack for spots where Fire Arrow keeps hitting a wall.

Clerics can go the Magic Claw way too. One Cleric in the 40–50 range killed Curse Eyes in two Magic Claws and stayed in that spot all the way to level 65. The full route is in the Cleric guide.

Should Magic Guard stay on?

Keep Magic Guard off while you grind. At max it moves 80% of the damage you take onto your MP, and refilling that damage as MP costs close to twice the mesos. Turn it on for bosses and maps where contact damage hurts. Three points are enough for a button you press when things go wrong.

  • Fire/Poison: the hardest single hit of the three, so it reaches one-hit kills first. Fire Arrow stops at walls and platforms, so you stand lower than the monster.
  • Ice/Lightning: Cold Beam is not blocked by terrain, and it freezes the monster it hits so it moves slowly. Ice-weak maps run from Fire Boars at 35 to Red Drakes at 60.
  • Cleric: Heal refills your HP and your party's, which makes it the easiest start with no money. In exchange it kills slowest solo, and the Jr. Wraith stretch in the 40s is the hardest part of the class.

The second job starts at level 30 when you talk to Grendel the Really Old in Ellinia. He sends you to the Magician Job Instructor in the Forest North of Ellinia, who puts you in a hidden map where you collect 30 Dark Marbles and bring them back. The whole process is in the second job guide.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Putting LUK in early: LUK is there to let you wear wands. Stay at or under 20 LUK until you equip the Mithril Wand at 30, and put every spare AP into INT.
  • Taking a staff over a wand: the level 25 Wizard Staff gives 45 magic attack and needs 20 LUK; the Ice Wand at the same level gives 48 and needs 15. Use wands until second job.
  • Grinding with Magic Guard on: your MP potion bill nearly doubles. Switch it on only when you're about to die.
  • Sticking to maps at your own level: a lower map where monsters die in one or two hits beats a higher map that takes three. Level 39–41 Ice/Lightning Wizards made about 92,000 EXP an hour in an Ant Tunnel party, on a low map.
  • Living with a near miss on one-hit kills: a Magic Potion adds 10 magic attack and costs {{price:2002001}} right now. If a monster survives your hit by a sliver, one potion turns two hits into one.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Energy Bolt to learn Magic Claw?

Yes, Magic Claw needs Energy Bolt 1. In the Magic Claw build your first point still goes into Energy Bolt, and the next 20 go into Magic Claw. Keep that one point even after an SP reset.

Where should a level 26 Magician train?

Go to the Bubblings in Kerning subway Line 1 Area 1. They die in one hit from about level 26, and the flat map suits the Energy Bolt build best. If it's full, go to Excavation Site I or the Kerning party quest.

Why are mage attacks so slow? Is there a Booster?

No second-job Magician has a Booster. None of the Fire/Poison, Ice/Lightning or Cleric skills raise attack speed, so the only way to kill faster is to need fewer casts. Spend your mesos on wands and glove magic attack scrolls that push you over a one-hit threshold.

How much LUK do I need?

Only what your next wand asks for: 20 for the Mithril Wand at 30, 25 for the Wizard Wand at 35, 30 for the Fairy Wand at 40 and 40 for the Cromi at 50. LUK on your gear counts, so put in that much less. If you already overspent, an AP Reset Scroll ({{price:5050100}} on the Free Market) moves the points back to INT.

Should I really max Magic Armor?

Yes. Those points have nowhere better to go before level 30, so Magic Armor gets all 20 after Magic Guard 3. It raises weapon and magic defense for up to 600 seconds, and your attack is already maxed, so it costs no killing speed.

Which second job is best with no money?

Cleric. Heal tops up your HP, so you hardly buy HP potions, and the ones you pick up while grinding can be sold for spending money. Undead maps run level after level from Jr. Wraith to Wraith to the Excavation tombs. The trade-off is slower solo killing than Fire/Poison or Ice/Lightning.

When and where is the first job advancement?

At level 10 in Ellinia. An NPC in Lith Harbor sends you straight to your class town; ride it to Ellinia and talk to Grendel the Really Old. The advancement can't be undone.
