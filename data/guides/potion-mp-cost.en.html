Your level went up. Your potion hotkey stayed the same.

Restocking the potion you used early on is easy and familiar. Small refills also waste little when only a small amount of MP is missing. But a growing MP pool takes more presses of the same old potion to fill.

Elixir restores 35% of maximum HP and MP in this game. At 4,000 maximum MP, that is 1,400 MP per bottle. When that much is missing, our saved prices make it cheaper than restoring the same amount with Blue Potions.

Receiving all the recovery from 100 Elixirs replaces 700 Blue Potions at that maximum MP. The saved price difference was 58,200 mesos. A small difference on each refill accumulates as you keep hunting and restocking.

Pressing Elixir after a small MP loss wastes the bottle

A bigger MP pool does not mean replacing every refill with Elixir. If only 500 MP is missing, the rest of its recovery has nowhere to go. You bought a large refill and used it as a small one.

Keep Blue Potions and Elixirs together. Use the small potion for small gaps and Elixir for large gaps. You do not need to wait until you cannot cast a needed skill just to receive every last point of recovery. The small potion fills the space between larger refills.

Elixirs also have a reuse cooldown. Use their combined recovery when HP and MP are heavily depleted together, then use ordinary potions while waiting to use one again. Keep those ordinary potions in the inventory.

When a cheap Mana Elixir listing catches your eye

Put that listing beside the Elixir price you would pay. Mana Elixir restores more MP per bottle. The useful question is how much more you pay for it.

The saved Elixir median was 888 mesos. At that price, Mana Elixir at 1,268 mesos or less costs less per MP. Listings below that line existed in the saved market. Searching only for your usual potion would miss that buying opportunity.

If your hunting also drains HP, count the HP potions too. Elixir refills HP as well, while Mana Elixir only refills MP. When the MP savings go straight into extra HP potions, Elixir can be the cheaper and easier choice.

Change the next restock

At around 1,500 maximum MP, keep Blue Potion as the main refill. Around 2,500, add Elixirs alongside it. Give Elixir more of the large refills as the pool grows. Keep Power Elixirs for a large refill when HP is heavily depleted too.

After changing hunting grounds or equipment, look at what you used during the session. Taking fewer hits or killing faster changes the balance of HP and MP potions you need. Restock what ran short in that session instead of filling the bag with the same quantities every time.

Prices and arithmetic

These are median asking prices from listings scanned within 24 hours, saved at 14:37 KST on November 28. NPC sell-back values are not used. Repeated seller, channel, room and price records count once.

Recovery uses MCW 1.2 item values: 200 MP, 35% HP and MP, 50% MP, and 100% HP and MP. The calculation rounds maximum MP × recovery rate down to an integer. Actual recovery is the smaller of that amount and missing MP. Cost per 1,000 is price ÷ actual recovery × 1,000; table values are rounded.
