At what maximum MP should you add Elixirs?

At today's medians, rounding fractional MP recovery down, Elixir becomes cheaper per MP than Blue Potion at 2,418 maximum MP. Mana Elixir reaches that point at 2,666, and Power Elixir at 2,434.

Start carrying Elixirs alongside ordinary potions around 2,500 maximum MP. A larger MP pool does not make Elixir the right answer for every refill. Fill small gaps with Blue Potions and large gaps with Elixirs.

The Power Elixir figure requires receiving a whole MP bar's worth of recovery. Elixir is easier to use for regular partial refills. Keep Power Elixirs for moments when your HP is heavily depleted too and you need a large refill at once.

What is a good price for Mana Elixir?

For MP alone, Mana Elixir restores 50 ÷ 35 times as much as Elixir. With Elixir at 888 mesos, buy Mana Elixir at 1,268 mesos or less for a lower price per MP. Elixir wins against the 1,399 median, but the saved lowest Mana Elixir listing of 1,000 is below that buying line.

When hunting also drains HP, Elixir adds 35% HP recovery. If Mana Elixir leaves you repeatedly buying HP potions too, add those costs together. For new prices, use Elixir price × 50 ÷ 35 as your MP-only Mana Elixir buying line.

Prices and arithmetic

These are median asking prices from listings scanned within 24 hours, saved at 14:37 KST on November 28. NPC sell-back values are not used. Repeated seller, channel, room and price records count once.

Recovery uses MCW 1.2 item values: 200 MP, 35% HP and MP, 50% MP, and 100% HP and MP. The calculation rounds maximum MP × recovery rate down to an integer. Actual recovery is the smaller of that amount and missing MP. Cost per 1,000 is price ÷ actual recovery × 1,000; table values are rounded.
