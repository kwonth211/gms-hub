A Cleric maxes Holy Arrow by level 41, takes Heal to 5 to unlock Invincible, then raises Invincible if you solo or Bless if you party. For training, follow the undead: Jr. Wraith at 35 → Curse Eye and Zombie Lupin in the 40s → Wraith at 48 → the Excavation tombs at 52 → the military camp at 58. Heal damages undead monsters only.

Even at level 1, MP Eater pulls more MP out of a Jr. Wraith (70 MP) than one Energy Bolt costs (16 MP). One point at 30 cuts your potion bill right away. Skeledogs, Mummydogs and skeletons have 0 MP, though, so it gets nothing from them.

Another road: Magic Claw at the Curse Eye caves

If you built Magic Claw in first job, you can get through 40–50 without Holy Arrow. Take Heal 1 at 30, run the Kerning party quest to 40, and max MP Eater and Teleport on the way. From 40, kill Curse Eyes in the Curse Eye caves with two Magic Claws each. In a party of 2–3 that's 1,800–2,000 EXP a minute, 100,000–120,000 an hour.

This spot makes money. You take in 80,000–90,000 mesos an hour plus 30,000–40,000 in drops and spend 50,000–60,000 on potions. Save that way to level 50 and you can afford a Cromi and +12 magic attack gloves. The moment Curse Eyes die in one hit, you jump to 3,000 EXP a minute and 100,000 mesos an hour, with potions under 10,000.

Can you grind with Heal?

Heal only damages undead. It splits 6 slots and you take one, so solo it hits up to 5 undead, but the more it hits the less each takes, and it doesn't carry the holy element. A level 49 Cleric with five successful scrolls each on gloves and wand did about 270 per Heal. So solo, kill with Holy Arrow and use Heal for recovery and for pulling groups.

Mixing the two works well. Healing Wraiths in Subway Depot B3 and finishing with Holy Arrow gives 255,000 EXP an hour. Profit is 35,000 mesos an hour, about 70,000 once you sell the drops. B3 sits behind a jump map, so getting in takes about 3 minutes.

  • Heal next to undead and the monsters it hits chase you, and you heal less. When you only want HP, step away first.
  • In a party, members fill Heal's slots first, so the bigger the party, the fewer undead it hits.
  • As a party healer, don't spam Heal in one spot. Move around the map and cast once near each party member.

Undead in Forgotten Hollow

Forgotten Hollow, open since November 18, has undead that Heal can hit. Rotten Mushroom (56, 3,520 HP, 183 EXP) spawns 14 to a map in Dilapidated Tomb and 10 in Another Sanctuary. Sporewood (59, 4,834 HP) and the boss Rotten Mushmom (60) are holy-weak undead too. A level 64 mage killing Rotten Mushrooms in two hits in a party of 4 made 2,650 EXP a minute in Another Sanctuary, but the Jr. Boogies there keep cursing you.

The area map and where each monster spawns are on the Forgotten Hollow page.

Frequently asked questions

Can I grind with Heal alone?

Only on undead maps. Heal damages undead and nothing else, and solo its hit is small, so mixing it with Holy Arrow is faster. Heal-led grinding on Wraiths in Subway Depot B3 gives 255,000 EXP an hour.

Does Bless raise Heal damage too?

Yes. Bless raises Heal's recovery for the whole party, and the damage Heal does to undead goes up with it. That's why the party build takes Bless to 16 early.

Should I equip an Arc Staff at 45?

Yes, if you're going to 40 LUK anyway. The Arc Staff has 73 magic attack, 4 more than the Fairy Wand, and the level 50 Cromi also needs 40 LUK, so you're just reaching it five levels early. It sells for {{price:1382005}} right now.

What level should I start the military camp?

Level 58. Camp 1 has 20 Skeleton Soldiers out of 25 monsters, so you need 111 accuracy, and around 60 you move on to Camp 3 with its Officers (120 accuracy). The usual setup is a party of 6 with one person per floor on floors 1–4.

Where does a level 56–60 Cleric train?

Remains Tomb III, Camp 1, or the Rotten Mushroom maps in Forgotten Hollow. Tomb III asks for little accuracy and works solo; the camp and the Rotten Mushroom maps are for parties.

Why does Holy Arrow keep getting blocked?

Because walls and terrain stop it. Stand on flat ground at the monster's height and cast with your feet on the ground. On maps full of platforms, teleport up to the monster's floor before you attack.
