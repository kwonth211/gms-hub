Cleric is the second most played second-job mage right now. Yet the community line is that "second job has nothing going for it, and everyone plays it for third job". Others say the opposite: if you're starting with no money, play a Cleric. Heal tops up your HP so you buy fewer potions, and undead training spots run level after level, from Jr. Wraith to Wraith to the tombs. Skill numbers below come from the game data; the hunting notes are reports from players who levelled the class.

Heal hunting or Holy Arrow

Many players pick Cleric hoping to hunt with Heal, but right now most say Heal hunting is hard.

  • Heal splits 6 slots, and you take one. Solo it hits up to 5 undead, but the more it hits, the less each one takes, and it doesn't carry the holy element.
  • A level 49 Cleric with five successful scrolls each on gloves and wand still only did about 270 per Heal.
  • A level 60 Cleric who gathered Mummydogs and Healed them down found the EXP good but lost mesos solo.

So guide sites and most high-level Clerics make Holy Arrow the main attack. It's a holy spell that hits one monster at 160 magic attack, 25% more on holy-weak monsters. Until it's maxed, though, it's no better than your first-job attack: level 16 Holy Arrow is 130 magic attack, the same as maxed Energy Bolt, but costs more MP and has half the mastery. By guide-site maths it beats your first-job attack from level 18 on holy-weak monsters and level 24 on everything else.

Stats and gear

INT first, LUK only to what your wand needs. Wands go Mithril (30) → Wizard (35) → Fairy (40) → Cromi (50). Spells take the same time to cast whatever the weapon speed, so staff or wand, wear the higher magic attack. A level 60 Cleric's advice: "a well-scrolled Fairy Wand is enough, don't chase expensive weapons". See the Magician equipment list for each level.

  • Scroll gloves for magic attack, not critical rate.
  • Clerics don't get Meditation (+25 magic attack) like Ice/Lightning and Fire/Poison. With the same gear you're 25 magic attack short, which makes one-hit-kill thresholds that much harder to reach.
  • Heal covers your HP, so sell the HP potions you pick up instead of drinking them.

The job advancement NPC still describes Bless as raising accuracy and avoid. Overseas players were surprised to find that effect was removed at launch and replaced with damage reduction. In parties, Bless and Heal are so welcome that overseas players come back for them every time they're up.

Skill build: three roads

  1. Level 30: Heal 1 (self-healing). Next level, Teleport 1 and MP Eater 1
  2. Option A (guide-site pick): Holy Arrow first. Max Holy Arrow by 41 → Heal 5 to open Invincible → Bless if you party, Invincible if you solo
  3. Option B: Teleport first. While Holy Arrow is weak you're grinding with first-job attacks (Magic Claw, Energy Bolt) anyway, so make moving easy first
  4. Option C: Heal first, then an SP reset into Holy Arrow. A fair number of overseas Clerics went this way. It takes one SP Reset from the Cash Shop.

The short version

Cleric skill numbers haven't changed once since launch. The 10/29 patch buffed only Ice/Lightning and Fire/Poison, and plenty of players asked why Clerics were left out. Right now Cleric is a class you level on staying power, not damage. You hardly buy HP potions and there's an undead map at every level, but getting through Jr. Wraith in the 40s is the hardest part. When Forgotten Hollow opens on November 18, more undead that Heal can hit arrive: Rotten Mushroom, Sporewood and the boss Rotten Mushmom, so it's worth taking another look at Cleric then.
