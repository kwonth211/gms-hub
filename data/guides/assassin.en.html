Assassin is the most played second job right now; overseas players even call it "the class with no weakness". Yet plenty of Korean players hit a wall after 50 and switch classes. Until third job you have no multi-target attack: Lucky Seven alone has to carry you to 70. So your second-job points should go into making Lucky Seven hit harder. Skill numbers below come from the game data; the hunting notes are reports from players who levelled the class.

In one look

  • Skill order: Claw Mastery → Critical Throw → Haste → Critical Recovery → Drain last
  • Stats: DEX only to your claw's requirement, everything else LUK
  • Maps: Stirges → (35) Murky Swamp, Burnt Land or Lorangs → (40) Cold Eyes or Zombie Lupins → (48) Wraiths → (55) Golems, Drakes or the Camps

Stats and accuracy

Most Assassin damage comes from LUK; DEX is for wearing claws and hitting things. Claws need DEX equal to their level, so a level 50 claw needs 50 DEX including gear.

Skimp too much and you miss. One player worked out that a level 40 Assassin with 25 DEX hits Zombie Lupin only 82% of the time. A miss still spends two stars and the MP, so check accuracy with the hits-to-kill calculator before moving maps.

Second job skills at a glance

SkillMaxWhat it does (at max)
Claw Mastery20Minimum damage up, attack +20, and a 25% chance a star comes back. Level 3 opens Critical Throw, level 5 opens Booster
Claw Booster20Attack speed +2 steps from level 1; more levels only extend the duration (300 seconds at max)
Critical Throw30Critical rate 15%, critical damage +35%
Haste20Whole party nearby: speed +30, jump +10, 300 seconds
Critical Recovery20Chance to restore HP and MP on a critical (100% for 10% each at max), once every 30 seconds
Drain30One monster at 240%, MP 28. Needs Critical Recovery 3

Skill build (the common order)

  1. Level 30: Haste 1
  2. By about 32: Claw Mastery 5, then Claw Booster 1. One point already gives full attack speed.
  3. By about 37: Claw Mastery 20. The +20 attack cuts your hits-to-kill right away.
  4. 38 to 47: Critical Throw 30. On its own it doesn't change your usual hits-to-kill, but Critical Recovery only triggers on crits, so it comes first.
  5. Then: max Haste. Moving between platforms and gathering monsters gets much easier, and the whole party gets it.
  6. Leftover points: Critical Recovery, then Claw Booster.
  7. Drain last: 240% looks strong, but Lucky Seven throws two 140% stars, 280% in total. For grinding, Lucky Seven wins.

This reflects the current balance and may change with patches or new maps.

Star attack adds straight onto claw attack, so upgrading stars feels like upgrading your claw a tier. Shops sell up to Wolbi; Kumbi and Tobi are expensive, so Wolbi is enough until you have money. Check current prices on the live market.

  • Scroll gloves for attack, not critical rate: crits only matter when they land, attack counts on every star.
  • Bring several usable stacks of stars rather than one expensive stack, so grinding never stops.
  • Players report that the level 35 female-only thief overall (Dark Avenger) carries LUK, so with the same scrolls it ends up with more LUK than the male one.

Where to train by level

Assassins kill one monster at a time from range, so the test is "does it die in two Lucky Sevens, and do I never miss", not how many monsters a map spawns. This order is cross-checked across several Classic World assassin guides and one measured test.

Do not camp Ant Tunnel Park or Evil Eyes. In the same 35 to 37 test Ant Tunnel Park gave about 70,000 EXP an hour, near the bottom. The Evil Eye cave floor 4 to 1 loop is a Bandit (dagger) route. Damage calculators can rank the Ant Tunnel high, but by the mid 30s the maps above are faster.

Standing too close makes you punch with the claw instead of throwing, which does almost nothing. Jump backwards while throwing and the stars come out even up close.

In short

Some say "after 50 there's nothing but Lupins", but that stretch also has to be done with Lucky Seven alone. Players say third job (Hermit) opens on the test server in early 2027; until then, follow this order and build up Lucky Seven. Pick a map for your level from the Assassin training routes.
