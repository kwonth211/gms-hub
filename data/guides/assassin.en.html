Put an Assassin's points in this order: Haste 1, Claw Mastery 20 (by level 37), Critical Throw 30 (by 47), Haste 20, Critical Recovery 20, Claw Booster, and Drain last. For stats, raise DEX only to your claw's level and put everything else into LUK. There is no multi-target attack before third job, so Lucky Seven alone carries you to 70.

Skill order by level, and why

  1. Level 30: Haste 1. Chasing Stirges between platforms gets faster right away.
  2. Level 32: Claw Mastery 5, then Claw Booster 1. A single point of Booster already gives full attack speed; more levels only extend how long it lasts.
  3. Level 37: Claw Mastery 20. The +20 attack applies to every star you throw, so your hits-to-kill drops at once. Stars also come back 25% of the time, which cuts recharge costs.
  4. Level 47: Critical Throw 30. It barely changes your usual hits-to-kill, but Critical Recovery only triggers on crits, so this goes first.
  5. Level 54: Haste 20. The +30 speed goes to the whole party, which makes you welcome in Wraith and Camp parties.
  6. Level 60: Critical Recovery 20. A guaranteed 10% HP and MP every 30 seconds cuts your potion bill.
  7. Level 67: Claw Booster 20. All it does is save you recasts.
  8. Level 70: Drain 11. 240% looks strong, but Lucky Seven throws two 140% stars, 280% in total. Grind with Lucky Seven.

Stats and accuracy: what you hit at which level

Most Assassin damage comes from LUK. DEX is only there to wear claws and land hits. Claws need DEX equal to their level, so the level 40 Steel Avarice needs 40 DEX and the level 50 Steel Slain needs 50, gear included.

Skimp too much and you miss. A level 40 Assassin with 25 DEX hits Zombie Lupin only 82% of the time. A miss still spends two stars and the MP. At level 45 you land only 72% on Wraiths, so go there from 49.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

  • Standing too close: up close you swing the claw instead of throwing, which does almost nothing. Jump backwards while you throw and the stars come out even with a monster on top of you.
  • Taking Drain early: you grind with Lucky Seven. Drain gets the last 11 of your 121 points.
  • Moving before your accuracy is there: a missed throw still costs MP and stars. Pass the number in the accuracy table, then move.
  • Carrying one expensive stack: when it runs out, so does your session. Bring several usable stacks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I max Critical Recovery before Claw Mastery?

Take Claw Mastery first. Lucky Seven works off LUK and ignores Claw Mastery's mastery bonus, but Mastery 20's +20 attack and 25% star return apply to every throw. Critical Recovery only triggers on crits, so it pays off only after Critical Throw is up.

Why does Drain barely heal me?

Drain only turns part of the damage it deals into HP, capped at 25% of your max HP per cast, and never more than the monster's max HP. Its 240% also hits weaker than Lucky Seven's 280%, which is why it comes last.

Where should a level 40 Assassin train?

Go to the Cold Eyes in Another Path: 23 of them and nothing else, and 82 accuracy means no misses. If it is full, go to Lorang Lorang; at 95 accuracy, move to the Zombie Lupins in Monkey Swamp III.

Does a well-scrolled Meba two-shot Lupins around 40?

Once each star does at least 334, every Lupin dies in two casts. Lupin has 1,336 HP, and two Lucky Sevens throw four stars. Put your Meba's attack and your stars into the hits-to-kill calculator to get your number.

Where do I go at 47?

Back to Lorang Lorang: from 47 to 50 the Lorangs are profitable. Once you reach 104 accuracy and Wraiths die in two casts, move to Line 2 Area 3.

Is it true Assassins fall off after 50?

The saying comes from having no multi-target attack until 70; Lucky Seven does all the work. On packed maps like the Wraith lines and the Camps, two casts per monster still clear fast. That is why so many Assassins grind the Camps and the tombs in their sixties.

When should I buy the Steel Slain?

At level 50, once you have 50 DEX and 105 LUK. It costs about {{price:1472018}} right now and drops from Stone Golems. If that is too much, scroll a Steel Avarice ({{price:1472014}}) and swap later.
