Choose platforms that let you attack before contact. The detailed second-job route here is for Hunters; Crossbowmen should follow the Crossbowman guide for their weapon and skills.

Equipment and reliable kills

Check requirements and gear bonuses before assigning stats. Judge upgrades by consistent kill thresholds, not a lucky maximum hit. Check that you stop missing with your own Focus level and equipment.

Early Hunter progression

After advancing, hunt slow Ligators from range, then move to Deep Ant Tunnel I with assigned platforms. Use the spots that cover the roof and cut down on falls and unnecessary climbing. Jr. Wraith readiness depends on accuracy investment.

Lorang in the forties

A Hunter reached reliable two-hit Lorang kills with an upgraded Valter 2000 at 43 after failing that threshold at 40. Check your own damage and party role. The upper platforms are safer; the first floor never runs dry but you take more hits. Keep an earlier map when those conditions fail.

Skill decisions

Each second job has its own detailed guide with skill build, weapons and training spots by level: Hunter · Crossbowman.

Prepare Soul Arrow and build Bow Mastery for steadier damage. Manage Booster and Soul Arrow together. Use Arrow Bomb where targets group up and a single-target attack where they do not; do not copy Hunter skill names into a Crossbowman build.

Later maps

Wraith needs an accuracy check; Cold Eye needs suitable firing positions. Dark Stone Golem can remain expensive even after accuracy investment. Go with a fixed budget; it is not a default low-cost route.
