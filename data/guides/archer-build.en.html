Put every AP into DEX until level 10, then raise STR only to your next weapon's requirement and put everything else into DEX. First-job skill order: Arrow Blow 1 → The Eye of Amazon 3 → Arrow Blow 20 → Critical Shot 15 → The Eye of Amazon 15 → Focus 10. At level 30, see Athena Pierce in Henesys and become a Hunter or a Crossbowman.

Stat build: DEX and STR by weapon

DEX is your main stat; STR goes in only so you can equip your weapon. No INT, no LUK. From the level 15 Composite Bow on, a bow needs STR equal to its level, and crossbows ask for less. STR on your gear counts toward the requirement.

The advancement starts with Athena Pierce in Henesys. She gives you a letter for the Bowman Job Instructor on The Road to the Dungeon, who sends you into a hidden map to collect 30 Black Marbles from its monsters. Hand them in, take the proof back to Athena Pierce, and you have your new job. The first two steps give 4,780 EXP each.

Knockback and back-to-back hits in the test map burn more potions than normal training. Bring plenty of HP and MP potions plus arrows. The whole process is in the second job guide.

Training after 30, in short

A new Hunter shoots slow Ligators from range. In the late 30s go to Deep Ant Tunnel I and shoot several floors from the roof platforms; in a party, split the floors. At 40 Lorang does not die in two shots, and one Hunter moved to Lorang Lorang at 43 with an upgraded Vaulter 2000.

Crossbowmen at the same levels head for long straight platforms like Line 1 <Area 2>. At 40 the Ant Tunnel IV mushrooms take two shots, and from 50 both classes one-shot the mushrooms in Ant Tunnel III. Dark Stone Golems in Sleepy Dungeon cost one Hunter money even after accuracy potions and gear, so leave them off your route.

On Lorang Lorang, shoot from the left platforms and the top, and avoid routes that drop you onto the floor into contact damage. Floor 1 never runs dry but you take more hits; floor 2 saves potions. Without reliable two-shot kills, stay longer in Deep Ant Tunnel or Burnt Land. See every option on the level 43 Hunter routes.

Accuracy and when to change gear

Upgrade by how many shots your minimum damage needs, not by one lucky big hit. If a new weapon doesn't cut a shot off your kills, scrolling the one you hold is cheaper.

With Focus at 10 by level 30, Jr. Wraiths (82 accuracy) stop dodging from level 35. For anything tougher, scroll your hat for accuracy: 100% gives +1 ({{price:2040000}}), 60% gives +2 ({{price:2040001}}).

Bows and crossbows use separate arrows. Iron Arrows for Bows add +2 attack and Bronze Arrows +1. After second job, Soul Arrow lets you fire without using any.

Common Bowman mistakes and the fix

  • Raising STR early → stop at your next weapon's requirement. Everything else is DEX.
  • Shooting with a monster on top of you → inside 65 you swing the bow weakly instead of shooting. Step back one tile and fire.
  • Trying to shoot in mid-jump → attacks don't come out while jumping. Land first, then shoot.
  • Shooting past a slope → floors and slopes block arrows. Move to the monster's height.
  • Copying the Hunter skill order as a Crossbowman → Crossbowmen finish Booster and Soul Arrow before Final Attack.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Bowman good at?

Killing from range before monsters reach you. With The Eye of Amazon at 15, Arrow Blow reaches 470, so from a ledge you almost never take contact damage. The catch is terrain: arrows stop at floors and slopes, so where you stand matters.

Which first-job skills come first?

Arrow Blow 1, Power Knockback 1 and The Eye of Amazon 3, then Arrow Blow to 20. After that, Critical Shot 15, The Eye of Amazon 15 and Focus 10. That spends exactly 61 points by level 30.

Do I need Double Shot?

No, it isn't in the standard order; Arrow Blow alone carries you to 30. Future Hunters sometimes max it to cover the early 30s while Arrow Bomb is still low.

How much STR should a Bowman have?

Only your next weapon's requirement. Bows need STR equal to their level from 15 (30 for the level 30 Ryden); the level 30 Eagle Crow crossbow needs 20. Undecided? Follow the bow numbers.

Where do I do the second job advancement?

Talk to Athena Pierce in Henesys at level 30. Deliver her letter to the Bowman Job Instructor on The Road to the Dungeon, collect 30 Black Marbles in the hidden map, and bring the proof back to her.

Does Focus really need 10 points?

Yes, if you want to hunt Jr. Wraiths from level 35. If melee monsters keep reaching you, a Focus 3 and Power Knockback 8 split also works.
