At level 30 a bowman chooses Hunter or Crossbowman. This guide covers what comes next: the order to put SP in, when to change bows and crossbows and where to hunt from 30 to 50. The detailed differences between the two jobs are in the second job guide.

Hunter or Crossbowman, in short

At 30, see Athena Pierce in Henesys to advance. Hunters use bows, Crossbowmen crossbows.

  • Hunters crit more often and move faster thanks to mastery. Final Attack fires three arrows that spread over up to three monsters. The area attack, Arrow Bomb, explodes and hits monsters around the target.
  • Crossbowmen deal more damage on crits. Final Attack is a single arrow as strong as the Hunter's three combined, and Iron Arrow pierces up to six monsters lined up on the same platform. It doesn't travel far and can't hit monsters on other floors.
  • Crossbows need 10 less STR than bows of the same level, so you can put that much more into DEX, but they attack more slowly.

Hunter if you like firing fast, Crossbowman if you like big single hits. Third job isn't out, so nobody knows yet which ends up stronger.

SP order

  1. Mastery 5, then Booster 1 and Final Attack 1. Booster unlocks at mastery 5; even at level 1 it speeds attacks up two steps, and more levels only improve duration and MP cost. Final Attack is pressed when its icon lights up at the top right while you attack, so one point makes it usable right away. It switches off if you don't press it within 60 seconds.
  2. At 33, 1 point in the crit slow skill. It slows monsters you crit, so it triggers more often for Hunters.
  3. Finish mastery by 38. Your minimum damage goes up and more monsters die in one hit. Around 37, Wooden Masks in the Excavation Site die to one shot every time.
  4. Then Crossbowmen take Iron Arrow. On a flat platform it hits all six with one shot.
  5. Hunters choose between Final Attack and Arrow Bomb.

Hunter: Final Attack or Arrow Bomb first?

This is where Hunters disagree the most. Decide by where you hunt.

  • Final Attack first: Arrow Bomb at level 1 does far less than Arrow Blow and needs a fair few levels to be useful. Final Attack lands all three arrows even on a single monster. On maps where you kill one at a time, like Drakes, Final Attack is better.
  • Arrow Bomb first: on maps like Lorangs or Cold Eyes, where five or six monsters pile up while you climb ropes or get knocked back, bursting them together beats shooting one at a time. It also catches Lupins hiding behind Dark Stone Golems.

Soul Arrow (no arrow use) needs Booster at level 5, so with Booster 1 you can't take it yet.

Stats and arrows

Put only as much AP into STR as your next bow needs and everything else into DEX. Crossbowmen can put more into DEX thanks to the lower STR requirement.

Making arrows yourself is cheap. Save the Stiff Feathers that Wooden Masks drop: one Processed Wood and one feather make 500 arrows through woodworking.

40Vaulter 2000 (attack 52 → 60, one step slower)Heckler (one step faster than Eagle Crow)

The Vaulter 2000 is a little slower than the Red Viper but turns more monsters into two-hit kills, so most players switch. A well-scrolled Red Viper is fine to keep, though. Crossbowmen attack two steps slower than Hunters from 35 to 40, which makes that stretch feel sluggish.

Where to hunt from 30 to 50

  • 30-35: KPQ and the swamp. Gator runs in the Kerning party quest give the best EXP, and parties that do the bonus stage still profit after potions. Solo, the swamp Ligators are slow, so you get hit less, and they drop level 30 and 40 daggers.
  • 35-43: Jr. Wraiths, Deep Ant Tunnel I. Assuming you maxed Focus at first job, you stop missing Jr. Wraiths around 35. In Deep Ant Tunnel I, shots from the top floor reach the floors below, a good Hunter spot. The Excavation Site also turns a profit after potions.
  • 40-51: Fire Boars, Lorangs. With a Vaulter 2000, Fire Boars usually take two hits, one on a crit. Lorangs take exactly two hits at 43 with a well-scrolled Vaulter. The left side, with platforms, is better than the right, where a fall takes you all the way down.
  • 43+: Drakes in the valley. You stop missing from 43. Shooting from the first-floor sniping spot gets you hit less while covering a wide area. It's crowded, so getting into a party isn't easy.
  • 50+: Cold Eyes, Wraiths, Sleepywood Dungeon. Cold Eyes make good money but are always busy; hunt comfortably from the rooftop or use the sniping spot two floors down. Wraiths in subway Line 1 Area 4 move sluggishly and are hard to hit. Dark Stone Golems in the Sleepywood Dungeon are for your early 50s once accuracy potions and a helmet accuracy scroll stop the misses, but they hit harder than expected and can lose you money.

One Hunter's actual route: Ligators at 31-35, Deep Ant Tunnel I at 35-43, Lorangs at 43-51 after scrolling the weapon, then Cold Eyes from 51. Doing it again, they'd aim for Cold Eye parties from the early-to-mid 40s and fall back to Lorangs or Fire Boars if no spot was free.
