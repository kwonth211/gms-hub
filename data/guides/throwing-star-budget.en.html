Where does another 2 ATT get expensive?

Kumbi to Tobi takes star attack from 21 to 23. Tobi to Steely takes it from 23 to 25. Both add 2 ATT, but the median price gaps are 3.41m and nearly 15.70m per set. Steely is a large extra purchase even after you own Tobi.

When should your claw or gloves come first?

Count the attacks needed to kill your current target. Spending another 3.41m on Tobi does not save an attack if the monster still takes two hits. In the Hits-to-Kill Calculator, keep the rest of your equipment fixed and change only the star attack.

Glove attack applies whichever stars you use. Before putting all the money into one Tobi set, price your glove upgrade with the 4, 6 and 8 ATT Work Gloves table. If you are close to the claw changes in the Assassin guide, keep the new claw money aside first.

Prices and arithmetic

The prices are listings still on sale and scanned within 24 hours, saved at 14:37 KST on November 28. Repeated seller, channel, room and price records count once. Each listing contributes one price to the median. Prices are not divided by the remaining number of stars, and listing counts are not stock counts.

Attack and base capacity use MCW 1.2 item values. Basket cost is median price per set × set count. An upgrade gap is the new median minus the old median. Half-meso medians come from averaging the two middle prices.
