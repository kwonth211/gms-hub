When you have saved enough to shop for better stars, leave yourself enough sets to keep hunting after the new set runs out. Build a supply of Kumbi, then add the Tobi you need. That leaves more room for the rest of your equipment than emptying your wallet on one set.

Our market data made the gaps between Kumbi, Tobi and Steely more useful than their order in a list. Attack rises a little at each step, but the money needed rises sharply before Steely. The next star in the progression does not have to be your next purchase.

The hunting session continues after the new stars run out

Seeing more damage with a better set feels good. Once that set runs out and you return to your old stars, the damage drops again. Plan for both parts of the session when buying.

With a 10m budget, five Kumbi sets cost about 4.45m at the saved prices. If Kumbi already kills your main target in one hit, stop there and keep the remaining money for a claw or gloves. A monster that already dies in one attack does not need your whole star budget.

If Tobi is what gets that one-hit kill, start with one set. One Tobi plus four Kumbi came to about 7.86m. You can keep hunting on Kumbi after Tobi runs out while saving for another set.

Steely does not need to be the purchase immediately after Tobi

Kumbi to Tobi already costs money, but Steely deserves a fresh look at the wallet. It adds 2 ATT over Tobi at a median price gap of roughly 15.70m per set. Replacing several sets at once can tie up money intended for other equipment.

Before spending it, put your hunting target into the Hits-to-Kill Calculator. If Steely does not remove an attack, keep the next claw's money aside. That matters especially near the weapon changes in the Assassin guide.

Look at gloves too. Glove attack applies while using either Kumbi or Tobi. See which Work Gloves upgrade the cost of one better star set could buy. That gives the next equipment purchase a clearer purpose.

Some more expensive stars have the same attack

Wooden Top can look like a stronger step above Mokbi. Their attack is the same; the difference is base capacity. In the saved market data, their median prices were almost identical too. At the same price, choose Wooden Top to carry a little more.

Kumbi and Icicle also have the same attack. The premium for Icicle buys base capacity. Use Kumbi while equipment attack is urgent, and spend on Icicle when inventory space or recharge trips matter more to you.

Owning several sets does not add their attack together. It buys more throws before you need to refill. Work out whether your hunting is interrupted by low damage or by running out of usable stars, then buy for that problem.

Look at the mesos left before leaving the shop

Once you have chosen a star, include the number of sets in the budget. A plan based on one set's price grows quickly when you fill several slots. Keep claw and glove money aside, build your Kumbi supply, and add the Tobi needed for your target.

Prices and arithmetic

The prices are listings still on sale and scanned within 24 hours, saved at 14:37 KST on November 28. Repeated seller, channel, room and price records count once. Each listing contributes one price to the median. Prices are not divided by the remaining number of stars, and listing counts are not stock counts.

Attack and base capacity use MCW 1.2 item values. Basket cost is median price per set × set count. An upgrade gap is the new median minus the old median. Half-meso medians come from averaging the two middle prices.
