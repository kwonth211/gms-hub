Page is the least played second-job warrior right now: about 3,000 of roughly 22,000 ranked second-job warriors, one in seven. Early on, players complained that Threaten, the Page's own skill, was useless. People who levelled one disagree: used well, Threaten lets a melee warrior take far fewer hits and makes grinding much easier. Skill numbers below come from the game data; the hunting records come from Pages who reached level 50.

Blunt weapon or sword?

Mastery, Booster and Final Attack come in separate sword and blunt versions, so decide right after the advancement.

Level 50 is where it gets hard. The level 50 two-handed sword, Lion's Fang, has no reported drop in the open areas, while the two-handed blunt Golden Mole can be crafted, players say. So many Pages level with a sword to 50, then switch to Golden Mole and re-spend their skills.

Some go blunt from the start: all three blunts can be crafted, and 60% scrolls cost about half of two-handed sword ones. So few people want blunts that the Page is called "the class that can still afford gear from its own grinding". The downside is speed: blunts are slow, so monsters reach you between swings. Two-handed weapons do reach further than one-handed ones. One-handed weapons with a shield are weak right now because there is no attack scroll for shields.

Stats and gear

DEX only to your next weapon's requirement, everything else into STR; Titan needs DEX 40 including gear. See the Warrior equipment list.

  • Scroll gloves for attack, not critical.
  • Potions: Meat and Lemons.
  • Switching from sword to blunt at 50 loses Mastery's Accuracy 20. Make it up with accuracy potions or helmet accuracy scrolls beforehand.

Final Attack3025% chance to charge, use within 60 seconds. One monster at 360%, MP 16

How you use Threaten is everything. Unlike the old version it does not lower monster attack. It lowers their weapon defense, so your party's physical hits land harder too. Its real use: threatened monsters walk slowly toward you. Melee warriors usually get hit walking up to monsters; with Threaten they come to you, slowly. Players report taking noticeably fewer hits with just 3 points in it.

  • Monsters on one side: Rush to gather, Threaten, then Slash Blast.
  • Monsters on both sides: Threaten first to pull them in, jump away, then Rush and Slash Blast.
  • It does not reach other platforms. On slopes, jump to match the height before casting.
  • You have to recast it on every new group, which some find tedious.

Final Attack hits one monster, so before 50, when Slash Blast does the work, it matters little. It earns its keep at the drakes, where single hits need to be strong.

Skill build

  1. Level 30: Rush 1
  2. By about 35: Mastery 5, then Booster 1 and Threaten 1
  3. By about 38: Mastery 20
  4. Then, path A (guide sites): fill Final Attack.
  5. Then, path B (blunt Pages): max Rush first (around 45), then Threaten, then Final Attack. In the time a slow blunt swings one Slash Blast, Rush goes off almost twice, cleaning up what you missed.

Do not drop first-job Slash Blast. Final Attack hits one monster, so Slash Blast is your only way to clear what Rush and Threaten gathered. If you grind somewhere that hurts, like Curse Eye, raising Threaten first cuts the hits you take.

In short

The Page lives on Threaten. Learn to pull monsters in and slow them, and you take fewer hits than any other melee warrior. Sword to 50, then Golden Mole for bigger single hits is the safest road right now. Players say third job may open on the test server early next year.
