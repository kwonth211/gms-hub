The smoothest Page road is to level with a two-handed sword until 50, then switch to the two-handed blunt Golden Mole. Spend second-job SP in this order: Rush 1, Mastery 5, Booster 1, Threaten 1, Mastery 20 (by level 38), then Final Attack 30 (by level 48).

Both builds start the same way: Rush 1 at 30, then Mastery 5, Booster 1 and Threaten 1 by about 35, and Mastery 20 by 38. After that they split.

  1. Final Attack first (the most used order): Final Attack 30 (level 48), Threaten 30 (57), Booster 20 (64), Rush 20 (70). It suits the drakes, where single hits need to be strong, and the level 50 blunt.
  2. Rush first (for blunt Pages): Rush 20 (level 45), Threaten 30 (54), Final Attack 30 (64), Booster 20 (70). In the time a slow blunt swings one Slash Blast, Rush goes off almost twice, so stragglers get cleaned up fast.

If you grind somewhere that hurts, like Curse Eyes, raise Threaten first. Even 3 points cut the hits you take noticeably.

How to come out of first job

A Page goes Power Strike 20, Improved HP Recovery 15, Slash Blast 11, Precise Strikes 11, Max HP Increase 3, Iron Body 1 in first job. The Page's Final Attack hits one monster, so Slash Blast is your only way to clear what Rush and Threaten gathered. Do not drop Slash Blast.

Blunt Weapon Mastery adds no accuracy, which is why Precise Strikes goes to 11 as well. The full first-job table is in the warrior build guide.

A blunt has 2 to 5 more attack than the sword of the same level, but it swings slowly. Monsters reach you between swings, so you take more contact damage. Once you have a Golden Mole, most Pages keep it all the way to 70.

Some go blunt from the start. All three blunts can be crafted, and a 60% two-handed blunt scroll costs {{price:2044201}}, far less than the two-handed sword one at {{price:2044001}}. So few people want blunt weapons that a Page can still buy gear with what it earns grinding.

One-handed weapons with a shield are weak right now because there is no attack scroll for shields. One-handed blunts do swing fast, though, so some Pages who live in the Curse Eye cave use one.

Stats and gear

Raise DEX only to your next weapon's requirement and put the rest into STR. Titan and Zard at level 40 need DEX 40; Golden Mole at 50 needs DEX 50. DEX from gear counts toward it.

  • Gloves: scroll for attack, not critical. With a warrior's low crit chance, attack is what gets you to one-hit kills sooner.
  • Potions: Meat ({{price:2010003}}) and Lemons ({{price:2010005}}) heal the most per meso.
  • Accuracy: switching from sword to blunt at 50 loses Mastery's 20 accuracy. Make it up beforehand with 60% helmet accuracy scrolls ({{price:2040001}}, +2 each) or accuracy potions.
  • Gear list: see the warrior equipment list for what you can wear at each level.

How to use Threaten

How you use Threaten is everything. Unlike the old version, it does not lower monster attack. It lowers their weapon defense instead, so your party's physical hits land harder too.

Its real use is something else. Threatened monsters walk slowly toward you. Melee warriors usually get hit while walking up to monsters; with Threaten they come to you, slowly, and you hit first.

  • Monsters on one side: Rush to gather, Threaten, then Slash Blast.
  • Monsters on both sides: Threaten first to pull them in, jump away, then Rush and Slash Blast.
  • Range: wide horizontally, short vertically. It does not reach other platforms, so on slopes jump to match the height before casting.
  • Recasting: fresh spawns need a new cast. At max level it lasts 30 seconds.

Final Attack hits one monster, so before 50, when Slash Blast does the work, it matters little. It earns its keep at the drakes, where single hits need to be strong. Since the November 24 patch, Rush pushes monsters the instant it hits and keeps them from snapping back for 1.5 seconds.

Rush took the slot where Power Guard used to be. Power Guard moved to third job.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Skipping Threaten: it is how a melee warrior takes fewer hits. Take 1 point before 35, and 3 or more on maps that hurt.
  • Dropping Slash Blast: a Page's Final Attack hits one monster. You need Slash Blast to clear what you gathered.
  • Buying Lion's Fang at 50: the same money buys a Golden Mole plus scrolls, which hits much harder.
  • Switching to blunt without fixing accuracy: you lose Sword Mastery's 20 accuracy and suddenly miss. Scroll your helmet for accuracy before the switch.

Frequently asked questions

Is Blunt Weapon Mastery's critical damage worth anything?

Very little, because it only applies when you crit. Warrior crit chance comes from first-job Precise Strikes and tops out at 5%. That is why a sword, with its 20 accuracy, is the easier weapon until 50.

Should a Page use a blunt weapon or a sword?

A two-handed sword until 50, then Golden Mole. Lion's Fang costs {{price:1402008}}, while Golden Mole sells for {{price:1422007}} and can be crafted. Going blunt from the start saves the SP reset.

How far should I level Threaten?

Take 1 point before 35 and fill it to 30 after Final Attack. If you grind on maps that hurt, put 3 or more in early. At max it slows 4 monsters for 30 seconds.

Is the Page good for solo play?

Yes. Threaten lets it take fewer hits than other melee warriors, which makes soloing comfortable. A level 44 Page solo kept over 50,000 mesos an hour in the Evil Eye caves. For the fewest potions, though, the healing Spearman wins.

Can I use a shield?

Stick to two-handed weapons for now. There is no attack scroll for shields, so a one-handed weapon and shield deal much less damage. A few Pages use a fast one-handed blunt in the Curse Eye cave only.

Why are there so few Pages?

Early on, Threaten had a reputation for being useless, and without Rage a Page has a harder time getting into parties. On November 14, Pages were 13 of the top 1,000 characters by level. The upside is cheap gear: few people want blunt weapons or their scrolls.

When does third job open?

It is not open yet, and no date has been announced. Level to 70 with Golden Mole in the meantime.
