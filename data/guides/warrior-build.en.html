Warrior costs depend on landing hits, grouping targets and limiting contact damage.

Stats and equipment

Build around STR while checking equipment requirements, DEX from gear and actual accuracy. Repeated misses can erase an attack upgrade. Look at weapon speed, reach, price and the Mastery/Booster family together.

First-job positioning

Use compact platforms, and choose single-target or multi-target attacks by how many monsters you actually hit. Chasing scattered enemies while taking damage can cost more than a weaker, compact map. Stay on local mushrooms and tree-dungeon targets before an early Ant Tunnel move.

Second-job choices

Each second job has its own detailed guide with weapon choice, skill build and training spots by level: Fighter · Page · Spearman.

  • Fighter: Rage plus your chosen sword or axe, best in a party.
  • Page: control, shields and the sword or blunt-weapon requirements.
  • Spearman: match spear/polearm skills with grouping and recovery.

Develop the weapon family you use before spreading points. Check current skill prerequisites. Rush can improve grouping while also increasing contact exposure.

Route transitions

Keep KPQ or mushrooms as your home base in the thirties, and make short visits to Evil Eye. Go to Burnt Land once Fire Boars die in few hits, and to the compact Curse Eye/Monkey Forest routes later. High-level Kargo footage still includes repeated contact damage; fast kills do not establish net profit.

Supply checklist

  • Resolve repeated misses.
  • Reduce overhealing with oversized potions.
  • Limit long pursuits under ranged fire.
  • Check whether buffs actually reduce casts.
  • Keep rare drops separate from recurring supply income.
