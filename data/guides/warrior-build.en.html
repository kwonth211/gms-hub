Put every point into STR until level 10, then raise DEX only to your next weapon's requirement and put the rest into STR. Max Power Strike first (around level 17), then pick Fighter, Page or Spearman at level 30.

Most warriors stall in two places. In the early 30s monsters stop dying in one hit, and in the late 40s attacks start missing for lack of accuracy. The sections below give the numbers that get you past both.

How much STR and DEX

DEX is only for equipping weapons. STR raises your damage, and accuracy comes from the first-job skill Precise Strikes and from gear. DEX on your gear counts toward the requirement, so with 4 DEX on gear you put 4 fewer points into it.

Two-handed weapons ask for DEX equal to their level. One-handed weapons ask for 5 less, which leaves 5 more points for STR. Unless you want a shield, go two-handed: more attack at the same level, and the longer reach makes basic attacks land further out.

With DEX matched to the requirement, a level 40 warrior with DEX 40 has 62 accuracy from stats. Precise Strikes at 15 adds 20 more for 82, and a sword user with Sword Mastery 20 gets another 20 on top. That is why sword Fighters and Pages worry least about missing.

You get 61 first-job SP by level 30. The first 20 all go into Power Strike. Where the other 41 go depends on the second job you are heading for.

  1. Heading for Spearman: Power Strike 20, Improved HP Recovery 15, Slash Blast 11, Precise Strikes 15. Spears and polearms give no accuracy and their Final Attack hits one target, so you need both the group attack and the accuracy.
  2. Heading for Page: Power Strike 20, Improved HP Recovery 15, Slash Blast 11, Precise Strikes 11, Max HP Increase 3, Iron Body 1. Blunt Weapon Mastery adds no accuracy and the Page's Final Attack hits one target.
  3. Heading for Fighter: Power Strike 20, Improved HP Recovery 15, Max HP Increase 3, Iron Body 20, Precise Strikes 3. A Fighter's Final Attack hits three monsters, so it clears groups without Slash Blast, and Iron Body makes every hit you take softer.

Another option is to stop Improved HP Recovery at 3 and push Slash Blast to 19 with the saved points. Your group attack then hits that much harder.

All three get Rush in second job. Rush pushes up to 6 monsters ahead of you, and you can't be knocked back while rushing. Since the November 24 patch it pushes monsters the instant you hit, and for 1.5 seconds they no longer snap back to where they were.

On November 14, 161 of the top 1,000 characters by level (levels 75 to 92) were warriors. Spearmen were 106 of them, Fighters 42 and Pages 13, so the Spearman is the warrior that most often makes it to high levels.

Mastery, Booster and Final Attack are separate for each weapon family, so pick your weapon right after the advancement. Switching later takes an SP Reset scroll, which sells for {{price:5051001}} right now.

  • Train where things die in one hit. Melee classes take contact damage between swings. When two hits become three, you get hit more and HP potions vanish fast.
  • Fight on one platform. Chasing monsters along a long map means eating ranged attacks the whole way. Rush them together and clear them with one group attack.
  • Use small potions for small damage. Drinking a big potion after a scratch wastes most of the heal.
  • Drink Warrior Potions only when they cut a hit. If two hits stay two hits, the potion is money thrown away.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Adding DEX for accuracy: stop DEX at the weapon requirement and get accuracy from Precise Strikes and helmet scrolls. Spare AP goes to STR, which gets you to one-hit kills sooner.
  • Moving maps by level alone: move when the next map's monsters die in two hits or fewer. One hit is the signal for Evil Eyes; two hits for Curse Eyes and Drakes.
  • Switching weapons after second job: put points into sword skills and pick up an axe, and Mastery, Booster and Final Attack all stop working. Choose the weapon at the advancement.
  • Paying for a level 35 weapon: it is only about 5 attack below level 40, so spend that money on a level 40 weapon and potions.

Frequently asked questions

How much DEX does a warrior need?

Only your next weapon's DEX requirement. That is DEX 40 for a level 40 two-handed weapon, DEX 50 for a level 50 two-handed weapon and DEX 45 for a level 50 one-handed weapon. DEX from gear counts, so subtract it, and put everything else into STR.

Do I have to take Slash Blast?

No, not if you are going Fighter. The Fighter's Final Attack hits three monsters, and the points go into Iron Body 20 so hits hurt less. Pages and Spearmen have a single-target Final Attack, so they take Slash Blast to at least 11.

I'm a level 35 warrior. Where do I go besides KPQ?

Go to The Cave of Evil Eye. Around level 37 Power Strike one-shots Evil Eyes, and from then on it beats KPQ. If they still take two hits, stay on Fire Boars in The Burnt Land or in a KPQ Ligator party.

Which second job is best?

Spearman if you want to solo at a profit: its Final Attack heals you, so it drinks the fewest potions. Fighter if you mostly party, thanks to Rage. Page if you want cheap weapons and fewer hits taken, thanks to Threaten.

Why do warriors burn so many potions?

Because you take contact damage while standing next to monsters. Drop to a map where things die in one or two hits and the hits you take fall sharply. Buy only potions that heal the most per meso, like Meat and Lemons.

My attacks keep missing. What do I do?

Raise Precise Strikes first: at 15 it adds 20 accuracy. If that is not enough, use helmet accuracy scrolls and accuracy potions, and stay on low-accuracy monsters like Evil Eyes and Curse Eyes until you catch up.
