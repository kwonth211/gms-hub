Building a warrior the old-school way costs you. Power Guard and Hyper Body are gone from second job, Max HP Increase no longer punishes you for taking it late, and Final Attack is now a skill you press yourself. This guide covers SP order, how much DEX to add and weapons and maps by level for Fighters, Pages and Spearmen. For how the three differ and how to choose, read the second job guide first.

How first job skills changed

Max HP Increase is a percentage. In the old game, taking it late meant losing the bonus from earlier levels. Now it raises max HP by 10% at level 1 and 25% at master, so there's no rush: put points into attack or survival, whichever you need right now.

Power Strike and Slash Blast are stronger at level 1. Power Strike is still 260% at master, but level 1 went from 114% to 160%. Slash Blast is 70% at level 1 and 130% at master, hitting up to six monsters. That's why early hunting is easier than it used to be.

Accuracy comes from the precision passive. Maxed, it gives +20 accuracy and +5% crit chance. If you miss often, take this before more attack skill levels. Iron Body raises defence by up to 25%, and HP recovery boosts potion healing by up to 20% and keeps regenerating while you move.

One attack-first example: finish Power Strike, Slash Blast and the precision passive, then put 3 each into HP recovery and Max HP Increase. If potions cost too much or you keep dying, move recovery and defence earlier.

Second job SP order (levels 30-40)

  1. First SP: 1 in Rush. Even at level 1 it pushes up to six monsters. More levels add distance and cut MP from 35 to 15 but don't push more monsters, so 1 is enough for now. Use it to herd monsters and catch them all with one Slash Blast. Cooldown is 2 seconds, and since the October 15 patch it can't push bosses.
  2. Weapon mastery 5, then Booster 1. Booster unlocks at mastery 5. It raises attack speed two steps from level 1 and lasts 100 seconds (300 at master); more levels only make it last longer and cost less MP.
  3. At 33, 1 each in Rage and Final Attack. Everything else goes into mastery.
  4. Finish mastery by 38. Mastery raises minimum damage, so more monsters die in one hit. Mastery and Booster are per weapon type; only invest in the one you use.
  5. After that, it depends on your job. Fighters raise Rage, from +10 attack at level 1 to +40 at master, shared with the party, at the cost of the same amount of weapon defence for everyone. Pages and Spearmen raise Final Attack first. Rush comes last, since more levels don't push more monsters.

Final Attack isn't automatic. Using an attack skill has a 25% chance to ready it, and you press it yourself within 60 seconds, so put it on a hotkey. Fighters hit up to three monsters in front (140% at level 1, 200% at master), Pages one monster for up to 360%, and Spearmen one monster for up to 350% while healing 10% of the damage dealt (up to 25% of max HP per hit).

Spearman order to level 70

Rush 1 → mastery 5 → Booster 1 → mastery 20 → Final Attack 30 → Booster 20 → Rush 20 → Iron Will 30 fills everything by 70 with one point to spare. Iron Will is a self-buff with +50 weapon defence and 35% knockback immunity. Invest only in the spear skills if you use a spear, or the polearm skills for a polearm. Spear Mastery has up to a 20% chance to ignore enemy defence; Polearm Mastery adds 20 speed.

DEX: only as much as your next weapon needs

The rule is simple: take the DEX requirement of the next weapon you'll wear, minus the DEX your gear gives, as your base DEX target, and put every other AP into STR. If the requirement is 70 and your gear gives 10 DEX, base DEX 60 is enough.

Note that accuracy on gear doesn't replace DEX. +10 accuracy raises your hit rate but doesn't count toward a DEX requirement.

Weapons by level

Early on, look at shops and crafting rather than waiting for drops. Level 10 weapons are sold in the Perion weapon shop, and the blacksmith crafts helmets such as the Metal Koif (1 Bronze Koif + 2 Iron Ingots + 1,000 mesos) and the Juno (2 Iron Ingots + 2 Processed Leather + 6 Screws + 1,200 mesos). The level 20 Iron Viking Helm adds DEX and accuracy; the level 30 Steel Missel adds STR, accuracy and crit chance.

Many players switch to a Lionheart at 35, then to a level 40 weapon at 40, often a crafted one. The level 40 weapons from weapon-crafting stage 8:

Lower speed values are faster. Spears and polearms swing 60% of the time and stab 40%, and the swing, which favours polearms, comes up more often.

Fighters and Pages using one-handed weapons can also take a shield. Block chance comes from the shield's weapon defence; a Red Cross Shield (110 defence) gives about 18%. Compare a two-handed weapon's attack with a shield's survivability, potion costs included.

Where to hunt

In your early 30s, keep running KPQ. It has no level cap in Classic World, and gator runs (killing only the stage 1 Ligators) give good EXP. Around 37, a Fighter can one-shot Evil Eyes with Power Strike, so soloing works. Once you two-shot Curse Eyes around 40, join Curse Eye parties: Evil Eyes solo, Curse Eyes in a party.

After that, players typically go to Fire Boars in the Burnt Land, subway Line 1 Area 4, then Dark Stone Golems. A level 50 Fighter (240 STR, 60 DEX) with a specific weapon and buffs works out to one-shot Fire Boars (842 HP) even on minimum Power Strike damage, but that's one gear setup, not a class ranking. Put your own stats into the hits-to-kill calculator to see where you stand. All maps from 30 to 70 are in the level 30-70 training guide.

Third job isn't out yet

Only first and second job are available. Crusader, White Knight and Dragon Knight were tested, but the official roadmap puts third job in 2027 or later. Until then, finish your second job skills in the order above and gear up.
