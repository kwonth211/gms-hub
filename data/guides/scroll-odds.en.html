The calculator assumes a fixed success probability and independent attempts. Mathematical output does not validate every current game mechanic. Compare preset sources with the actual item tooltip.

Five attempts are not five slots

Exactly five independent attempts at 60% success have three expected successes and a 0.4⁵ = 1.024% chance of none. At 10%, the chance of none is 0.9⁵ = 59.049%. An expectation does not guarantee an individual result.

Failure consuming an upgrade slot has not been verified here. Do not interpret these as final outcomes for five-slot equipment. The former “nothing happens on failure” description is withdrawn.

Conditional destruction

Assuming 10% success and 50% destruction conditional on failure, destruction per attempt is 45%. Trying up to five times, stopping on destruction, gives 1 − 0.55⁵ ≈ 94.97% chance of destruction. Check whether the actual scroll uses these inputs.
