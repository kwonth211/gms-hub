Classic World scrolls come in four tiers: Lesser 100%, Intermediate 60%, Greater 10% and Chaos 10%. Only a failed Chaos scroll can destroy the item (half of its failures do); the other three just use up one upgrade slot when they fail.

The success rate is the same on every attempt, no matter how many failures came before. The numbers below are the same values the scroll calculator uses.

How often Chaos scrolls destroy items

Every Chaos scroll is a 10% success, a 45% "nothing happens" and a 45% destroyed item. A destroyed item ends the run, so the chance that a 5-slot item survives five Chaos scrolls is 5.03%. Keep Chaos away from gear you care about and use it only on cheap items you can afford to lose.

How to use the scroll calculator

  1. Open the scroll calculator and pick your scroll. The tier and the stat gain fill in by themselves; choose custom input for anything not listed.
  2. Set the number of scrolls to the upgrade slots left on your item.
  3. Enter the price of one scroll in mesos and press calculate.
  4. You get the average gain, the chance that every scroll passes, the destruction chance, the average number of scrolls used, the average cost and a chart of every success count.
  5. The tier table below it keeps your slot count and swaps only the tier: average gain, chance of ending below all-Lesser and average cost per point.

To include the item's price and what each result sells for, move to the scroll profit calculator. To put an item on the workbench and drag scrolls onto it yourself, use the scroll simulator.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Buying on the average alone: five 60% scrolls average +6, but +8 or better happens only 33.70% of the time. Decide on the "or more" chance.
  • Believing a streak of failures is due to end: a Greater scroll is still 10% after nine misses. Recalculate from the slots you have left.
  • Using Intermediate for HP: Lesser has the higher HP and MP average. Fill those slots with Lesser.
  • Putting Chaos on gear you value: each scroll destroys the item 45% of the time. Keep Chaos for gear you can lose.
  • Accepting a 10% in a trade you thought was a 60%: both have the same icon. Hover over the scroll in the trade window right before you press confirm. The usual tricks are listed on Scam reports.
  • Scrolling without knowing what each result sells for: a failed item with 0 slots left rarely finds a buyer. Check what every result leaves you in the scroll profit calculator first.

How the calculator does the math

The calculator assumes every scroll is independent and the success rate never changes. Under that model, the chance of exactly k passes from n scrolls at success rate p is C(n, k) × pᵏ × (1 − p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ. Average passes are n × p, and the average gain is n × p × the gain per success.

For Chaos it uses a destruction chance of (1 − success rate) × 50% on every scroll. When the item is destroyed, the scrolls you had left count as unused, which is why the average number used comes out below the number you planned.

  • Exactly five independent attempts at 60% have three expected successes and a 0.4⁵ = 1.024% chance of none.
  • At 10%, the chance of none is 0.9⁵ = 59.049%.
  • At 10% success with 50% destruction on failure, destruction per attempt is 45%. Trying up to five times, stopping on destruction, gives a 1 − 0.55⁵ ≈ 94.97% chance of destruction.

Frequently asked questions

How much ATT do five 60% glove scrolls give?

+6 on average. About seven times in ten you get +6 or more (68.26%), and a perfect +10 happens 7.78% of the time. Five Lesser scrolls give a guaranteed +5.

I have +4 ATT Work Gloves with 1 slot left. Use a 60% or sell?

Go with whichever result leaves you more mesos once you enter the selling prices. A 60% scroll gives +6 at 60% and +4 with no slots at 40%. If you can find a Lesser 100% scroll, +5 is guaranteed.

Can a failed scroll destroy my item?

Only a Chaos scroll can. Lesser, Intermediate and Greater scrolls just use up one upgrade slot when they fail, and the item stays as it was. A failed Chaos scroll destroys the item half the time, which is 45% per scroll.

I failed several 10% scrolls in a row. Is the next one more likely to pass?

No, every scroll is 10%. A Greater scroll passes once in ten tries on average, yet ten of them still fail completely 34.9% of the time.

Is gear scrolled only with 100% scrolls good enough?

Yes, every slot adds +1 with no risk, so a 7-slot weapon ends at +7 ATT. That is 1.4 below the all-60% average (+8.4), but all-60% ends below +7 three times in ten.

Which glove ATT scroll do Rotten Mushrooms drop?

The Lesser (100%) one. Hunt the Rotten Mushrooms in Forgotten Hollow's Dilapidated Tomb and Another Sanctuary. The maps are in the Forgotten Hollow guide.

Which scroll should I use for HP?

Lesser 100%. It adds a sure +10 per scroll, so 7 slots give HP +70. Intermediate averages +9 per scroll, +63 over 7 slots, and ends below all-Lesser 58.01% of the time.

Are the calculator's odds the same as in the game?

Yes, the success rates, stat gains and Chaos destruction chance come straight from the game client's scroll data. Every attempt is random, so the same item comes out differently each time.
