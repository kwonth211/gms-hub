When a quest hands you a scroll or you're about to scroll gloves for attack, the question is "if I use five Intermediate 60% scrolls, what do I usually end up with?". Classic World's scrolls differ in names and numbers from the old 10%/60%/100% system, so scrolling by feel easily costs you. The odds in this guide were calculated by GMS Hub.

The four scroll tiers

TierSuccessOn failureGlove attack gain
Lesser100%-+1
Intermediate60%Nothing happens+2
Greater10%Nothing happens+3
Chaos10%50% chance the item is destroyed+4

Glove attack, main stats, accuracy, speed, jump, avoidability and crit rise +1/+2/+3/+4 as above. Defence and glove magic are doubled (+2/+4/+6/+8), and HP/MP on hats, tops and bottoms are +10/+15/+20/+25.

Weapon attack and wand/staff magic rise more per tier. The scrolls' own data in the game client gives weapon attack +1/+2/+4/+5 and wand/staff magic +2/+4/+8/+10; sources used to disagree on these.

Also, whether a failed scroll uses up an upgrade slot isn't recorded in the game data, so it's unconfirmed. The numbers below assume, as in the old game, that a failure still uses a slot. If it doesn't, your results are better than shown.

The average is +6, one more than five Lesser scrolls (a guaranteed +5), but about one time in three you end at +4 or less. That's a 31.7% chance of doing worse than Lesser.

The average is +1.5, less than a third of five Lesser scrolls (+5). There's a 59% chance that all five fail. Treat Greater scrolls as a lottery ticket, nice if one hits, not as the backbone of your upgrades.

Chaos scrolls almost always break things

Chaos gives +4 at 10%, but half of the 90% that fail destroy the item. One scroll alone has a 45% chance to destroy it. Five in a row destroy the item 95% of the time. Use Chaos only on gear you can afford to lose, or on the last slot after you've already reached the numbers you wanted.

Compare by cost per point of attack

When deciding whether to buy, compare "how much does one point of attack cost on average". The average gain per scroll is:

  • Lesser: 100% × 1 = 1 per scroll
  • Intermediate: 60% × 2 = 1.2 per scroll
  • Greater: 10% × 3 = 0.3 per scroll

So the cost per attack point is the price for Lesser, the price ÷ 1.2 for Intermediate and the price ÷ 0.3 for Greater. If an Intermediate costs 600k, one attack point costs 500k on average; a Greater at 300k costs 1m per point. At citizenship rank 9 the town shop sells glove attack Lesser scrolls for 40k, which is 40k per point and far cheaper than anything in the market. Requirements are in the citizenship and housing guide, and current scroll prices are on the live market.

Upgrade slots are a cost too. Lesser is certain but adds only +1 per slot; Intermediate averages +1.2 per slot. For good gear with few slots, Intermediate's higher expected value per slot fits; when you need a guaranteed result regardless of slots, Lesser does.

Summary

  • Lesser if you want certainty, Intermediate if you're playing the average; Intermediate ends below Lesser about a third of the time.
  • Five Greater 10% scrolls fail completely 59% of the time. Don't rely on them.
  • Five Chaos scrolls destroy the item 95% of the time.
  • Decide whether to buy by cost per attack point.
