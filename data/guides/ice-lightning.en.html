Right after the second job advancement an Ice/Lightning Wizard has to pick one attack to level first. Today most players max Cold Beam first and leave Thunder Bolt for later. The skill numbers below are checked against the game client data and the October 29 patch; the hunting records come from players who levelled the class.

Thunder Bolt costs nearly twice the MP and does less than half the damage per monster. It only pays off when you hit three or more monsters every cast, and on crowded maps they rarely bunch up like that. After the patch many players said monsters that took three casts still took three, and one Wizard who reset into Thunder Bolt before the patch lost more than 10,000 mesos to potions in 20 minutes on the beach.

Cold Beam became the standard because of Fire Boar: a level 32 monster that is weak to ice, so Cold Beam hits it harder. Cold Beam also passes through walls and platforms, so you can hit monsters on the far side of a ledge. It no longer freezes monsters in place, though, and ice-resistant monsters like Ligator and Cold Eye take less damage and barely slow down.

Stats and gear

Put your points into INT and add only as much LUK as your next wand needs. Everything else in INT raises both magic attack and MP.

Players report that spells take the same time to cast whatever the weapon speed, so pick the wand with the highest magic attack. For a shield, Pan Lid is said to block best of anything a Magician can hold; see the Magician equipment list for what you can wear at each level. Glove magic attack scrolls tend to be cheaper than attack scrolls because few classes want them. Cromi is expensive enough that players joke it takes forever to save for at 30,000 mesos an hour.

Many players turn Magic Guard off while grinding, because damage taken out of MP means drinking that much more MP potion. For MP, food like Lemons and Oranges is a common choice over Blue Potions.

Level 1 Teleport costs 55 MP, which hurts early. Maxed, it lets you hold your spot on a crowded flat map, according to players who maxed it right after Cold Beam. Fire Boar has 0 MP, so MP Eater gets nothing there.

Skill build: same start, two paths

You get 1 point at the advancement and 3 per level after, so 121 points by level 70. That is enough to max all five skills and put 1 point in Slow, so every order ends in the same place at 70.

  1. Level 30: Teleport 1.
  2. Levels 31–40: Cold Beam only, maxed at 40. While it is low your first-job attack is stronger, so switch to Cold Beam at about 35 for Fire Boar and about 38 for other monsters.
  3. From 41, path A: fill Thunder Bolt, then Meditation. This is what most guide sites suggest.
  4. From 41, path B: MP Eater 3, then Meditation and Teleport, Thunder Bolt last. Most players who levelled the class went this way.

If you plan to use an SP reset scroll, some players start with Meditation, keep using first-job attacks and move to Cold Beam around 37. The scroll is a cash item, so without spending money it is simpler to go Cold Beam from the start.

One level 39–41 Wizard logged an hour on each map: the best was the middle platform of Ant Tunnel in a party of 4–6, at about 92,000 EXP. Soloing Fire Boar gave about 80,000 EXP but cost around 37,000 mesos in MP potions, and another player made 80,000 EXP an hour while losing mesos. The lesson: a lower map where monsters die in one or two casts is often faster than a map at your level.

If you took Thunder Bolt early, look for lightning-weak monsters: Iron Hog (28), Lorang (38) and Clang (49). Its vertical reach is short and rarely hits the platform above, so use it where monsters line up.
