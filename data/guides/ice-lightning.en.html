Skill order by level

You get 1 SP at the advancement and 3 per level after, so 121 SP by level 70. That is enough to max all five main skills and put 1 point in Slow, so every order ends in the same place at 70. What changes is what you have between 40 and 70.

  1. Level 30: Teleport 1.
  2. Levels 31–40: Cold Beam only, maxed at 40. While it's low your first-job attack is stronger, so switch to Cold Beam at about 35 for Fire Boar and about 38 for everything else.
  3. Level 41: MP Eater 3, which unlocks Meditation.
  4. Levels 42–48: Meditation 20: +25 magic attack for 5 minutes.
  5. Levels 49–54: Teleport 20. The cost drops from 55 MP to 15, enough to hold your ground on a crowded flat map.
  6. Levels 55–60: MP Eater 20.
  7. Levels 61–70: Thunder Bolt 30, and the last point into Slow.

If you party and gather monsters, or grind a map where monsters line up like Hot Sand, fill Thunder Bolt first from level 41. That order maxes Thunder Bolt at 50, Meditation at 58 and Teleport at 70. If you plan to buy an SP Reset Scroll ({{price:5051001}} on the Free Market), you can also start with Meditation, keep using first-job attacks, and move the points to Cold Beam around 37.

Cold Beam freezes the monster it hits for 3 seconds, slowing everything it does. Freeze a monster walking toward you, step back while it's slowed, and cast again. Cold Beam also passes through walls and platforms, so you can hit monsters on the far side of a ledge. Ice-resistant monsters take half damage and don't freeze.

Thunder Bolt costs nearly twice the MP and does less than half Cold Beam's damage to each monster. It only pays off when you hit three or more monsters with every cast. Its vertical reach is short and rarely touches the platform above, so use it where monsters line up on one floor.

Monsters an Ice/Lightning Wizard should skip

Ice-resistant monsters take half damage from Cold Beam and never freeze. These maps are not for you.

  • Ligator (31), Cold Eye (40), Croco (52): fire-weak, so they belong to Fire/Poison.
  • Drake (50): resists both fire and ice.
  • Raffle (40), Rafflesia (47), Aqumander (43): the ice-resistant monsters of Forgotten Hollow. Aqumander is weak to lightning, so Thunder Bolt still works on it.

If you took Thunder Bolt early, hunt lightning-weak monsters: Iron Hog (28), Lorang (38), Clang (49) and Echopus (45). In Forgotten Hollow the only ice-weak monster is Duskmander (50, 2,682 HP).

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Taking Thunder Bolt first and then grinding solo: twice the MP for half the damage per monster. Solo, go from Cold Beam straight to Meditation.
  • Going to Cold Eye or Ligator maps: they resist ice, so Cold Beam does half damage and never freezes them. At the same level, hunt Fire Boar or Copper Drake.
  • Getting around on level 1 Teleport: every jump costs 55 MP. Walk on flat ground and teleport only to cross platforms or dodge.
  • Counting on MP Eater at Fire Boars: Fire Boar has 0 MP. Budget for MP recovery in The Burnt Land.
  • Grinding with Magic Guard on: contact damage drains your MP and the potion bill grows. Turn it on only when things get dangerous.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cold Beam freeze monsters?

Yes, it freezes whatever it hits for 3 seconds and slows its actions. Ice-resistant monsters (Ligator, Cold Eye, Croco, Drake) don't freeze and take half damage. Freezing, stepping back and casting again is the core of Ice/Lightning grinding.

When should I take Thunder Bolt?

Last, at 61–70, if you grind solo. Take it right after Cold Beam only for party grinding where you hit three or more monsters every cast, or for maps like Hot Sand.

Is there a spot that gives over 200,000 EXP an hour?

Yes. A level 65 Wizard made 274,000 EXP an hour solo with Thunder Bolt in Hot Sand on Florina Beach. The second floor of Golem's Temple II gives 200,000–220,000 an hour in a party. Both are for 60 and up; before that, level in Dangerous Valley.

Is The Burnt Land V a good map?

Yes, for levels 35–45. It has the most Fire Boars of any map, 26, and they die in two Cold Beams from level 36. Fire Boar has 0 MP so MP Eater won't help; bring plenty of MP recovery.

When do I max Teleport?

Right after Meditation, at 49–54. Level 1 costs 55 MP, so walk early on and teleport only when you must. Maxed, it costs 15 MP and lets you hold a spot on crowded maps.

When should I buy a Cromi?

At 50, once you have 40 LUK. Cromi gives 83 magic attack, 14 more than the Fairy Wand's 69, but it costs {{price:1372007}} against {{price:1372006}} for a Fairy Wand. Until then, scroll your Fairy Wand for magic attack and lean on glove scrolls.

Ice/Lightning or Fire/Poison?

Ice/Lightning ignores terrain and freezes what it hits; Fire/Poison hits one monster harder. Ice/Lightning hunts Fire Boar, Copper Drake and Red Drake while Fire/Poison hunts Jr. Wraith, Cold Eye and Croco, so the two barely share maps. The fire side is covered in the Fire/Poison guide.
