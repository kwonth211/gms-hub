Fighter is the second-job warrior people most often tell you not to pick. One in three ranked second-job warriors is a Fighter, yet far fewer of them reach the top levels than Spearmen. Still, go axe and you never worry about weapons, and Rage makes you welcome in any party. Skill numbers below come from the game data; the hunting records are reports from players who levelled the class.

Axe or sword?

Mastery, Booster and Final Attack come in separate sword and axe versions, so switching weapons later means re-spending points. Decide right after the advancement.

At the same weapon attack, axes hit a little harder than swords (two-handed axe over two-handed sword, one-handed axe over one-handed sword). The real difference is whether you can get the weapon at all.

Players report that all three two-handed axes (35, 40, 50) can be made with weapon crafting. The ores drop on the way, from Zombie Mushroom, Fire Boar and Cold Eye. Two-handed swords are hard to find, so many sword Fighters are weighing an SP reset to switch to axe at 50. Right now the easier road is the two-handed axe.

If you want a shield, take a one-handed axe: bleeding is calculated from STR and attack, not the weapon multiplier, so it barely drops when you go one-handed.

Stats and gear

Put only as much DEX as your next weapon needs and everything else into STR. Two-handed weapons need DEX equal to their level (Sabretooth at 40 needs DEX 40), counting DEX from your gear. See the Warrior equipment list for what you can wear at each level.

  • Scroll gloves for attack, not critical.
  • Buy potions that heal the most per meso: Meat and Lemons.

Second job skills at a glance

SkillMaxWhat it does (at max)
Rush20Dash forward pushing up to 6 monsters together. MP 35 at level 1, 15 at max
Sword/Axe Mastery20Minimum damage plus Accuracy 20 or 25% bleed. Level 3 opens Final Attack, level 5 opens Booster
Sword/Axe Booster20Attack speed +2 steps from level 1. 300 seconds at max
Rage30Every party member nearby gets attack +40 and weapon defense -40 for 300 seconds
Final Attack3025% chance to charge while attacking, use it within 60 seconds. Up to 3 monsters at 200%, costs HP 20 and MP 18

Rage is the Fighter. Attack +40 raises both minimum and maximum damage about as much as 80 more DEX would, and it grows with your weapon. The catch is -40 defense, so hits hurt more. It applies to the whole party, which is why some tanky party members turn the buff down.

Final Attack is rated lowest in the community: it does not heal like the Spearman's, and Slash Blast already hits six. Press it only when two or three monsters survive a Slash Blast; save it when one is left or everything already dies in one hit. When potions are tight, skipping it entirely is fine.

Skill build

There is no single right answer; patches and new maps can change it. The most common order:

  1. Level 30: Rush 1
  2. From 31: Rage 1, Mastery to 5, then Booster 1 and Final Attack 1
  3. By about 38: Mastery 20
  4. By about 48: Rage 30
  5. Then: Final Attack, Booster, Rush. Because Final Attack rates so low, many fill Booster and Rush first.

If MP is tight, Rush to 10 before Rage lowers its MP cost.

In a party, say something before casting Rage. Some members dislike losing defense; others are put out if you don't cast it.

In short

Fighter lives on Rage. Final Attack is weak and two-handed swords are hard to find, but with an axe you never worry about weapons, and Rage plus bleeding keep your damage steady. Third job is not open yet. Pick a map for your level from the Fighter training routes.
