Play Fighter with a two-handed axe and spend second-job SP in this order: Rush 1, Rage 1, Axe Mastery 5, Axe Booster 1, Final Attack 1, Mastery 20 (by level 38), Rage 30 (by level 48). Rage raises the attack of everyone in your party by 40, which makes the Fighter the warrior parties want most.

If Final Attack feels weak to you, fill Booster and Rush before it. If MP is tight, take Rush to 10 before Rage, because Rush costs less MP with every level.

With a sword, put the same points into Sword Mastery, Sword Booster and Final Attack: Sword. The numbers match the axe versions; only the Mastery bonus differs, Accuracy +20 instead of bleeding.

How to come out of first job

A Fighter can skip Slash Blast in first job. Go Power Strike 20, Improved HP Recovery 15, Max HP Increase 3, Iron Body 20, Precise Strikes 3. Your second-job Final Attack hits three monsters and takes over the group work, while Iron Body raises your weapon defense by up to 40%.

If you already took Slash Blast, that works too. Clear with Slash Blast and press Final Attack when two or three monsters survive. The full first-job table is in the warrior build guide.

All three two-handed axes (levels 35, 40 and 50) can be made with weapon crafting. The ores drop on the way, from Zombie Mushroom, Fire Boar and Cold Eye. Lion's Fang drops from a level 73 monster, so listings are few and it costs several times what The Rising does. Right now the axe is the easier road.

The sword does have one edge. Thanks to Mastery's 20 accuracy, a Fighter with a Zard can hunt Clangs, which need 98 accuracy, from level 48. Other warriors wait until 50 and add accuracy gear first.

If you want a shield, take a one-handed axe. Bleeding is calculated from STR and attack, not the weapon multiplier, so it barely drops when you go one-handed. The level 40 Blue Counter sells for {{price:1312007}} and the level 50 Buck for {{price:1312008}}.

Stats and gear

Raise DEX only to your next weapon's requirement and put everything else into STR. Two-handed weapons need DEX equal to their level: DEX 40 for Sabretooth, DEX 50 for The Rising. DEX from your gear counts toward it.

  • Weapon scrolls: a 60% two-handed axe attack scroll adds 2 attack and costs {{price:2044101}}. The two-handed sword one costs {{price:2044001}}, so the axe is cheaper to scroll too. One level 71 Fighter's level 50 two-handed axe was scrolled up to 83 attack.
  • Gloves: scroll for attack, not critical. A 60% glove attack scroll costs {{price:2040801}}, so scroll your weapon first.
  • Potions: buy what heals most per meso, Meat ({{price:2010003}}) and Lemons ({{price:2010005}}).
  • Gear list: see the warrior equipment list for what you can wear at each level.

How to use Rage and Final Attack

Rage is the Fighter. Attack +40 lifts both minimum and maximum damage about as much as 80 more DEX would, and the gain grows with your weapon. The cost is -40 weapon defense, so hits hurt more. It lands on the whole party, which is why some tanky party members cancel it.

In a party, say something before you cast Rage. Some people hate losing the defense; others are put out if you don't cast it. It needs recasting every 300 seconds, so cast it together with Booster and you won't forget.

Final Attack has a 25% chance to unlock each time you use an attack skill, and you then have 60 seconds to press it. It hits the 3 monsters in front of you for 200% and costs 20 HP and 18 MP. Unlike the Spearman's version, it does not heal you.

  • Press it when two or three monsters survive a Slash Blast.
  • Save it when only one is left or the monster already dies in one hit.
  • Without Slash Blast, Rush the group together, then hit three at once with Final Attack.
  • When potions are tight, skipping Final Attack and its HP cost entirely is fine.

Since the November 24 patch, Rush, Power Strike, Slash Blast and Final Attack push monsters back the instant they hit. Rush also keeps them from snapping back for 1.5 seconds, so gathering and clearing a group is much smoother.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Switching weapons after the advancement: put points into Axe Mastery and pick up a sword, and Mastery, Booster and Final Attack all go dark. Commit to the axe at 30.
  • Waiting for Lion's Fang at 50: it costs several times what The Rising does. Buying The Rising and scrolling it with 60% scrolls gives far more damage for the same money.
  • Casting Rage without a word: it lowers your party's defense too. Announce it, and let anyone who minds cancel it on their side.
  • Pressing Final Attack every time it lights up: it costs 20 HP. Use it only when two or three monsters are left.

Frequently asked questions

Should a Fighter use an axe or a sword?

A two-handed axe. At equal attack it hits a little harder, and the level 35, 40 and 50 axes can all be crafted. The level 50 Rising sells for {{price:1412007}}, while Lion's Fang goes for {{price:1402008}}.

I'm a level 35 Fighter. Where do I go besides KPQ?

Go to The Cave of Evil Eye. Around level 37 Power Strike one-shots Evil Eyes (606 HP). If they still take two hits, spend two more levels on Fire Boars in The Burnt Land first.

Does Rage affect my party?

Yes. Every party member nearby gets attack +40 and weapon defense -40. Mention it before casting; party members who tank sometimes cancel it.

Do I need Final Attack?

Take 1 point early and fill it to 30 after Rage. It hits the 3 monsters in front for 200%, which is good for finishing off what is left. Filling Booster and Rush first is also fine.

How do I switch from sword to axe?

Use an SP Reset scroll and re-spend Mastery, Booster and Final Attack. It sells for {{price:5051001}} right now. You need a new weapon anyway, so switch when you buy your level 50 weapon and pay only once.

Where does a Fighter go after level 70?

Go to Wild Kargo's Area. It needs 117 accuracy, and constant contact damage makes it expensive. If potions are eating your profit, stay at Golem's Temple or Sleepy Dungeon.

When does third job open?

It is not open yet, and no date has been announced. Level to 70 with Rage and The Rising in the meantime.
