A Hunter gathers monsters, blows several up with one Arrow Bomb, and uses the stun to open the distance again. Plenty of Bowmen shoot from range and still burn through potions. Korean guides agree on why: attack matters less than three things. Can you kill a monster before it reaches you? Will the arrow clear the terrain? How many monsters does one shot actually hit? Skill and weapon numbers below come from the game data; the hunting notes are reports from players who levelled the class.

Hunter or Crossbowman

  • Hunter: bow + Arrow Bomb. Best on maps where monsters bunch up around the blast. Bow Mastery adds movement speed.
  • Crossbowman: crossbow + Iron Arrow. Pierces a line of monsters on one floor and wants long, flat platforms. Crossbowman guide

Stats and bows

DEX is your main stat. Put STR only up to what your current bow needs, counting STR on your gear. No INT or LUK. Raising STR now for a level 70 bow you don't have yet is wasted.

Skill build (121 second-job points)

  1. Level 30: Arrow Bomb 1 (opens multi-target attacks)
  2. Up to 33: Bow Mastery 5 → Bow Booster 1
  3. By 34: Booster 5, Soul Arrow 1. Arrow costs drop sharply
  4. By 39: Bow Mastery maxed at 20
  5. By 49: Arrow Bomb maxed at 30
  6. Then: Final Attack → finish Booster and Soul Arrow, Amazon's Judgment 1
  • Count the 61 first-job points and the 121 second-job points separately. Following this order, at 70 you have Mastery, Booster, Soul Arrow, Arrow Bomb and Final Attack maxed with 1 in Judgment.
  • While Arrow Bomb is low, don't fire it at single monsters. Your finished first-job Arrow Blow (240% at max) or Double Shot (120% × 2) cleans up one monster better.
  • If you keep getting touched and drinking potions, raising Judgment's slow before Final Attack is a fair choice.

Hunting with Arrow Bomb

  • Gather monsters, fire the bomb, and use the 2-second stun to reset your distance. If an unstunned monster reaches you, step back first, then fire again.
  • Don't jump to the next group after one shot; finish the leftovers first.
  • If only the first monster is hit and the rest scatter, the six-target advantage is gone. Watch how many it actually hits and shift where you shoot from.
  • With Amazon's Eye maxed, Arrow Blow reaches 470 and Double Shot 420. Inside 65 you swing weakly instead of shooting.
  • Even in range, floors and slopes block arrows. More attack won't fix that: change floors or match the monster's height.
  • When hunting feels slow, check in order: buffs on → monsters too close → arrows blocked by terrain → how many it really hits. Only if you still need more hits after that, upgrade gear and skills.

Three checks before moving up: almost no misses, hits-to-kill you can handle, and hunting that keeps going before potions run out. If any fails, prepare a bit more gear or skill where you are. To compare maps, hunt each for 5 minutes with the same gear and buffs and note EXP, potions and mesos. When it's crowded, briefly try another map with similar monsters.
