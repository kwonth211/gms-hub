A Hunter gathers monsters, blows several up with one Arrow Bomb, and uses the two-second stun to open the distance again. When training feels slow, fix three things before buying attack: killing range before monsters reach you, terrain your arrows can clear, and how many monsters one shot really hits.

Skill build by level

You get 1 SP at the job change and 3 per level after that, so 121 points by level 70. Count them apart from the 61 first-job points. Follow the order below and five skills are maxed at 70 with one point left for Amazon's Judgement.

Choosing a bow: stats, speed and price

DEX is your main stat. Raise STR only to what your current bow needs, counting STR on your gear. From the level 15 Composite Bow on, a bow asks for STR equal to its level. No INT, no LUK.

How to hunt with Arrow Bomb

  • Gather monsters, fire the bomb, and use the two-second stun to reset your distance. If an unstunned monster reaches you, step back first and fire again.
  • A stunned monster keeps walking for a moment before it stops. Fire one beat early instead of waiting until it is on top of you.
  • Don't jump to the next group after one shot. Finish what is left first.
  • If only the first monster gets hit and the rest scatter, you lose the six-target advantage. Watch how many really take damage and shift your spot until the count goes up.
  • With The Eye of Amazon maxed, Arrow Blow reaches 470 and Double Shot 420. Inside 65 you swing the bow weakly instead of shooting.
  • Floors and slopes block arrows even inside your range. Change floors or match the monster's height.
  • You can't shoot in mid-jump. Land on the new floor, then fire.

Getting enough accuracy

With Focus at 10 from first job, Jr. Wraiths stop dodging at level 35. Maps that ask for more, like Wraith at 104 for level 50, need gear. Hat Accuracy scrolls: 100% gives +1 ({{price:2040000}}), 60% gives +2 ({{price:2040001}}), 10% gives +3 ({{price:2040002}}).

If you hunt Cold Eyes, bring 25 of their quest items to Arwen the Fairy in Ellinia for a Helmet Accuracy scroll at 60% or 10%. In a pinch, drink a Supreme Sniper Potion ({{price:2002011}}), and count that cost when you work out whether the map pays.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Raising STR early for a level 70 bow → meet your current bow's requirement only and put the rest in DEX.
  • Maxing Booster first → level 1 already gives the same speed. Stop at 5 and go to Mastery and Arrow Bomb.
  • Running to Lorang right after the job change → at 40 Lorang does not die in two shots; one Hunter moved there at 43 with a scrolled Vaulter 2000. Stay on Ligators and Deep Ant Tunnel until then.
  • Going to Curse Eye → you end up whacking with the bow at close range and burn potions. In the late 30s go to Deep Ant Tunnel or Jr. Wraith.
  • Picking Dark Stone Golem for its EXP → even with full accuracy you get hit a lot. In the 50s go to Wraith, Cold Eye or Ant Tunnel III.

Frequently asked questions

Where should a level 40 Hunter train?

Cold Eye (82 accuracy at 40) or Jr. Wraith. Move to Lorang at 43, once a scrolled Vaulter 2000 kills it in two shots. If those are full, Fire Boars in Burnt Land also die in two.

I'm 38 and still on Ligators. Where next?

Deep Ant Tunnel I or Jr. Wraith. With Focus 10 you stop missing Jr. Wraiths at 35. Curse Eye forces you into close range, where you whack with the bow and burn potions.

What level should a Hunter go to Forgotten Hollow?

From 54. A Vaulter 2000 two-shots Glowshrooms there, and at 57 a level 50 bow two-shots Aqumanders too. The entrance zone suits levels 39–45; the Forgotten Hollow guide has every map.

I'm a level 60 Hunter. What do I upgrade first?

Get an Olympus and scroll it with Bow Attack scrolls. Then scroll your hat for accuracy until you reach the 104 that Wraiths need. Gloves attack comes from the gloves attack scroll quests.

How many points go into Bow Booster?

Five by level 34. Level 1 already gives both speed steps, and 5 is what unlocks Soul Arrow. Fill the rest after level 60.

When can I use third-job skills like Arrow Rain?

Not yet. Third job isn't out, so you keep levelling with second-job skills past 70.

Hunter or Crossbowman: which is better?

Hunter if your maps make monsters bunch up; Crossbowman if you line them up on long, flat platforms. Hunters buy distance with the Arrow Bomb stun, Crossbowmen pierce six monsters with Iron Arrow. The Crossbowman guide covers the other side.
