Where to put AP

  • Warrior: all STR to level 10. After that, add DEX only up to your next weapon's requirement (DEX on gear counts) and put the rest into STR. Get accuracy from Precise Strikes and gear, not DEX.
  • Magician: all INT to level 10. Add LUK only for wands: 10 for the level-20 Metal Wand, 15 for the level-25 Ice Wand, 20 for the level-30 Mithril Wand.
  • Bowman: all DEX to level 10. Add STR only for your next weapon. From the level-15 Composite Bow onward, bows ask for STR equal to the weapon level, and crossbows ask for less. If you haven't picked a second job, follow the bow numbers.
  • Thief: all LUK to level 10. Add DEX only for claws, which ask for DEX equal to their level (the level-15 Steel Titans need 15 DEX). Leave out the DEX your gear already gives.

Made a mistake? An AP Reset scroll undoes it, and it sells for about {{price:5050100}} right now.

Common early mistakes and the fix

  • Hunting monsters that take three or more hits. You get hit more often and potions cost more than the EXP is worth. Drop to monsters you kill in one or two.
  • Buying armor first. More weapon attack means fewer hits per kill and fewer hits taken. Upgrade only the weapon at 15, 20 and 25.
  • Throwing away quest materials. Green and Blue Mushroom Caps go into Pia's and Jane's quests. Keep the caps until those quests are done.
  • Running back and forth for one distant quest. Take every quest in the same town and map at once. Quest rewards hand out plenty of return scrolls.
  • Pumping DEX or LUK as a secondary stat. Add only what your next weapon needs and put everything else into the main stat.

Frequently asked questions

At what level do I take the first job?

Level 10. Warriors go to Perion, magicians to Ellinia, bowmen to Henesys and thieves to Kerning City.

Which class is easiest to level alone?

The star-throwing thief, the Assassin. It attacks while jumping and dodges a lot, so it burns fewer potions. The class guide covers each class in detail.

Where do I sell Pig's Heads and Slime Bubbles?

Put Pig's Heads on the Free Market. A shop pays 7 mesos, while the market price is about {{price:4000008}}. Slime Bubbles sell to a shop for 50 mesos, so the shop is fine for those. Both are early quest materials, so check the quest list for a use first.

When can I join the Kerning Party Quest?

From level 21. Talk to Lakelis in Kerning City on channels 3–12. The PQ has no level cap on this server, so people keep running it into their mid-30s. The Kerning PQ guide covers every stage.

When should I join a town?

Right at level 12. One board greeting a day builds Contribution, and at 17 you can buy a house for 15,000 mesos. The citizenship guide has the details.

When do I prepare for the second job?

Save potions and travel money at 28–29. At 30, talk to your first-job instructor and the quest starts. The second-job guide walks through it.
