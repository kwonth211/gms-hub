A level bracket alone leaves out gear and supply costs. A level 20 archer and a level 13 magician report supply problems; neither names a hunting map.

Before following a guide

  • Check the game version and whether footage comes from an earlier test.
  • Look for the writer’s class, level, equipment, skills and party. A commenter does not inherit the questioner’s conditions.
  • Distinguish measured EXP from calculations assuming instant kills.
  • Subtract consumable costs before comparing income. One rare drop or a growing potion stack does not establish repeatable profit.

What this site provides

Training pages summarize personal accounts. Calculators produce conditional estimates; market prices are observations. None guarantees your character’s profit.
