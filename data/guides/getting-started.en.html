Choose a class, advance, establish a sustainable route, then upgrade. High-level footage is not a first-session equipment target.

Prepare controls and supplies

Compare melee/ranged and solo/party preferences. Place attack, jump, recovery and main skills on usable shortcuts. Reserve ammunition, recharge and return-travel money.

Reach the first job

Bundle island quests and save recovery rewards. From Lith Harbor, visit your chosen town and check level-10 advancement. Prepare a basic weapon and supplies before buying a full set of equipment.

Find a sustainable route

Use the training tool’s level, class, supply-cost and party filters. Read entry requirements before expanding access and rotation details. Include Pia, Jane and KPQ errands when they fit your route rather than buying difficult materials blindly.

Make the first purchase

Check level/stat requirements and upgrade state. Market data represents observed listings, not guaranteed completed trades or current stock. Compare seller, location and observation time. Do not spend anticipated proceeds before selling an item.
