A Fire/Poison Wizard starts with Teleport 1 at level 30 and Fire Arrow maxed by level 40. After that it's MP Eater 3 → Meditation → Teleport → MP Eater, with Poison Breath filled last. For training, follow the fire-weak monsters: Dark Axe Stump and Jr. Wraith → Cold Eye → Wraith → Croco → Forgotten Hollow.

Skill order by level

You get 1 SP at the advancement and 3 per level after, so 121 SP by level 70. That maxes all five main skills and leaves one point for Slow, so all you choose is the order. Most of your grinding is one monster at a time, so Meditation comes before Poison Breath.

  1. Level 30: Teleport 1, so wide maps stop being a slog.
  2. Levels 31–40: everything into Fire Arrow, maxed at 40. Past 18 points, around level 36, it clearly beats your first-job attack. Switch to Fire Arrow at about 35 on fire-weak monsters and about 37 on everything else.
  3. Level 41: MP Eater 3, which unlocks Meditation.
  4. Levels 42–48: Meditation 20. Its +25 magic attack for 300 seconds is often what turns two hits into one.
  5. Levels 49–54: Teleport 20. The cost drops from 55 MP to 15 and the distance grows.
  6. Levels 55–60: MP Eater 20.
  7. Levels 61–70: Poison Breath 30, and the last point into Slow.

If you mostly party and gather monsters, fill Poison Breath right after Fire Arrow instead. With that order Poison Breath is maxed at 50, Meditation at 58 and Teleport at 70. Some Fire/Poison players grind Wraiths at 49 with no Poison Breath at all, on Meditation 16 and Teleport 9.

Poison Breath costs 40 MP and hits each monster for half of Fire Arrow's magic attack. It only pulls ahead when it hits more than two monsters per cast, and on crowded maps monsters rarely bunch up like that. On top of that, a lot of monsters resist poison: Curse Eye, Stone Golem, Rafflesia, Rotten Mushroom and Sporewood. The only poison-weak monster is the level 29 Fairy, and with one to three per map it's not worth hunting.

Where Fire Arrow gets blocked and where to stand

Fire Arrow is a spell that travels to its target, so walls stop it. Platforms only let it through from below to above. On slopes and stacked floors, stand lower than the monster. On a map with steep height changes like Lorang's, nine casts in ten get blocked, so Fire/Poison skips it.

Burn deals extra damage every second for 3 seconds to whatever Fire Arrow hits. It finishes monsters that survive two hits by a sliver, but a bug makes burn land late or show a number without dealing damage. Count your hits-to-kill without burn. Fire-resistant monsters take 25% less damage and can't be burned at all.

Frequently asked questions

When do I take Poison Breath?

Last. It only beats Fire Arrow when it hits more than two monsters per cast, and Curse Eye, Stone Golem and several Forgotten Hollow monsters resist poison. Take it early only if you party and gather monsters.

Do I need an SP reset at level 40?

No, not if Fire Arrow is maxed at 40. Use one only to move points out of Meditation or Poison Breath into Fire Arrow. An SP Reset Scroll costs {{price:5051001}} on the Free Market right now.

Potions are eating all my mesos in the 40s. How do I cut the cost?

Hunt monsters that have MP and level MP Eater. Jr. Wraith (70 MP), Wraith (80) and Cold Eye (50) give MP back, while Ligator and Fire Boar have 0 MP and give nothing. Turn Magic Guard off and refill MP with food like Lemons and Oranges.

Fire Arrow keeps getting blocked. What do I do?

Move below the monster. Platforms let the spell through only from underneath, and walls never do. If you built Magic Claw in first job, switch to it on spots where Fire Arrow can't get through.

Where does a level 60 Fire/Poison train?

Myewood and Sporewood in Forgotten Hollow, or Cold Eye. Both are weak to fire, and with Teleport maxed at this level you can hold a spot on a wide map. Sporewood resists poison, so use Fire Arrow only.

Fire/Poison or Ice/Lightning?

Fire/Poison hits one monster harder; Ice/Lightning ignores terrain and freezes what it hits. Fire/Poison hunts Jr. Wraith, Cold Eye and Croco while Ice/Lightning hunts Fire Boar, Copper Drake and Red Drake, so the two barely share maps. The other side is covered in the Ice/Lightning guide.
