The first thing Fire/Poison players complain about is "Fire Arrow gets blocked by the terrain all the time". In return it hits one monster harder than any other mage attack, so some players say it reaches one-hit kills soonest. Fire/Poison is the least common second-job mage on the rankings, yet of the three mage paths it has the highest share of characters past level 50. Skill numbers below come from the game data; the hunting notes are reports from players who levelled the class.

Most Fire/Poison players make Fire Arrow their main attack and leave Poison Breath for later, for three reasons.

  • By guide-site maths, Poison Breath only beats Fire Arrow when it hits more than two monsters at once. On crowded maps monsters rarely bunch up that much.
  • Victoria Island has a fire-weak monster in almost every level range, but the only poison-weak one is the level 29 Fairy, in an area that isn't open yet.
  • A weakness adds 25% damage and a resistance takes 25% off; resistant monsters also can't be burned or poisoned.

Stats and gear

INT is your main stat; put LUK only up to what your next wand needs. INT raises magic attack, MP and accuracy together. Wands go Mithril Wand (30) → Wizard Wand (35) → Fairy Wand (40) → Cromi (50). Spells take the same time to cast whatever the weapon speed, so just wear the highest magic attack you can equip. See the Magician equipment list for each level.

  • Pure INT, no LUK: the level 40 Yellow Umbrella has no stat requirement at all (52 magic attack), so you can wear it without a single point in LUK. Right now Clang on Florina Beach drops it; once Forgotten Hollow opens, Raffle will too.
  • Scroll gloves for magic attack, not critical rate.
  • Turn Magic Guard off while grinding. Blocking damage with MP costs close to twice as many mesos to recover the same damage.
  • If you're just short of a one- or two-hit kill, a Magic Potion's +10 magic attack can close the gap.

Watch Fire Arrow's hitbox: a wall in its path stops it, even when the arrow seems to fly through. Platforms only let it pass from below to above, so on slopes stand lower than the monster. One player went to the Lorang map and came back because nine shots in ten were blocked.

Burn: the monster takes extra damage every second for 3 seconds, which finishes off monsters that survive two hits by a sliver. But players say a bug still makes burn land late, or show a number without dealing damage, so count your hits-to-kill without it to be safe.

Skill build

  1. Level 30: Teleport 1
  2. Up to about 40: everything into Fire Arrow until it's maxed
  3. Option A (the guide-site default): from 41, Poison Breath → Meditation → MP Eater
  4. Option B (if you mostly kill one monster at a time): Meditation first. One level 49 Fire/Poison had no Poison Breath at all, with Meditation 16 and Teleport 9.
  5. Option C: raise Meditation first, then move the points into Fire Arrow with an SP reset; plenty of players recommend this too.

Whichever road you take, by level 70 all five other skills are maxed and only Slow keeps a single point.

After Forgotten Hollow on November 18

The new area brings four fire-weak monsters: Raffle, Rafflesia, Myewood and Sporewood. Some say the weakness bonus is too small to change much, but just having somewhere to go has players looking forward to it. Pick a map for your level from the Fire/Poison training routes.
