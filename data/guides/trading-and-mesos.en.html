Our prices come from shop listings players uploaded and from price reports.

Reading the market

All listings shows the seller, channel, Free Market room, asking price and when each was last seen. Asking listings were seen within the last 24 hours; stock can have changed since. Sold marks a listing confirmed as sold.

Reports without a seller or location are separate from shop inventory. Seeing a listing again is not another trade. Chart gaps mean missing observations.

Find the channel and room selling your item

  1. Search for an item in Live Free Market prices and open its name. English and Korean names work, including shortcuts such as “gfa60” and “steelies”.
  2. In the item details, look at the asking price, equipment options, seller, channel (CH) and Free Market room (FM) side by side. Upgraded equipment and listings seen at different times cannot be priced against each other directly.
  3. Visit that channel and room in the game and check the current stock and price. “Last seen” is when the site last read the listing; the item can sell or change price before you arrive.

Save items and set Discord price alerts

Choose Save item in the item details to keep it in My saved items for your next visit. The list stays in this browser and does not automatically sync to other devices.

To watch for a price, enter your maximum in Price alert and choose the Discord alert button. This requires connecting Discord and allowing DMs. An alert can notify you of matching shop listings or megaphones; it does not reserve stock. Confirm the price and availability with the seller before buying.

Hunting income is different

Counting loot at asking prices inflates your profit. Record actual proceeds and consumable costs separately.
