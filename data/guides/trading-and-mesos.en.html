In Classic World the biggest mesos come from quests that reward scrolls, then from selling hunting drops, then from selling to players instead of NPCs. When you buy, check the price and the seller's channel and room in Live Free Market prices first; when you sell, list at a price that actually sells using the sell price calculator.

How to buy cheap on the Free Market

  1. Search for an item in Live Free Market prices and open its name. English and Korean names work, including shortcuts such as “gfa60” and “steelies”.
  2. In the item details, read the asking price, equipment options, seller, channel (CH) and Free Market room (FM) side by side. Upgraded equipment and listings seen at different times carry different prices even for the same item.
  3. Visit that channel and room in the game and look at the current stock and price. “Last seen” is when the site last read the listing, so the item can sell or change price before you arrive.
  4. Hover over the item in the trade window to read its options and quantity before you press confirm.

Listings marked as selling were seen within the last 24 hours. Sold marks a listing confirmed as sold. Seeing the same listing again does not count as another trade.

Price reports without a seller or location are shown apart from shop inventory. A gap in a chart means nothing was collected during that time.

Save items and set Discord price alerts

Choose Save item in the item details to keep it in My saved items for your next visit. The list stays in this browser and does not sync to other devices by itself.

To watch for a price, enter your maximum in Price alert and choose the Discord alert button. You need to connect Discord and allow DMs. You get an alert when a matching shop listing or megaphone shows up. An alert does not reserve the item, so ask the seller about price and stock before you buy.

How to list your items at a price that sells

Enter your asking price in the sell price calculator. It shows how many out of ten listings in that price band sold in the last 7 days, and where your price would rank among current listings. In a hurry, list below the "sell fast" price: the highest price band where more than half the listings sold.

Overpriced listings do not sell. In the 7 days to 24 November, 158 of 321 listings of the 60% glove ATT scroll under 1.44m mesos sold. From 1.89m up, just 1 of 411 sold.

Open your shop in a Free Market room. Live prices shows every listing by channel (CH) and room (FM) number, and buyers walk to that number. To get your own shop onto the price list right away, start Shop scan in Chrome or Edge on a PC, share the MapleStory window and open your shop.

In-game megaphones sell for {{price:5070000}} and item megaphones for {{price:5070006}}. To advertise for free, send a free megaphone from Live megaphones. It stays up for 24 hours, you can send one every 2 minutes, and it also appears on the Buy & sell board.

How to avoid trade scams

Search the other player's name on Scam reports before you trade. Walk away from names that several different players have called out.

The trickHow to stop it
Scroll swap: a 60% goes into the trade window, then gets swapped for a 10% (same icon)Hover over the scroll and read the % right before you press confirm
Equipment swap: they show you scrolled gear and put a clean one in the windowHover over the gear in the window and read its ATT and slots left
Bundle short: they sell a stack and put in only part of itCount the quantity in the window before you confirm
Spot scam: they take money for a hunting spot and stay or come backPay after you hold the spot and they have changed channel
Fame scam: they take fame first and never paySplit it: half the mesos, then fame, then the other half

Buying or selling mesos or items for real money breaks the game rules and gets accounts banned. If you were scammed, take a screenshot, file the in-game report first, then post the name on Scam reports.

Count hunting income at what you actually sold

Counting loot at asking prices inflates your profit. Record actual proceeds and consumable costs separately. Before you sell, go by the Sold price, not the asking price.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to make mesos?

Clear every scroll-reward quest your level allows. Jane's mushroom quest at level 20 gives an Intermediate weapon ATT scroll, and Rowen's 150 Cursed Dolls at 36 gives a 60% glove ATT scroll. After that, hunt a monster you kill in one or two hits that has a pricey drop.

How many mesos an hour is a good rate?

Party hunting in the 40s and 50s leaves about 40k–60k mesos an hour. A four-player party with a level 70+ hunter in Primeval Forest II leaves about 250k an hour. Classes that burn more potions keep less on the same map.

Where should I open my shop?

Open it in a Free Market room. Live prices shows each listing with its channel and room number, so buyers come straight to you. If cheaper listings of the same item pile up, yours sells later, so look at the lowest price in Live prices before you list.

I opened a shop but it does not show up on price sites.

Upload it yourself with Shop scan and it shows up right away. Open Shop scan in Chrome or Edge on a PC, share the MapleStory window and open your shop. Phone browsers cannot share a game window, so scanning does not work there.

What price should I list at?

List below the "sell fast" price in the sell price calculator. That is the highest price band where more than half the listings sold in the last 7 days. If dozens of cheaper listings sit ahead of yours, you wait that much longer.

My character is trade-restricted and I cannot sell anything.

The in-game restriction message tells you when it lifts. Until then, keep the items you plan to sell instead of dropping them, and buy potions and arrows from NPC shops. Once it lifts, price everything with the sell price calculator and list it in one go.

Can I sell cash items for mesos?

Yes, buying and selling them for mesos inside the game is allowed. A Selective 5-slot Coupon goes for {{price:5680001}} and a Mystery Fashion Crate for {{price:5222000}}. Only selling for real money gets accounts banned.

An item's price jumped several times overnight.

Prices swing hard right after a patch. After the 25 November patch changed how the Tania Cloak looks, its price went from 50k–80k mesos to over 700k in a day. At times like that, trade on recent Sold prices, not asking prices.

For Gloves Attack scrolls, use the sold shares and competing offers calculated from 3,363 listings.
