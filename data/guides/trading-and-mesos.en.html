Our prices come from observed shop listings and price reports. They are not verified transaction payments.

Reading the market

Compare seller, channel, Free Market room, asking price and observation time in All listings. Asking listings were observed within 24 hours; stock may have changed. Sold is an observed status.

Reports without a seller or location are separate from shop inventory. Seeing a listing again is not another trade. Chart gaps mean missing observations.

Hunting income is different

Assuming loot sells at asking prices can inflate profit. Record actual proceeds and consumable costs separately.
