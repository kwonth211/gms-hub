Our prices come from observed shop listings and price reports.

Reading the market

Compare seller, channel, Free Market room, asking price and observation time in All listings. Asking listings were observed within 24 hours; stock may have changed. Sold is an observed status.

Reports without a seller or location are separate from shop inventory. Seeing a listing again is not another trade. Chart gaps mean missing observations.

Find the channel and room selling your item

  1. Search for an item in Live Free Market prices and open its name. English and Korean names work, including shortcuts such as “gfa60” and “steelies”.
  2. Compare the asking price, equipment options, seller, channel (CH) and Free Market room (FM) in the item details. Upgraded equipment and observations from different times may not be directly comparable.
  3. Visit that channel and room in the game and check the current stock and price. “Last seen” is the time of observation; the item may sell or its price may change before you arrive.

Save items and set Discord price alerts

Choose Save item in the item details to keep it in My saved items for your next visit. The list stays in this browser and does not automatically sync to other devices.

To watch for a price, enter your maximum in Price alert and choose the Discord alert button. This requires connecting Discord and allowing DMs. An alert can notify you of matching shop listings or megaphones; it does not reserve stock. Confirm the price and availability with the seller before buying.
