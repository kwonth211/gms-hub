Earning and spending mesos in Classic World works quite differently from old MapleStory. New accounts can't trade right away, trade posts are all shorthand, and there's even a system that puts "debt" on your account if you receive mesos of dubious origin. Here are the rules to know before you start trading and realistic ways to build mesos early, up to date with the October 30 patch.

New accounts can't trade right away

A new account can use trading and personal shops once one character reaches level 15 and 24 hours have passed. Dropping items on the ground used to have the same requirement, but the October 22 patch lifted it. Whispers open at level 10 and megaphones at 15, so haggling on a brand-new character is hard.

So S> GFA 60% 580k neg means "selling a glove attack 60% scroll for 580k, negotiable", and "C/O 2m, A/W 3m" means the best offer so far is 2m and 3m buys it outright.

Why you shouldn't buy mesos: meso debt

Classic World has a meso debt system the old game didn't. When mesos from real-money trading or other illegitimate sources reach your account through a trade, that amount is put on your account as debt. Buying cheap mesos from an unknown seller leads here:

  • While in debt you can't trade, open a personal shop, send Cash Shop gifts or drop items.
  • Since the October 30 patch you also can't pick up mesos or items others dropped, only drops from monsters you killed.
  • Every login shows how much you owe, and a "repay" button sits at the top centre of the screen until it's paid.
  • You repay in town, in amounts as small as 1 meso. Once it's fully paid, everything unlocks again.

Real-money trading also breaks Nexon's terms of service. Earning mesos in game is the safest way, and in the end the cheapest.

Where to check prices

Always check a price site before buying or selling. Sites collect prices differently, so their numbers differ a little.

  • Based on real sales: some sites show prices items actually sold for in personal shops and where they're on sale now (channel and room). These tend to be the most accurate.
  • Megaphone feeds: some sites stream in-game megaphones live, handy for seeing who wants what right now.
  • Our live market: GMS Hub's live market shows medians from shops players scanned, with where to buy.

The sites are listed under prices and trading. Some price sites can list your shop's items automatically via Discord screen sharing, but they can't tell gear stats apart (how many scrolls were used). When buying gear, visit the shop and check the stats yourself.

Realistic ways to earn early mesos

1. Quests that reward scrolls. Some quests have valuable rewards: Jane's weapon attack scroll at 20, the earring scroll from the Kerning party quest, the glove scroll from the Cursed Doll quest. If they aren't for your class, selling them right away pays for early gear. See seven early quests worth doing.

2. Kerning party quest reward boxes. Every clear gives a box with scrolls, gear and crafting materials, a good way to earn EXP and mesos together from 21 to 30. See the Kerning party quest guide.

3. Cut potion costs first. Spending less matters as much as earning. Pick monsters you kill in one or two hits, use efficient potions (Meat for HP, Lemons for MP) and get shop discounts through citizenship, and your spending drops sharply.

4. Pick things up. Etc. items are in demand as quest materials. Keep materials other quests use, like Fire Boar tusks, and you'll find buyers.

Selling without losing out

  • Check recent prices for the same item before listing. Far above the market it won't sell; far below and a reseller snaps it up.
  • For scrolls whose prices swing a lot, the C/O on megaphones is another guide.
  • When you open a personal shop, put your main item in the shop title so passers-by come in.
