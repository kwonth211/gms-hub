The second job starts when you talk to your first-job instructor at level 30. You carry their letter to a test instructor, collect 30 Black Marbles in a hidden test area, bring back the proof and pick your second job from your old instructor.

Each test instructor is an NPC simply called the Job Instructor for that class. The advancement is four quests in a row, and the first two pay 4,780 EXP each, 9,560 in total.

Second-job steps: four quests

  1. The Warrior's / Magician's / Bowman's / Thief's Next Journey: at level 30, talk to your first-job instructor. You must still be in your first job. Pays 4,780 EXP.
  2. Finding the Instructor: take the instructor's letter to the test instructor from the table above. Handing it over pays another 4,780 EXP.
  3. Test of Qualification: the test instructor sends you into a hidden area. Kill the monsters inside until you have 30 Black Marbles and hand them in.
  4. Proof of Qualification: bring the proof the test instructor gives you back to your first-job instructor and choose your second job.

All four steps move between the same town and the same map, so one or two return scrolls cover the whole thing.

Where each test instructor stands

Warrior: West Rocky Mountain IV

Get the letter from Dances with Balrog in Perion and head to West Rocky Mountain IV. The map holds 15 Wild Boars (Lv 25), 14 Dark Axe Stumps (Lv 23) and 3 Lupins (Lv 37). Stay on the platforms while you look for the instructor so the Lupins don't wear you down.

Magician: The Forest North of Ellinia

Get the letter from Grendel in Ellinia and go to The Forest North of Ellinia. With 26 Slimes, 21 Stumps and 13 Axe Stumps (Lv 17), it poses no threat to a level-30 magician.

Bowman: The Road to the Dungeon

Get the letter from Athena Pierce at the Bowman Instructional School and walk to The Road to the Dungeon. The strongest monster there is the Blue Mushroom (Lv 19), so the trip is easy. The map returns you to Henesys, so restocking takes a minute.

Thief: Construction Site North of Kerning City

Get the letter from Dark Lord in the Kerning City hideout and go to the construction site north of town. It holds 23 Red Snails, 11 Dark Stumps, Orange Mushrooms and Octopuses, nothing that threatens a level-30 thief.

Don't jump to Lorangs or Wraiths the day you advance. A low-level new attack is weaker than your first-job attack, so stay on your current map until the new skill catches up. The route continues in levels 30–70 and on the levels 31–40 training page.

Common mistakes right after advancing

  • Hunting with a level-1 new attack. Fire Arrow needs 18 points and Cold Beam needs you to reach about level 35–38 before they beat your first-job attack. Until then, keep using the old one.
  • Taking the wrong Mastery for your weapon. Mastery, Booster and Final Attack are separate for each weapon type. Settle on your level-30 weapon first.
  • Maxing Booster early. Level 1 already gives the full speed boost. Take one point and fill your attack and Mastery first.
  • Hunters still buying arrows. One point in Soul Arrow means you stop using arrows.
  • Walking into the test area with no potions. Knockback and repeated hits make the test burn more potions than normal training. Bring the supplies listed above.

Frequently asked questions

What level is the second job?

Level 30. Reach 30 while still in your first job and your first-job instructor gives you the Next Journey quest.

How many Black Marbles do I need?

Thirty. The monsters in the hidden test area drop them. Hand all 30 to the test instructor to get the proof.

I spent my second-job SP wrong. What now?

Use an SP Reset scroll. It sells for about {{price:5051001}} on the Free Market right now. Thieves who leveled with stars and want to become Bandits reset their skills at level 40–45 and switch to daggers.

Do magicians get a Booster?

No. Fire/Poison, Ice/Lightning and Cleric all lack a Booster skill. They get Teleport instead, and leveling it drops its MP cost from 55 to 15.

When is the third job?

Only the first two jobs are open right now. Plan to fill all your second-job skills by level 70.

Which second job should I pick?

Pick Assassin to hunt alone and make mesos, or Cleric to heal in parties. The class guide lays out all ten jobs in one table.
