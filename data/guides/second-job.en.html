Level 30 is a point to choose a weapon and skill direction, not an instruction to leave your current training map.

Prepare for the trial

  1. Check the quest alert and first-job instructor.
  2. Review available points and the intended weapon family.
  3. Restock recovery, ammunition and travel money.
  4. Allow for repeated contact damage inside the trial.

Return to your instructor

Warriors return to Dances with Balrog in Perion, Bowmen to Athena Pierce in Henesys, Magicians to Grendel in Ellinia, and Thieves to Dark Lord in Kerning. Follow the assigned instructor route. The reviewed trial collects 30 Dark Marbles; prioritize your actual quest window and submission status.

Before spending the first points

Match Mastery and Booster to the intended weapon. Hunters need ammunition management and consistency; claw users need reliable damage; Warriors need accuracy and weapon skills; Magicians must compare new and old attack costs. Read current prerequisites before allocating.

Your first post-advancement session

Keep the old map as a baseline with the same gear and buffs. A low-level new attack may not yet outperform the old one. Compare ten-minute sessions before moving into Lorang or Wraith routes.
