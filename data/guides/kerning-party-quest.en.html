The Kerning party quest needs four players of level 21 or higher; the party leader talks to Lakelis in Kerning City, and you get 30 minutes. The stage 1 answer is always 10, 15, 21, 25 or 30, and in stages 2–4 you find the combination by keeping the first two players in place and moving only the last one. Accept Lakelis's Proof of Companionship before your first run and one King Slime kill earns you a 60% earring scroll.

Entry requirements and how to find a party

Lakelis stands next to the three sewer pipes at the bottom of Kerning City. Bring four players of level 21 or higher in one party, and the leader talks to her to enter. Classic World's KPQ has no level cap, so parties in their 30s go in too. It is a good choice whenever field maps cost too many potions or have no room.

Find a party in Kerning City chat. To join, post your level, class and run type, for example "J>KPQ 27 Rogue Bonus"; leaders recruit with "L>KPQ". Most KPQ parties gather on channels 3–12.

Before entering, agree as a party on rush or bonus. Pack separate HP potions for the boss and a full stack of stars or arrows. Magicians should put at least one point into Magic Guard so MP soaks the King Slime's slam.

Stage 1: Cloto's questions and the coupons

Everyone except the leader talks to Cloto for a question. The answer is 10, 15, 21, 25 or 30, and bringing that many coupons earns you a pass. Hand your pass to the leader.

Ligators drop the coupons. The stage 1 Ligators are level 21 with 778 HP and 68 EXP, weaker than the level 31 field Ligators with 853 HP. Coupons can be passed around, so everyone, the leader included, keeps killing and feeds whoever is short.

If you collect the number someone else called out instead of reading your own question, you end up hunting again. Each player types their own answer in chat and hands in the coupons to Cloto as soon as they have enough.

Stages 2–4: the order to try ropes, platforms and barrels

In all three stages, three players each take one spot, and the right combination changes every run. One player calls the order; the first two stay put and only the last player moves, one spot at a time. Once everyone is in place, nobody moves until the leader sees the result.

Follow the order below and you never try the same combination twice. The numbers are the rope numbers, the platform numbers set by the cats, and the numbers written on the barrels.

Stage 4 can take up to 20 tries, so it eats the most time. To finish inside 30 minutes, decide who calls the stage 4 order before you get there. If you lose track, type the last combination you tried in chat and pick up from there.

Final stage: how to kill the King Slime

In the last map, kill the Curse Eyes and Jr. Neckis up top and the King Slime at the bottom, and pool 10 passes with the leader. Jump just before the King Slime's slam hits the ground to dodge it. Keep your HP comfortably high and your potion key under your finger.

Low-level parties sometimes skip the King Slime and fill the passes from the upper floors. That leaves Proof of Companionship unfinished, so anyone with the quest should say so before the run starts. The King Slime drops shoes.

The bonus map, and rush vs bonus EXP

Clearing the final stage sends you to the bonus map, which spawns 12 Green Mushrooms and 24 Horny Mushrooms. Rush parties talk to Nella right away, leave, and start the next run. Bonus parties take one floor each and hunt until the time runs out.

A rush run is worth 5,500–6,200 EXP. With a good party, rush and bonus both land around 60,000 an hour; one player clocked 69,000 on rush against 63,000 on bonus. At 60,000 an hour, 21 to 22 takes about 25 minutes.

From 21 to 30, a bonus party is the better pick. If you can stay behind alone in the bonus map after the others leave, you have every monster to yourself and that gives the most EXP of all. Rush barely uses potions and never involves fighting over a map, and some players rush until their mid-40s for that reason.

A box is worth about 3,500 mesos on average. Warriors in their 30s spend 30 to 38 in Ligator parties, staying in stage 1 and killing the Ligators that keep spawning instead of clearing it. The 30-minute limit still applies, and you never get a clear box.

Proof of Companionship and what the earring scrolls sell for

Lakelis's Proof of Companionship is a one-time quest available from level 21. Accept it, clear the whole party quest with the King Slime killed, then talk to Lakelis again. It pays 1,870 EXP, 687 mesos, 1 fame and one 60% earring scroll.

Mistakes that ruin a run, and the fix

  • Collecting coupons without reading your question: answers differ from player to player, so copying someone else's number leaves you short or over. Type your own answer in chat first.
  • Several players moving at once in stages 2–4: you lose track of what you tried and repeat combinations. Only the last player moves, and only one player calls the order.
  • Thinking the run is over once the King Slime dies: it ends only when all 10 passes are in. Check for leftover Curse Eyes and Jr. Neckis up top and hand the remaining passes to the leader.
  • Following someone else's floor in the bonus map: one floor sits empty while monsters pile up on another. Assign floors at the start and split a leaver's floor again.
  • Repeating because one run paid well: count recruiting time, boss potions and bonus EXP, and set that against the same time in the field. When the waits get long, go to the field.

When to leave KPQ for the field

  • When recruiting drags on, time a full hour including the wait. If the field gives more, go to the field.
  • If the King Slime eats your potions, level in the field until your damage improves and come back around 24.
  • Between 21 and 30, the field options are the tree dungeon Green Mushrooms or, for claw thieves, Stirges; in your 30s, the Excavation Sites or the Ant Tunnel. Maps for every level are in the 1–30 leveling guide and the 30–70 leveling guide.

Frequently asked questions

What levels is KPQ for?

From 21, with no upper limit. Warriors in their 30s stay in Ligator parties until 38, and some players rush until their mid-40s.

Does rush or bonus give more EXP?

They are about even, around 60,000 an hour each. If you can stay alone in the bonus map, bonus gives the most. Rush is the lower-effort option and barely uses potions.

What are the stage 1 answers?

One of 10, 15, 21, 25 or 30. The first job is at level 10, level 1 to 2 takes 15 EXP, KPQ opens at 21 and the second job is at 30. Read your own question and collect that many coupons.

Are the stage 2–4 answers fixed?

No, they change every run. Keep the first two players in place and move only the last, as in the order table, and you always hit it within 4 rope tries, 10 platform tries and 20 barrel tries. That is 34 tries at most across all three stages.

What happens if we skip the King Slime?

The party quest still clears once the passes are in, but Lakelis's Proof of Companionship does not. If anyone in the party has the quest, kill the King Slime that run.

Should I open my KPQ boxes?

Yes. A box gives about 3,500 mesos on average. Saving them up and opening them all at once is fine.

What do I type in chat to find a party?

To join, type something like "J>KPQ 27 Rogue Bonus". Leaders post the run type, minimum level, channel and head count, such as "KPQ bonus 30+ ch12 2/4". Ligator parties recruit separately, for example "KPQ ligator ch10 2/4".

I play a magician and keep dying to the King Slime.

Learn Magic Guard and keep it on. At level 1 it moves 30% of the damage you take onto your MP, and at level 15, 80%. Jump just before the slam lands, and bring plenty of MP potions.

How many runs do I get through an hour at 21?

A rush run takes about 6 minutes, so roughly 10 an hour. A party in the low 20s makes about 40,000 an hour, so 21 to 22 takes under 40 minutes. Past 25 it climbs to about 60,000 an hour.
