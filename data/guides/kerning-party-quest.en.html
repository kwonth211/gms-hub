KPQ starts at level 21 with four players.

Agree before entering

Rush parties repeat clears; bonus parties stay to train in the final map. Check Lakelis quests, recovery supplies and ammunition. Let the leader verify current entry availability and assign someone to coordinate combinations.

Coupons

Read your actual question before collecting. Reviewed examples include first job 10, second job 30, KPQ entry 21 and EXP from level 1 to 2 of 15. Convert the requested coupons into a pass and give it to the leader. Help identify unfinished requirements rather than overcollecting blindly.

Ropes and platforms

Three players test combinations. Change one position at a time and wait for the leader’s result. These are per-run combinations, not a universal fixed answer.

King Slime and remaining passes

Keep HP ready for the lower boss area. Afterward, finish remaining targets and passes rather than assuming the boss ends the stage. If survival is unreliable, improve recovery and party preparation before chasing damage.

Bonus training

Divide the map and reassign when a player leaves. Compare a full session including waiting, boss supplies and bonus EXP against field training.
