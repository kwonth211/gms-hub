The Kerning City party quest (KPQ) is the most recommended way to level from 21 to about 30. EXP comes faster than grinding, and the clear reward box drops scrolls, gear and potions, so you build up early mesos too. This guide covers finding a party, each stage, and an order for solving the platform puzzles quickly so your first run doesn't get lost.

Requirements and prep

Talk to Lakelis next to the Kerning City sewer entrance. You need a party of four, all level 21 or higher, and party quests are reported to run only on some channels, so go where people gather. The final boss hurts at 21, so many players suggest starting around level 24.

On your first visit, take the "Proof of Partnership" quest from Lakelis. Clearing a run that includes King Slime gives an earring 60% scroll. Details are in seven early quests worth doing.

Rush parties and bonus parties

KPQ parties come in two kinds, and recruiting posts say which.

RushBonus

First time? Start with a bonus party. It's a good way to learn the stages, and on the bonus map each player takes a floor, so you don't get in each other's way.

Finding a party

Post your level, class and the kind of party you want, then wait near the people recruiting.

  • Example: LF KPQ 27 thief bonus means "looking for a Kerning PQ party, level 27 thief, bonus run".
  • KPQ is Kerning Party Quest; LF or LFP is Looking For (Party).
  • warrior, mage, archer (or bowman) and thief are understood by everyone.

Stage by stage

Stage 1: coupons

Talk to Cloto on the right; each party member gets a different question. Collect as many coupons as the answer, dropped by the Ligators on the map. Most questions are game trivia.

If the question asks aboutAnswer (coupons)
First job advancement level10

Party quest minimum level21
Second job advancement level30

Trade your coupons to Cloto for a pass, then drop the pass on the floor for the party leader. Coupons can be handed to other members. Once you have yours, keep killing Ligators and pass coupons to whoever still needs them; it finishes much faster.

Stage 2: ropes

Three players each hang on one of four ropes to find the right combination. If it's wrong, change positions and try again.

Stage 3: cat platforms

Three players each stand on one of five platforms. With 10 combinations it's confusing at first.

Stage 4: barrels

Three of six barrels, 20 combinations.

Solving the platform stages: try them in this order

Stages 2-4 are all "which three spots do we stand on", so the number of combinations is fixed. No memorising needed: try them in ascending order as below and you'll never repeat a combination and always hit the answer. Spots are numbered from the left (or top). Assigning each of the three players a digit (first, second, third) makes it faster still.

StageCombinationsOrder to try

The nice thing about this order is that usually only one player moves at a time: going from 123 to 124, only the third player steps from spot 3 to spot 4. Even with the barrels' 20 combinations, you hit the answer in about 10 tries on average.

Stage 5: King Slime

Work down a tall map through Curse Eyes and Jr. Neckis, then King Slime at the bottom. Holding down-jump as soon as you enter gets you to King Slime fast. Hand coupons from monsters to the party leader.

King Slime's body slam and magic attack hurt. Usually a warrior clears the small monsters while ranged classes hit King Slime. Don't worry too much about dying: you respawn on the stage 1 map, not in town, and can walk back to stage 5.

Rewards

A clear gives a reward box with gear, scrolls, crafting materials, potions and a chance at furniture for your house. King Slime can also drop Squishy Shoes. Selling the scrolls from these boxes to buy gear is the easiest way to build mesos early on.

Rush parties get more clear rewards but give up the bonus map drops; bonus parties are the opposite. The bonus map is full of mushrooms, so gear like the Pan Lid and valuable scrolls are possible. Check what drops are worth on the live market.

When to move on from KPQ

KPQ is best up to about level 30. After that, grinding spots like the Ant Tunnel are more efficient. Where to go after second job is covered class by class in the second job guide.
