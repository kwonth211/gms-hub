Citizenship is new in Classic World; old MapleStory didn't have it. Pick a town, help it out, and your rank rises; higher ranks make that town's shops, taxis and storage cheaper and unlock citizen-only goods. Housing is tied to citizenship too. This guide sticks to what has been confirmed so far.

Getting started

You can apply from level 12. Only two towns offer citizenship, Henesys and Kerning City: apply at the Henesys town hall or the Kerning City civic centre. You can be a citizen of one town at a time.

Citizenship belongs to the account, not the character. Joining another town on an alt moves the whole account there and costs mesos. Your old town's rank is kept and resumes if you return, but returning costs mesos again. So pick the town that suits your main character's class as soon as your main hits 12.

The points that raise your rank are called contribution. You earn them from requests on the town's community board.

  • Daily requests: 100 at rank 1, plus 25 for each rank.
  • Weekly donations: hand in monster etc. items; 700 at rank 1, plus 175 for each rank.

The October 30 patch lowered the contribution needed for ranks 4 to 10. Rank tables from before the patch make it look slower than it is. At rank 5 the town leader's VIP daily request opens, worth more than twice the EXP of the regular ones.

Do the daily request every day and the weekly donation every week. Rank 2 takes about a week, but the higher ranks take months.

Without the level you can't rank up, however much contribution you have. Players also report that you don't rank up automatically (you have to talk to the NPC) and that contribution above the requirement is lost. Neither is officially confirmed, but going to the NPC as soon as you qualify is the safe choice. Discounts apply only to marked items in the town you currently belong to.

The real goal: rank 9 glove attack scrolls

Higher ranks unlock citizen-only scrolls in the town shop. Most cost about the same as in the Free Market, or more. One exception stands out: at rank 9 you can buy glove attack Lesser and glove magic Lesser scrolls for 40,000 mesos. Lesser scrolls succeed 100% of the time for +1 attack.

Compared by cost per attack point, the gap is huge. Intermediate (60%) and Greater (10%) can fail, so you have to count how many scrolls one success takes on average, and at launch prices they cost hundreds of thousands of mesos per attack point. With Lesser scrolls at 40k, you can take 5-slot gloves to +5 attack for around 200,000 mesos. The maths is in the scroll odds guide, and the scroll calculator runs it for your gear.

Rank 9 needs level 52 and a lot of contribution, though, so it's a long-term goal that takes months of steady dailies.

Housing

You can buy a house at citizen rank 2 and level 17, with mesos, from the housing NPC in Henesys or Kerning City. It's one house per account, shared by all its characters. Two housing quests around level 17 give 100 contribution each, which helps you on from rank 2 to rank 3.

House prices haven't been published officially. Nexon's notice only says houses are bought with mesos, and the price isn't in the game files, so it appears to be set by the server.

Most furniture is decorative, but a few pieces are useful:

  • Storage furniture (an old wooden chest or a rusty safe): 10,000 mesos, opens your storage inside the house.
  • A training dummy: from rank 4, 300,000 mesos, to hit as much as you like at home.
  • A resident's chair: from rank 7, 500,000 mesos. Sitting on it restores an extra 20 HP and 5 MP every 10 seconds, 7,200 HP and 1,800 MP an hour. It cost 10,000 in the closed test and rose to 500,000 at launch.

Summary

  1. At 12, become a citizen of the town that suits the class you mainly play.
  2. Never skip the daily request and the weekly donation.
  3. At rank 2, get 200 contribution from the housing quests.
  4. Your long-term goal is rank 9 at level 52, for Lesser glove attack scrolls.
