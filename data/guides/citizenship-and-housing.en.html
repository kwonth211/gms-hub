Citizenship starts at level 12, when you join Henesys with Arthur in the Town Hall or Kerning City with Roxy in the Civic Center. One board greeting a day and one donation a week earn Contribution, which raises your citizen grade. At level 17 (grade 2) you can buy a house for 15,000 mesos, and at level 57 (grade 10) you earn the town's earrings.

Henesys or Kerning City?

Warriors and bowmen join Henesys; thieves and magicians join Kerning City. Each citizen shop sells different scrolls, so pick the town that matches your main stat.

How to join

  1. At level 12 the quest "To Henesys, the Prairie Town" starts by itself. Go to the Henesys Town Hall and talk to Arthur.
  2. Arthur then offers "To the Gray City, Kerning City" so you hear both towns out. To join Kerning, visit Roxy in the Civic Center.
  3. Talk to the clerk of the town you pick (Arthur or Roxy) to become a citizen.
  4. Take your first greeting and this week's donation from that town's Community Board right away.

Every quest's location and steps are on the Henesys citizenship quests and Kerning City citizenship quests pages.

Daily: resident greetings from the board

How to buy a house (level 17, grade 2)

  1. Once you reach grade 2 (Visitor), Arthur (Henesys) or Roxy (Kerning City) tells you that you can now own a house. This pays 100 Contribution.
  2. Talk to Tommy in the same Town Hall (Henesys) or Jack in the same Civic Center (Kerning City).
  3. Tommy sends you to Bianca, in front of the yellow mushroom house on the east side of Henesys; Jack sends you to Scarlett, right of center at street level in Kerning City. You get three pieces of furniture and 100 Contribution.
  4. Pay Bianca or Scarlett 15,000 mesos for the house.

The house is one per account and every character on the account shares it. You can place crafting stations and storage furniture inside. The quest level (17) and the citizen grade (2) are separate requirements, so you need both. Keep house and furniture money apart from your training budget, and buy only when your next weapon and potions are covered.

How to earn Contribution faster

  • Never skip a greeting. Eight grade-1 greetings (800) plus one donation (700) add up to exactly 1,500, enough for grade 2.
  • Greet with an alt. Citizenship is account-wide, so a level-12 alt parked in town can do the daily greeting and the Contribution still counts.
  • Buy donation materials. If this week's material isn't on your training route, buy 30 on the Free Market.
  • Take both house quests. Accept them the moment you reach grade 2 for an extra 200 Contribution.

Frequently asked questions

My Contribution is full but my grade didn't go up.

Talk to Arthur (Henesys) or Roxy (Kerning City) to be promoted. You also need the character level for that grade: 17 for grade 2, 22 for grade 3, and so on.

Which town is better, Henesys or Kerning City?

Henesys for warriors and bowmen, Kerning City for thieves and magicians. The Henesys shop sells STR and DEX Overall Armor scrolls plus Earring Crit. Damage, while the Kerning shop sells LUK and INT Overall Armor scrolls plus Earring Evasion.

Can I switch towns?

Yes. Re-joining costs 50,000 mesos, and the grade you earned in your old town stays on record.

Do my alts need their own citizenship?

No, the whole account shares one citizenship. Greeting with a level-12 alt every day builds the same Contribution.

How much is a house?

15,000 mesos. Buy it from Bianca or Scarlett from grade 2 (level 17). There's one per account, and all your characters can enter it.

How long does grade 2 take?

About eight days of daily greetings and one weekly donation. The house quests only open at grade 2, so they can't speed up that first promotion.
