Treat citizenship as long-term progress separate from today’s supply budget. Future shop access is not a discount you can already use.

Choose a town at 12

Check Arthur at Henesys Town Hall or Roxy at Kerning’s civic center. Compare your usual routes and intended shop items before choosing a town. Check current rank requirements and prices rather than choosing from class alone.

Community-board routine

  1. Read available daily tasks.
  2. Combine them with your current hunting targets.
  3. Check weekly donation materials and rewards.
  4. Ask the NPC about promotion when level and contribution requirements are met.

Do not assume automatic promotion after a fixed number of days. Verify the current UI before buying large quantities of materials.

Housing budget

Check housing quests after Visitor rank, with Bianca in Henesys or Scarlett in Kerning. Quest level and citizenship rank are separate conditions. Compare the house price, furniture and return access while leaving funds for equipment and supplies.

Shop comparisons

Citizen-shop items are not automatically cheaper than market listings. Compare the same scroll grade and effect, and distinguish displayed base prices from the actual purchase price. Keep furniture purchases separate from training funds.
