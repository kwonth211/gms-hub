Treat citizenship as long-term progress separate from today’s supply budget. Future shop access is not a discount you can already use.

Choose a town at 12

Check Arthur at Henesys Town Hall or Roxy at Kerning’s civic center. Pick the town by the routes you usually hunt and the shop items you want. Check current rank requirements and prices rather than choosing from class alone.

Community-board routine

  1. Read available daily tasks.
  2. Combine them with your current hunting targets.
  3. Check weekly donation materials and rewards.
  4. Ask the NPC about promotion when level and contribution requirements are met.

Promotion is not automatic after a fixed number of days. Read the requirement on the current screen before buying materials in bulk.

Housing budget

Check housing quests after Visitor rank, with Bianca in Henesys or Scarlett in Kerning. Quest level and citizenship rank are separate conditions. Add up the house price, the furniture and the way back, and keep enough for equipment and supplies.

Shop prices

Citizen-shop items are not automatically cheaper than market listings. Match the scroll's grade and effect, and go by the price you actually pay, not the displayed base price. Keep furniture purchases separate from training funds.
