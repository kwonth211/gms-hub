Start with your current weapon: claw or dagger. A Bandit using stars during progression needs a different route from one already using melee attacks.

Early claw training

Practice range and platform coverage before chasing harder targets. Budget star recharge with MP. Go to Stirges once your weapon upgrade makes them one-hit; early Ant Tunnel needs gear and a party and is not the default.

Assassin transitions

Each second job has its own detailed guide with skill build, gear and training spots by level: Assassin · Bandit.

Check Jr. Wraith accuracy and damage consistency. Lorang needs a suitable party and short kills; each floor hits you differently. Later, make sure you stop missing on Wraiths before Tomb III or Camp I floor assignments. Party changes can alter the same setup’s performance.

Bandit paths

Pure dagger users should favor compact grounded targets rather than flying Stirges. A claw-to-dagger transition requires reset costs, a new weapon and enough skill development. Do not switch just because you reached 35 or 40; first settle the main attack you will use and the stats the new gear needs.

Later melee training

Evil Eye, Burnt Land, Lupin, then Curse Eye: move up when kills are short and contact damage stays low. High-level Sanctuary footage shows finished skills and equipment; it says nothing about the early game.

Before buying

  • Make sure you can equip it right away.
  • Match Mastery and Booster to the weapon.
  • Check reliable kill-count improvements.
  • Include stars, shields or reset costs.
  • Leave supplies for the next session.
