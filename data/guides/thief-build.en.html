Thieves have a reputation for being strong in Classic World. One calculation comparing single-target damage of all ten second jobs at level 70 puts the Bandit first and the Assassin second. A successful Steal gives Bandits +35 attack for a minute, and Assassins throw Lucky Seven close to 100 times a minute. The catch is weak multi-target attacks, so map choice matters. This guide covers stats, SP order, claws and stars, and maps from level 1 to 40, in order.

Levels 1-10: Maple Island and first job

Clear every Maple Island quest and take the boat at about level 7. For Beginner skills, 3 each in Three Snails, Recovery and Nimble Feet is all you need. Put every stat point into LUK.

At 10, see the Dark Lord in Kerning City to become a thief. First job has no stat requirement in Classic World, so there's no need to add DEX in advance.

Stats: DEX for your claw, LUK for the rest

LUK stays the main stat after advancing. Add only as much DEX as your next claw needs: 15 DEX at level 15, 20 at 20, 25 at 25, 30 at 30. Everything else goes into LUK, so at 30 you're at roughly 30 DEX and 132 LUK.

First job SP order

  1. 3 in Nimble Body at 11 and 2 in Keen Eyes at 12.
  2. Max Lucky Seven (20) by 18. It throws two stars and is the thief's main attack.
  3. Then extend range with Keen Eyes and raise accuracy with Nimble Body.
  4. Leftover points go into Disorder and Dark Sight.

Spend all your first job SP before 30. SP from second job can't go into first job skills.

Start with Subi Throwing-Stars (15 attack) and switch to Ilbi Throwing-Stars (17 attack) at 21. Kerning City citizenship gets you shop discounts, which matters for thieves who keep buying stars. Details are in the citizenship guide, and current star prices are on the live market.

Throw from too close and you punch with the claw instead of throwing. It does almost no damage, so keep your distance.

Maps for levels 1-30

  • Level 10: Ribbon Pigs. About four throws each at first.
  • Level 15: Green Mushrooms. Two throws each, reliably, in the northern forest tree dungeon or West Rocky Mountain. A little later, Bubblings in subway Line 1 Area 1 are great: 64 of them spawn on one map.
  • Level 20: Stirges. The flying Stirges in the Transfer Area suit throwing stars: two throws at 20, nearly one at 25. If it's busy, Dangerous Steam has plenty of Stirges too. Jr. Neckis die to one throw at 25 but are crowded.
  • From 21: the Kerning party quest. Talk to Lakelis at the Kerning City sewer entrance with a party of four. Clear five stages in 30 minutes and kill King Slime; the bonus map spawns 36 mushrooms for nice EXP. At 21 you often miss the Curse Eyes in the last stage; from 25 you hit them all. How to run it is in the Kerning party quest guide.
  • From 25: the Ant Tunnel. Ant Tunnel III spawns 99 monsters, the densest of all. At 20 your accuracy is short and one throw in four misses; at 25 they all land. Zombie Mushrooms and Horny Mushrooms reliably die in two throws, and near 30 so do the Evil Eyes in Ant Tunnel Park. There's no taxi out once you take one in, so bring return scrolls.

Do quests too: quests alone give 28% of the EXP you need to reach 30. Which ones to do is in seven early quests worth doing.

Level 30: second job, and 30-40

At 30, the Dark Lord sends you to the thief instructor at the construction site north of Kerning City. Bring back 30 Dark Marbles from the test map and choose between the Assassin, who keeps throwing stars, and the Bandit, who picks up daggers.

If you went Bandit, don't switch to a dagger right away. Until 40, keeping your claw and Lucky Seven is faster. Stop DEX at 30 and put everything into LUK. For second job, take Haste first and max it at 37, with the rest in the Nimble Body line. If you plan to reset SP and move to daggers at 40, you get these points back anyway.

If you went Assassin, you won't be resetting, so take Claw Mastery and Critical Throw before Haste.

Keep hunting in the Ant Tunnel. At 30 Zombie and Horny Mushrooms die in two throws, and around 39 Zombie Mushrooms nearly die in one. Moving up to Curse Eyes or Lupins too early means four throws each, which is slower. For 40 onward, continue with the level 30-70 training guide; the monkey forest in particular is where claw thieves are most welcome.

Summary

Stats: mostly LUK, DEX only for your claw. Skills: Lucky Seven first. Maps: Ribbon Pigs → Green Mushrooms → Stirges → Ant Tunnel. Run KPQ alongside from 21, and stick with a claw until 40. To check your throws per monster, use the hits-to-kill calculator.
