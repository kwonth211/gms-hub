Level a Thief with a claw and throwing stars, and put every point into Lucky Seven until it hits 20 at level 18. For stats, raise DEX only to your claw's requirement and put everything else into LUK. Even if you plan to become a Bandit, you can level first job with stars and switch to daggers with a skill reset around level 40 to 45.

Skill order if you level with a dagger

Starting with a dagger costs you no skill reset and no stars. The price is melee: you have to stand next to monsters, so levels 10 to 30 are harder than with a claw.

  1. Level 17: Double Stab 20, from 80% ×2 up to 160% ×2.
  2. Level 22: Nimble Body 15. You take hits up close, so avoid saves potions right away.
  3. Level 23: Disorder 3 to open Dark Sight.
  4. Level 29: Dark Sight 20.
  5. Level 30: the last 3 points take Disorder to 6.

Dagger Thieves skip the flying Stirges and go where monsters gather on the floor. Line 1 Area 1 in the Kerning subway spawns nothing but Bubblings, 64 of them, and the Ellinia tree dungeons pack Slimes and Green Mushrooms onto each floor.

Stats: all LUK, DEX only to your claw's level

Put everything into LUK until level 10. After that, raise DEX only to what your next claw asks for and put the rest into LUK. Claws need DEX equal to their level: Steel Titans at 15 needs 15 DEX, Meba at 25 needs 25, and Steel Slain at 50 needs 50.

DEX from gear counts toward that requirement. If your gloves or earrings carry DEX, put that many fewer points into DEX and move them to LUK. The level 10 Garnier has no DEX requirement at all; it only asks for 25 LUK.

Your accuracy is (DEX × 1.2 + level × 2 + LUK × 0.6) ÷ 4 + 15, plus Nimble Body and any accuracy on your gear. LUK feeds accuracy too, so a level 25 Thief with only Meba's minimum stats (25 DEX, 55 LUK) sits at 43 accuracy before Nimble Body, and with Nimble Body never misses a Stirge (44 needed). Each map's accuracy figure is in the training table below.

  • Levels 15 to 21: split the floors of Caution Falling Down with your party. If Octopuses take three throws, move to the Green Mushrooms in the tree dungeons; the spot at the top of the forest gives about 2,500 EXP every 5 minutes.
  • Levels 20 to 35: the Stirges in the Kerning subway Transfer Area are the claw Thief's signature spot. A level 20 Thief makes about 3,500 EXP every 5 minutes there, and the Lemon and ore drops keep you in profit. If Stirges take two throws, buy a Meba at 25 and come back.
  • Levels 21 to 30: no room at the Stirges? Run the Kerning Party Quest. Clearing the bonus map alone pays well in both EXP and mesos inside its 20 to 25 minute limit.
  • Ant Tunnel: go with a level 20 claw and a party, once Horny Mushrooms die in two throws. Zombie Mushrooms drop the 60% Claw Attack scroll.

Pick Assassin if you want to kill one monster at a time from a safe distance. Stars and the level 50 claw cost a lot, but almost every map works for you. Pick Bandit if you enjoy standing in close and deleting one monster fast.

A Bandit can still level first job with a claw, advance, and reset skills into daggers around 40 to 45. If you go that way, save up for the reset, the new dagger and the dagger's STR or DEX requirement before you start. Do not reset the moment you hit the level; settle the dagger and the main attack first, then reset.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

  • Standing on top of monsters and punching with the claw: up close you swing the claw instead of throwing, which does almost no damage. Jump backwards while you throw and the stars come out even when a monster is touching you.
  • Raising DEX early: every point of DEX above your next claw's requirement is a point of LUK you lost. Raise it on the level you swap claws, not before.
  • Bringing a single stack of stars: when it runs out, your session stops. Carry several usable stacks.
  • Putting points elsewhere before Lucky Seven: until 18, Lucky Seven is the fastest place for every point. Only Keen Eyes 1 goes in before it.
  • Living with two-hit Stirges: two throws means double the MP and stars per kill. Get a Meba or go to the Party Quest.

Frequently asked questions

How much DEX does a Thief need?

Exactly your next claw's level, minus the DEX on your gear. That is 25 DEX for Meba and 40 for Steel Avarice. Everything else goes into LUK.

Isn't a LUK earring the same as a DEX earring?

For damage, yes. A DEX earring lets you put that many fewer points into DEX and more into LUK, so your final LUK and DEX come out the same. The catch: take the DEX earring off later and you can fall short of your next claw's DEX, so pick an earring you plan to keep.

Is a no-money Thief hard to level?

Not before 30. Shop Wolbis go for about {{price:2070001}} and a Meba for about {{price:1472007}}, both easy to pay for with Stirge drops. The real costs start at level 50 with the Steel Slain and pricey stars like Kumbi and Tobi.

I want to be a Bandit. Do I have to use a dagger from the start?

No. You can level first job with a claw and reset into daggers around 40 to 45. If the reset cost bothers you, start with Double Stab 20 and train where monsters gather on the floor, such as the tree dungeons and Line 1 Area 1.

Do I need Dark Sight and Disorder?

You will barely use them while grinding; they are where your spare points end up. Disorder 3 opens Dark Sight, and the points left by 30 give a claw Thief Dark Sight 8 and a dagger Thief Dark Sight 20.

Is the Pan Lid a good shield for a Thief?

Yes, for a dagger Thief it is a strong first shield. You can wear it from level 10, and it gives 44 defense with 7 upgrade slots. Green Mushrooms drop it, and it sells for about {{price:1092002}} right now.
