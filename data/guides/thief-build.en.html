Start with your current weapon: claw or dagger. A Bandit using stars during progression needs a different route from one already using melee attacks.

Early claw training

Practice range and platform coverage before chasing harder targets. Budget star recharge with MP. Compare one-hit Stirges around your weapon upgrades; early Ant Tunnel is a separate gear-and-party trial, not a default.

Assassin transitions

Each second job has its own detailed guide with skill build, gear and training spots by level: Assassin · Bandit.

Check Jr. Wraith accuracy and damage consistency. Lorang requires a suitable party and short kills; compare floor-specific contact costs. Later, test Wraith accuracy before Tomb III or Camp I floor assignments. Party changes can alter the same setup’s performance.

Bandit paths

Pure dagger users should favor compact grounded targets rather than flying Stirges. A claw-to-dagger transition requires reset costs, a new weapon and enough skill development. Do not switch solely because you reached 35 or 40; test the resulting main attack and requirements first.

Later melee training

Compare Evil Eye, Burnt Land, Lupin and Curse Eye by short kills and contact damage. Steal and recovery skills do not guarantee profit everywhere. High-level Sanctuary footage includes developed skills and equipment and is not an early-game benchmark.

Before buying

  • Verify immediate equip requirements.
  • Match Mastery and Booster to the weapon.
  • Check reliable kill-count improvements.
  • Include stars, shields or reset costs.
  • Leave supplies for the next session.
