What about five Lesser scrolls for 5 ATT?

Lesser Gloves Attack scrolls add 1 ATT at a 100% success rate. Five produce 5 ATT. A median scroll price of 649,999.5 plus a clean pair at 739,999 puts the total at about 3.99m. The Lesser price comes from 12 open listings by 12 sellers.

Finished 6 ATT gloves started at about 4.20m, only 210,000 more. At these prices, save that extra amount and buy 6 ATT. Scrolls already in your inventory still have a resale price; counting them as free hides money you could keep.

Should you use the other four scrolls after the first fails?

Stop at the first failure if your goal is 10 ATT. Four more Intermediate successes only reach 8 ATT. Spending four more scrolls does not bring that pair back to a 10 ATT result.

For an 8 ATT goal, a failed first scroll means all four remaining scrolls must succeed. That is 0.6⁴ = 12.96%, with 7.28m more in scrolls. Sell the failed pair with four slots left and keep the rest of your upgrade budget.

The 9.84m cost and outcome table above use all five scrolls, whatever happens. A first-failure stopping strategy uses a different number of scrolls and needs its own calculation.

The prices and arithmetic used here

  • Snapshot: November 28, 2026, 13:59–14:01 KST. Open listings were read within the previous 24 hours; sold figures cover the previous seven days.
  • Gloves: Work Gloves, 6 weapon defence, 2% critical rate. Clean means zero successful upgrades and five slots left; finished means zero slots left.
  • Duplicates: repeated readings with the same seller, channel, room, price and options count once. Listing counts are not stack quantities.
  • Odds: five independent scrolls with a 60% success rate. Exactly k successes: 5Ck × 0.6ᵏ × 0.4⁽⁵⁻ᵏ⁾. At least one 10 ATT pair out of five: 1 − (1 − 0.6⁵)⁵.
  • Rounding: abbreviated prices in the text are rounded. Calculations use the original meso amounts; the sold-price table rounds a half-meso median to the nearest meso.
