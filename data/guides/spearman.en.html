Level a Spearman with a polearm until 49 and spend second-job SP in this order: Rush 1, Mastery 5, Booster 1, Mastery 20 (by level 38), Final Attack 30 (by level 47). Final Attack heals you for 10% of the damage it deals, so the Spearman drinks the fewest potions of any warrior and can solo at a profit.

Maxing all five second-job skills takes 120 SP, and this order finishes around level 70. On November 14, Spearmen were 106 of the top 1,000 characters by level, the most of any warrior class (161 warriors in total).

  2. By level 35: Mastery 5, then Booster 1. Booster gives its full speed at level 1: at level 70, Power Strikes per minute go from 70 to 81.
  3. By level 38: Mastery 20.

On maps where gathering matters most, take Rush to 10 first. If getting knocked back annoys you, take Iron Will earlier. Iron Will and Booster both last 300 seconds, so cast them together.

With a spear, put the same points into Spear Mastery, Spear Booster and Final Attack: Spear. The numbers are the same; only the Mastery bonus changes, a 20% chance to ignore weapon defense instead of movement speed.

How to come out of first job

A Spearman goes Power Strike 20, Improved HP Recovery 15, Slash Blast 11, Precise Strikes 15 in first job. Spears and polearms give no accuracy and Final Attack hits one monster, so you need both the group attack and the accuracy.

If you have accuracy gear, you can stop Precise Strikes at about 6 and put more into Slash Blast. The full first-job table is in the warrior build guide.

Power Strike and Slash Blast come out as a swing six times in ten, and polearms swing hard. Speed +20 matters a lot for a slow warrior: five 100% shoe speed scrolls only add 5. With weapon crafting you can make the Axe Pole Arm at crafting level 7 and the Crescent Polearm at level 8.

The real question comes at level 50. Most Spearmen in their 30s carry a polearm, but by the 50s spears are the more common sight.

Stats and gear

Raise DEX only to your weapon's requirement and put the rest into STR. A level 40 weapon needs DEX 40, so with 4 DEX on your gear you stop at 36. Level 50 weapons need DEX 50.

  • Skip level 35 weapons. They are only about 5 attack below level 40 ones, so on a tight budget go straight from 30 to 40.
  • Skip level 35 and 45 armor too. It has the same stats as the tier below; spend that money on potions or your weapon.
  • Weapon scrolls: a 60% polearm attack scroll costs {{price:2044401}} and a 60% spear one {{price:2044301}}. Each success adds 2 attack.
  • Gloves: scroll for attack, not critical. Spearman crit damage is low, and attack is what makes more monsters die in one hit.
  • Potions: buy only what heals most per meso: Red Potions ({{price:2000000}}), Fried Chicken ({{price:2020001}}), Meat ({{price:2010003}}), Oranges ({{price:2010004}}) and Lemons ({{price:2010005}}). White and Blue Potions are poor value.
  • Accuracy potions: drink them only on maps where you miss. A miss still costs HP and MP, so where you miss, the potion pays for itself.

How to use Rush and Final Attack

The basic loop is Rush to gather, Slash Blast to sweep. Rush's MP cost stings, but taking hits while you save MP costs more. When in doubt, Rush.

Since the November 24 patch, Rush pushes monsters the instant it hits and keeps them from snapping back for 1.5 seconds. Power Strike, Slash Blast and Final Attack now push on impact too.

Final Attack has a 25% chance to unlock each time you use an attack skill, and you then have 60 seconds to press it. One heal is capped at 25% of your max HP, and it never heals more than the monster's max HP.

  • Press it when you are hurt. 10% of a 350% hit comes straight back as HP.
  • Save it on monsters that already die in one hit. It has its own animation, so you only waste time and MP.
  • Against four or more, Slash Blast again. That beats a single-target Final Attack.

The heal saves HP potions, not MP. A level 70 Spearman spamming only Power Strike drinks about six and a half Lemons a minute.

Common mistakes and the fix

  • Taking Final Attack late: the heal is the reason a Spearman saves on potions. After Mastery 20, put everything into Final Attack until level 47.
  • Pressing Final Attack every time it charges: on monsters that die in one hit you only lose animation time. Use it on the last monster when you are hurt.
  • Packing too few MP potions: the heal covers HP, not MP. Bring plenty of Lemons.
  • Overspending on The Nine Dragons at 50: Serpent's Tongue costs far less. Spend the difference on scrolls and potions and you will grind faster.

Frequently asked questions

Spear or polearm?

A polearm until 49. It adds 20 speed, its swings hit hard, and you can craft the level 35 and 40 ones. From 50, pick whichever you like: the faster Serpent's Tongue spear or the higher-attack Nine Dragons.

Is a Nine Dragons with 87 attack better than an 82 one plus attack gloves?

Spend on the weapon first. You keep using The Nine Dragons until you buy your next-level weapon, so the higher-attack one pays off longer. Attack gloves can wait until their price comes down.

How much does Final Attack heal?

At max level it heals 10% of the damage it deals. One heal is capped at 25% of your max HP and never exceeds the monster's max HP. At level 1 it is only 5%, which is why filling it to 30 by 47 comes first.

Do Spearmen really not drink potions?

They barely drink HP potions, but they still drink MP potions. A level 70 Spearman spamming only Power Strike goes through about six and a half Lemons a minute. A level 55 solo on Hot Sand used 750 Meat and 300 Lemons in an hour.

Where does a Spearman train after 50?

Solo, go to Hot Sand or Remains <Tomb> I and III; in a party, Drake Hunting Ground or Dangerous Valley. From 60, Dark Stone Golems, and from 63, Camp 1 gives the most EXP.

When should I take Iron Will?

Last. At max it gives a 35% chance to ignore knockback and raises weapon defense. On maps where knockback keeps breaking up your gathering, put a few points in earlier.
