Every day someone posts that grinding only costs them potions. Yet one melee class barely drinks HP potions: the Spearman. One in two ranked second-job warriors is a Spearman, and they lead the warriors in the top 500 by level. The secret is a Final Attack that heals you. Skill numbers below come from the game data; the hunting records are reports from players who levelled the class.

Spear or polearm?

Mastery, Booster and Final Attack come in separate spear and polearm versions, so switching later means re-spending points.

At max levelSpear MasteryPolearm Mastery
MasteryRaises minimum damage (same)Raises minimum damage (same)
Bonus20% chance to ignore weapon defenseMovement speed +20

Power Strike and Slash Blast swing six times in ten, and polearms swing hard. Speed +20 matters for slow warriors: five shoe speed scrolls only add 5. Players report you can craft the Axe Pole Arm at weapon crafting level 7 and the Crescent Polearm at level 8.

The question comes at level 50.

Level 50 weaponWhat it's likeWhere from
Serpent's Tongue (spear, attack 69)Normal attack speed; feels much better than polearms, and faster swings mean Final Attack charges and heals more oftenDrops from Mummydog, Skeleton Soldier and others
The Nine Dragons (polearm, attack 75)About 9% higher shown attack at the same scrolls, so more monsters die in one hit, and you keep the speed. Few on the market and much pricierDrops from Skeleton Officer and others

Either is fine. If you want one recommendation: use a polearm to 49 for the speed and easy weapons, try Serpent's Tongue at 50, and if you like it, switch to spear with an SP reset (70,000 to 80,000 mesos).

Stats and gear

DEX only to your weapon's requirement, the rest into STR. A level 40 weapon needs DEX 40, so with 4 DEX on gear, stop at 36. See the Warrior equipment list.

  • Level 35 weapons only add about 5 attack; if money is tight, skip to a level 40 weapon.
  • Level 35 and 45 armor has the same stats as the tier below; spend that money on potions or weapons.
  • Scroll gloves for attack, not critical: Spearman crit damage is low, and attack is what makes more monsters die in one hit.
  • Buy potions that heal the most per meso: Red Potions, Fried Chicken, Meat, Oranges, Lemons. White and Blue Potions are poor value.
  • Accuracy potions only where you miss. A miss still costs HP and MP, so on maps where you miss, the potion pays for itself.

The basic loop is Rush to gather, Slash Blast to sweep. Rush's MP stings, but taking hits costs more: "if you're unsure, just Rush".

Don't press Final Attack every time it charges. It has its own animation, so on monsters that already die in one hit it wastes time and MP. Against four or more, one more Slash Blast beats a single-target Final Attack.

Skill build

  1. Level 30: Rush 1 and start gathering right away
  2. By about 35: Mastery 5, then Booster 1
  3. By about 38: Mastery 20
  4. By about 47: Final Attack 30, which is what cuts your potion bill
  5. Then: Booster and Rush, Iron Will last

Where gathering matters most, Rush to 10 first is fine; if being knocked back annoys you, some take Iron Will early.

In short

Swing versus thrust animations make damage swing a lot, so hits-to-kill is hard to pin down, and on damage alone Bandits do better. You juggle Rush, Power Strike, Slash Blast and Final Attack, so your hands stay busy. But while everyone else is tired of losing mesos to potions, a Spearman can solo at a profit, and that is its biggest strength. Pick a map for your level from the Spearman training routes.
